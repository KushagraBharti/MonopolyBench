## Where the materials live

The final manuscript, derived evidence and release archive remain together in `docs/research_raw/paper_finalization_2026-11-14/integrated/`. The surrounding finalization directory preserves all four complete drafts, reviews and integration decisions. Earlier revisions remain in their dated directories under `docs/research_raw/`; original gameplay artifacts remain under `saved_games/`.

The existing `paper/figures/`, `paper/scripts/`, `paper/supplement/`, `claim_source_audit.md` and `final_build_manifest.json` are legacy materials from an earlier eight-game paper. They are retained for provenance. Use the links above for the current twenty-game paper.
