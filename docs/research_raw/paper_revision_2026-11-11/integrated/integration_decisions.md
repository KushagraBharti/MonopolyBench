# Integration decisions

This is the integration workspace, not a fifth independent draft. The four full drafts and their original reviews remain frozen. Astra, Opus and Fable's whole-paper cross-reviews are incorporated; Sol's current cross-review is pending. No final-review claim is made.

## Scientific architecture

The paper explains strategic attention to rival development, bargaining under cash pressure, and financial execution. Provenance checks support these observations; auditing is not the scientific headline. Exact outcomes do not establish optimality or a model ranking.

## Opus cross-review, independently checked by Astra

1. Adopt the deed-request comparator. Opening author-only completion requests receive 138/187 rejections, versus 105/176 other requests for deeds that complete neither party. The direction holds in five seed blocks and all four recipient configurations. Later rates are 21/102 and 20/108. These are subsets, not covariate-matched or randomized offers. Fable's subsequent offer-form split supersedes this pooled contrast as the headline; preserve it as context and the full completion table in the appendix.
2. Do not adopt the claim that cash pressure specifically dissolves a completion guard. Low cash is associated with less rejection for both types of request. Show stage and recipient conditions; do not turn the pooled 25%/69% split into a causal title.
3. Adopt the 19 successful payments using newly unlocked collateral: 33 applied mortgages absent from the first menu, after building sales, in groups initially developed. Astra checked all cases against original menus and applied actions. Two standard-condition payable declarations omit this collateral in their recorded arithmetic. Describe the computations and the successful sequences, without identifying an internal algorithm from text alone.
4. Reconcile the seven same-deed pricing comparisons by reporting medians and first asks. G4 alone changes the 4/2/1 median ordering to 5/1/1 for first asks. Fable's later check identifies G4 as the different-mortgage-status case; the six same-status comparisons give 4/1/1 under either summary. No premium is identified.
5. Keep sale-to-construction/winner associations out of the abstract: post-sale construction and eventual victory select the comparison; seller-game groups overlap. Keep the actual development trajectories.

### Interpretations not adopted from replacement prose

Cash bids do not “usually keep negotiation open”: 42/70 and 67/103 are rejected. A form-dependent contrast does not establish that form causes the response. Updated menus do not distinguish internal planning from reactive execution. A specific retrospective contrast is not a preregistered or independently replicated result. These distinctions are kept concise, next to the claims they qualify.

No new evaluation, engine execution, replay, synthetic test, model query, commit or publication is part of integration.
