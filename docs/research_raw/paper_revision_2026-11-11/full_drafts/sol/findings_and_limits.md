# Findings, objections, and remaining limits

## Central position after analysis

The best supported claim is competitive-option attention. The agents sometimes address a rival's future development finance, an intermediary's resale, and creditor succession. The scientific distinction is between this concrete attention and strategically effective use. I do not claim the distinction itself is new to negotiation research or that this corpus establishes a general cognitive architecture.

The old acquisition-to-estate organization was useful but too close to a description of Monopoly's rules. This draft uses economic paths as context for behavior and uses stronger comparisons to constrain its interpretation.

## New arithmetic that changes the account

- Seven game/seller/single-deed ask comparisons give four higher completing-buyer medians, two lower, and one equal. Requiring the same mortgage status and opening/later stage leaves five cells, three higher, one lower, one equal. The positive unmatched ordering should not become an identified premium.
- Across Sol/Opus/Grok's twelve seller-by-mortgage-by-stage comparison cells, eleven have higher completing asks; Opus's unmortgaged later asks reverse. Very small cells and unmatched deeds remain visible in the appendix. This is a descriptive pattern, not consistent strategic valuation.
- All 188 debt-menu episodes include 128 recorded payments and sixty eliminations. Each payment is matched to its final action's emitted event range. Combining actual payments with the three inherited payment witnesses gives 131 demonstrably payable episodes, three ending in elimination. This corrects the failure-selected denominator without rerunning an engine.
- First creditors lead or tie in prior paid rent in 15/20 games, buildings in 12/20, cash in 8/20, and deeds in 10/20. Prior-rent and building leaders are unique in those 15 and 12 cases. Incoming estates did not arrive into otherwise equal positions. This limits an inheritance-causes-victory reading without establishing that inheritance is irrelevant.
- First-window-built trade groups have 2,518 opponent moves and 150 group landings, versus 1,029 and 63 for the other twenty groups. Median completion turns are 66 versus 69.5; median ownership durations 62 versus 63.5. The 93.8% rent share is larger than the roughly 70% exposure share. Thus timing alone does not explain the receipt contrast, but construction, rent schedule, selection, mortgages and insolvency remain intertwined.
- Exact saved standard/recovery system messages and schemas retain public-deception permission in recovery. Private-note production, honest-private-reasoning wording, and the schema field disappear, and an explicit no-reasoning-transcript sentence is added. The recovery is more than merely dropping one field. Its state description still mentions private thoughts.

## Objections to old interpretations

1. “Seller premium” is too strong. Board price is a scale, prices are selected asks, comparison overlap is tiny, and same-deed directions are mixed.
2. The first estate's winner correlation must be read alongside pre-estate position. Most first creditors already lead prior receipts, though not always cash or holdings.
3. Three payable eliminations cannot characterize financing without the successful-debt denominator. Conversely, paying the present debt does not show prudent earlier investment.
4. Generic repricing and rival-sensitive statements coexist. Their coexistence supports neither a purely empty-talk interpretation nor a coherent-value-policy interpretation.
5. An inaccurate house count should not erase the correctly identified strategic constraint; a correctly named constraint should not certify valuation quality.
6. Opus's note and retry condition matters for all players. Deleting its rows cannot reconstruct a clean unaffected experiment. Missing notes are not missing all memory; provider token fields are not a cognition scale.
7. The 5/36 versus 4/36 G13 contrast is a first-roll arithmetic issue. Neither number is a full turn-level landing probability with extra movement. I removed the stronger next-turn phrasing.
8. A public-message argument need not be delivered. Fresh checks find no later recipient exposure for G12's bankruptcy message. This incident uses its recorded field, not a reception or persuasion claim.

## Objections and choices concerning the contract

I follow the full-paper and eight-incident requirements. Eight is a presentation requirement, not a scientifically selected sample size. The incidents earn their space as distinct contrasts, but the number should not imply coverage or prevalence. No peer draft from this revision was read.

The main 6,000–7,500-word target permits a coherent observational report, but it is not a ready-made conference format. The draft has about 6,300 main-prose words excluding quotes, tables, references and appendices, and 24 total PDF pages. I do not treat page count or internal agreement as an acceptance signal.

The ban on new engine calls is appropriate to this historical revision. It prevents completing the seven candidate static financing routes, testing prompt repairs, or measuring alternative strategy values. I do not disguise record arithmetic as those experiments. My pre-estate display intentionally avoids a new accessible-finance search and overfit prediction model.

## Remaining limits

The central pattern is supported in selected contextual records and inherited semantic labels. It has no independently validated cognitive measure or matched intervention. Novelty is modest relative to recent Monopoly, negotiation and state-grounded communication work. Generalization is constrained by five blocks, fixed relative order, one roster, differing response conditions, and endogenous opportunities. No statistics here converts those trajectories into independent offer trials. No evidence here ranks underlying models or establishes optimal prices, causal persuasion, stable coalitions, causal learning, memory benefit, or unfamiliar-opponent transfer.
