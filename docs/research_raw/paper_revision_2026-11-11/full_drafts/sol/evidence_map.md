## What is not verified

No optimal prices, strategic utility, counterfactual winners, causal message effects, hidden beliefs, memory benefit, learning effect, external transfer, or broad general/multi-agent capability relationship is measured. Historical replay does not validate those constructs. The new narrative is an independent scientific interpretation, not an independently validated annotation method.
