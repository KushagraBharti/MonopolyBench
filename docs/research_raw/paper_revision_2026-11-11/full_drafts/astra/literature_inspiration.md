# Primary-paper reading and presentation choices

Read during this revision, 11 November 2026. These are sources for specific ideas, not an assertion that every page or repository was independently reproduced.

- Lewis et al., EMNLP 2017, [paper](https://aclanthology.org/D17-1259.pdf): introduction, task, results Table 1 and analysis of negotiation behavior. Useful separation of language measures, agreement and utility, plus human and model opponents. Our study has no equivalent utility reference; do not borrow its effectiveness claims. I use its distinction to keep a low acceptance rate separate from agreement quality.
- He et al., EMNLP 2018, [paper](https://aclanthology.org/D18-1256.pdf): introduction, CraigslistBargain task, strategy/generation separation, dialogue examples and measurement. Coarse actions and natural-language realization are separate objects. Our records permit the analogous observational distinction but do not test that architecture.
- Li et al., [Arena v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.14825v3): sections 2.2, 4.1–4.3, results on follow-through and capability, limitations and annotation audit. Useful pattern: define a proposition, ground it in state, then inspect later action; methods explicitly cluster and validate labels. We retain contextual cards without importing a population misalignment rate or claiming equivalent validation. Task norms differ.
- Müller and Müller, [Cattle Trade v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.14537v1): abstract, design, code-agent comparisons, sections 4.3–4.5 and metric definitions. Useful focus on coherent deployment of capabilities rather than spending volume. Our study has no code-agent comparison. The inherited bibliography's workshop venue was not confirmed in this reading, so the local draft cites the verified arXiv version instead.
- Jehiel, Moldovanu and Stacchetti, [author-hosted paper](https://www.econ.uni-bonn.de/micro/en/moldovanu/publications-1/how_-not-_to_sell.pdf/@@download/file/how_-not-_to_sell.pdf): abstract, introduction and model motivation. Identity-dependent externalities and endogenous outside options supply established economic language for rival consequences. We do not estimate its mechanism or use it as a Monopoly equilibrium benchmark.
- Bianchi et al., [PMLR](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/bianchi24a.html): primary abstract and publication record. Supports the narrow comparison to controlled bargaining/tactic studies; not newly read in full.
- AgentMonopoly [v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.29330v2), FinBoardBench [v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.27896v1): primary abstracts, setup and structural contents; earlier detailed source readings remain archived. This revision deliberately avoids detailed absence claims about their code and does not claim that LLM Monopoly or counteroffers are new.
- [DECE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35773) and [HAL](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2026/hash/a0928f924a344aaebbb7f6cd8d56e34c-Abstract-Conference.html): primary abstracts. Supports evaluation of complete configurations. Detailed engine comparisons are not newly verified.
- [Anthropic official documentation](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/opus-5-5/whats-new-opus-5-5): the reasoning-extraction refusal category is documented. The corpus lacks the provider category field. We distinguish consistency with that documentation from proof of the exact historical trigger.

Rubinstein's bargaining paper was located on the author's page, but its PDF text did not extract through the web tool. I do not add a citation merely to lengthen the bibliography. Other inherited references support previously reviewed background statements; no new venue claim is made.

Presentation decisions: eight incidents remain, with fewer cards and more compact context; numerical populations and conditional comparisons precede their examples. The main figures show asks and successfully executed finance. Accounting totals move into the explanation of economic stakes. No claim that this manuscript is 10/10, causally identified, or guaranteed acceptance follows from these readings.
