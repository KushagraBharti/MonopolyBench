# Findings, dissent and limits

My proposed center is strategic attention plus executed local financial reasoning. The paper observes agents considering rival development and prospective estates, and it now includes all liquidity-resolution episodes rather than treating bankruptcy alone as the competence evidence. The 128 payments, including 61 multi-action paths, are the strongest new positive denominator in this draft.

The title, abstract and main argument are newly written. I retain eight incidents but reduce the displayed cards to seventeen, keeping context and subsequent behavior. I remove the headline that built groups earn most rent, and use estate totals as the economic continuation rather than the central discovery. The two figures describe asking prices and liquidation paths. The complete questions remain in an appendix table introduced in the main text.

I would reject an integrated claim that sellers charge a clean monopoly-completion premium: six seller/mortgage comparisons are positive, but seven same-seller/deed/game comparisons include two reversals and one tie. The buyer side also varies. Strategic attention is better supported than price optimization. Counteroffer bundle preservation is common in the non-completion comparison too.

I also reject treating the 128 payments as a difficulty-adjusted model success rate. The obligations and asset portfolios differ; some payments need only one obvious mortgage. The multi-action distribution establishes actual sequencing, not how hard the tasks are relative to a baseline. Conversely, it is too strong to say no action can be assessed at all without a rollout oracle: a paid current obligation is directly checkable.

The study remains small, confounded and retrospective. No new model performance, learning, persuasion, policy-value or interface-ablation claim is made. No human annotation was obtained. The eight incidents are selected, not representative. This draft does not solve those limitations by wording and should not be advertised as perfect or guaranteed main-track quality.

Independent authorship boundary: I have not opened any of the three new peer papers. Prior drafts and reviews were read. My fresh tabulations are not yet independently reviewed; all are candidates for the cross-review stage, with scripts and row-level outputs supplied.
