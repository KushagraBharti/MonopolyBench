# Independent position before new peer drafts

The manuscript should explain what these LLM agents attend to and how that attention translates into economic action. My starting distinction is strategic attention versus strategic effectiveness. A reader can observe consideration of rivals, contingent plans and bargaining positions without concluding that the chosen action maximizes value. The paper should make the positive behavioral findings readable, then specify their evidential level.

I will examine within-system and within-thread-position bargaining contrasts, seller asking prices, counteroffers, and development timing. Price ratios do not establish a completion premium because assets, counterparties and bargaining stages differ. Rent concentration is context unless exposure and timing make its interpretation more specific. Inheritance is an economic mechanism, not a discovery that winners own things.

The three most consequential alternatives are: a broad behavioral study; a narrower study of relational bargaining; or a study of the gap between expressed plans and executable economic action. I will choose the argument after tabulation rather than promising a result. I reject making an unvalidated general audit method the central contribution.

Eight contextual incidents should connect to population evidence and show both competence and failure. Public bluffing is authorized. Explanations are recorded text, not privileged access to cognition. All eight questions and A–C remain in the coverage map; unanswered questions must remain unanswered under the no-new-evaluations constraint.

The standard and recovery prompts, refusal message, information surface, and provider usage semantics need precise reporting once. The main text should not become a defect inventory. I will not infer that missing private notes means no memory, that a zero billing field means no thinking, or that an extra retry would certainly have fixed every default.

This position precedes reading any of the three new peer drafts. The previous manuscripts, reviews and feedback are available evidence. All work will stay under this directory; no engine execution or new evaluation is authorized.
