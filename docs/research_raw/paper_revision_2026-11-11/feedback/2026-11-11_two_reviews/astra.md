## My judgment

The serious remaining problem is the link between observations and explanations. The records support an exploratory bargaining pattern and successful local financing. They do not yet identify how much the bargaining pattern reflects rival concern rather than compensation and proposer composition, or how demanding the financing task is. The attached review explains that distinction more carefully than the inline review. Neither venue scores nor the assertion that two arms are simply “broken” should replace a claim-by-claim assessment.

We have promoted new exploratory contrasts into headline discoveries too quickly across revisions. Correcting comparison populations is necessary, but recomputing each new cut does not independently confirm it. We should preserve the analysis history and settle the claim's scope rather than search for a more striking headline on the same records.

## Ranked issues

1. **The bargaining interpretation is not isolated.** The reviewer’s proposer table reproduces exactly. The pooled opening-swap gap is partly compositional. Standardizing to the proposer distribution in the author-only group gives 16.0 percentage points; using the neither group gives 21.5, and the pooled distribution gives 18.1. The reference weights matter. All four within-proposer point differences remain positive, but several comparison cells are tiny. Neither the raw 30.5 points nor a standardized difference is a causal measure of concern about rival development. The monetary and property terms may explain a substantial part of acceptance. Calling the comparator “giveaways” based on list value adds an unestablished economic judgment.
2. **The semantic evidence does not explain the headline cells.** The 61/74 reasons concern rejected recipient-only or both-completing offers. The headline concerns author-only versus neither. Our transition saying those reasons “connect that pattern” invites a stronger explanation than this population overlap supports. Keep the semantic evidence as a separate observation unless the actual headline cells are examined. Define expressed concern, correct calculation, action consistency and effectiveness separately.
3. **The financial task's difficulty is not calibrated, but prospective text supplies evidence we can use.** I reproduce 16 episodes where cash plus all salvage and initially listed mortgages are insufficient while the generous full bound covers the debt. Fourteen are paid and two end in bankruptcy. This is distinct from the 19 successful episodes that use newly available collateral, which need not have been necessary. I read all sixteen initial fields. The fourteen successful cases mention or encompass the subsequent mortgages, but “all explicitly count them” is too uniform: G20 Opus says it is checking the pink mortgages, and Sol gives an aggregate plan. Five successes rely on provider-returned reasoning rather than an applied private note. These are useful prospective statements and observed execution, not identified internal computation or a validated semantic census. Ten calls also do not establish ten levels of lookahead.
4. **The uncertainty presentation is incomplete.** A finite-population count is exact for the selected cohort; that does not justify broad language about systems. Report proposer composition and the full form/completion table, define the statistical target, and quantify uncertainty only under explicit dependence assumptions. The inline review's intervals and regression p-values remain unverified without its implementation, weights and resampling details. Five seed blocks and one fixed roster complicate treating twenty games as twenty independent clusters. “Absent for cash bids” should describe the observed estimate, not a demonstrated zero effect. Additional regressions will not make retrospective selection disappear.
5. **The paper still mixes scientific exposition with its revision history.** Remove reviewer-directed sentences and repeated disclaimers, clarify the thesis, and allocate space according to what evidence each result contributes. Keep the eight incidents in the research package, with a shorter main-text selection if required by the eventual format. Positive payment and factual correction deserve their space. A narrower heading than “LLM agency” would better match the actual claims.
6. **A few source and release details are materially wrong or missing.** I checked G18 d442: its returned explanation objects to prolonging a nearly bankrupt rival and green development costs; it does not explicitly mention inheritance. The later estate cannot supply that missing motive. I also read the retained audit's canonicalization and comparison functions: complete decoded events are serialized with sorted keys; fields are not dropped. The current generalized statement about removing identifiers/timing does not describe that historical check. Restore the exact replay mode, the artifact URL/checkpoint/run mapping, and the evaluated tool-choice settings. The unfinished author placeholder needs a real human authorship decision; it does not establish that no human contributed.

## Where the reviews disagree

- **128/131:** the attachment correctly recognizes a complete, defensible feasibility-conditioned census. The inline review correctly asks whether it is easy, but ease does not invalidate the denominator. Keep the census as context and give the necessary-collateral subset more interpretive attention.
- **Greedy policy:** the attachment proposes a specified baseline and warns against assuming its result. The inline claim that any financing loop pays 131/131 is not a result we have verified. Ordering, even sale, bank stock and asset selection require care. No such baseline was run for this response.
- **Refusal cause:** official Anthropic documentation describes a reasoning-extraction category and native `stop_details`. The archived OpenRouter records do not contain that classification. A present-day direct call would be a new evaluation on a different path, would violate the current no-new-evaluations constraint and would not retroactively establish every historical cause. Retain the strong recorded prompt-path evidence without claiming an observed category that is absent.
- **New evidence:** a new saved-log analysis can provide new descriptive evidence. It is not a fresh independent cohort. “No new evidence” conflates those two claims.
- **Costs:** $141.71 is an accounting total with missing charges, not an invoice or a reliable quoted price for new studies. It cannot establish why the historical sample size was chosen.
- **Theory:** externalities provide a useful interpretation and possible predictions; G13 is not by itself a verified realization of a specific equilibrium theorem. No need to force it into one.
