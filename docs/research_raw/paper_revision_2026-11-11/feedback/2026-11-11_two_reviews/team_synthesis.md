# Assessment of both referee reports

Astra, Sol xhigh, Opus xhigh and Fable 5.2 xhigh each read both supplied reports and the whole active manuscript. Their independent assessments are preserved in this folder. Astra read all three completed peer reports and wrote this synthesis. It records shared conclusions and remaining editorial disagreements; the peers did not review this synthesis. The manuscript remains unchanged. No new evaluations were run.

## The main issues

1. **The bargaining headline exceeds the explanation established by the data.** The opening-swap rejection gap is 30.5 percentage points overall and 16.7 after excluding Grok-authored offers. Grok wrote half the comparator offers and fifteen of its seventeen accepted offers. The within-proposer point differences remain positive, but several cells are tiny. Compensation, proposer identity and response conditions have not been separated from concern about rival development. Retain the observed contrast as exploratory. Do not make it the sole discovery or describe it as an identified effect.

2. **The stated-reason analysis addresses different offers.** The 61 of 74 codes concern refusals where the recipient would complete a group. The headline concerns proposer-only completion versus neither party completing. Those reasons document expressed rival concern elsewhere in the corpus. They cannot explain the headline comparison. Keyword counts in the headline cells are suggestive checks, not a replacement semantic validation.

3. **The finance result deserves more scientific weight.** The complete count, 128 of 131 payable debts paid, remains useful context. The more informative subset has sixteen debts that required mortgages released by selling buildings. Fourteen were paid. Across the reviewers' readings, thirteen successful cases state the subsequent mortgages or a budget including them before acting; one raises them as an open check. Both failures omit the released collateral in their stated sums and declare immediately. Five successful texts are provider-returned explanations rather than applied private notes. This supports prospective statements followed by successful dependent actions. It does not establish internal computation, optimal liquidation or superiority to an unexecuted policy. The sixteen cases are retrospective and include only two failures.

4. **Uncertainty and dependence need a clearer account.** Recounting a selected contrast confirms its arithmetic, not independent replication. The study has five seed blocks, one interacting roster and unequal response conditions. Show the complete offer table and proposer composition. Any resampling must state its target and dependence assumptions. Opus reproduced game-resampling intervals similar to the inline review; Astra has not independently reproduced those intervals. Twenty-game resampling does not address every dependence or post-selection concern. Replace a claimed absence of a cash-bid effect with the observed estimate and its uncertainty.

5. **Configuration conditions affect the behavioral population.** Opus's recovery path and Grok's conflicting channels matter beyond the individual participant because other players respond to their offers. Opus checked source at both run commits and found forced tool choice for Grok, with automatic choice for the others. That setting must be disclosed. Its causal role in conflicts is untested. Preserve real transactions as observations of the configured systems without treating every applied offer as a coherent intended strategy.

6. **The paper needs a clearer hierarchy and a few consequential repairs.** Give prospective assessment and observed execution more space, reduce repeated caveats and reviewer-directed prose, and retain the full bargaining census as context. G18 supports withholding survival cash from the last opponent, not an explicitly stated inheritance motive. G13 supplies the explicit inheritance discussion. Name both stored replay modes, including full-event comparison, and restore the artifact entry point, game map, seeds and evaluated configuration. Human authorship needs resolution before submission. None of this requires new games.

## What remains worth publishing

The corpus documents agents discussing rival development, bluffing over prices, selectively presenting financing options, withdrawing assurances, correcting false claims, and executing financing dependencies. These are positive behavioral observations. A complete offer census and complete debt census place them in context. The paper can explain what agents said, what options they had, and what happened next without claiming that the choices were optimal or that persuasion caused an outcome.

The useful distinction is between expressed strategic consideration, accurate assessment, consistency between text and action, and effectiveness. Evidence for one does not automatically establish the others. Nor does uncertainty about effectiveness erase observed conduct.

## Where judgment remains necessary

Opus and Fable favor making the necessary-collateral result the principal headline. Sol favors giving it more weight within a broader account of bargaining and execution. Astra agrees that it is stronger evidence of local planning than the swap split is of its proposed mechanism, but opposes replacing one overstated headline with another. The final emphasis should connect stated assessment to observed action and report the finance subset at its actual scope.

The team differs over main-text incident count. Opus recommends about three; the user requested five to eight contextual transcripts. Preserve all eight in the research package. Any later compression should keep distinct scientific observations and positive-execution contrasts, with main-text placement determined by the selected format. No incidents were removed during this assessment.

## Feedback to qualify or reject

- A greedy financing policy has no established 131-of-131 score. No such evaluation was executed in this assessment.
- List-price transfers do not establish welfare, fair value or strategic giveaways.
- A present-day provider probe would not by itself identify the historical refusal cause. It is also outside the authorized scope.
- An unfinished byline does not demonstrate an absence of human contribution.
- Venue ratings are reviewer judgments, not results or acceptance forecasts.
- A new descriptive analysis of saved records can add information, but cannot substitute for an independent cohort or controlled test.
- A nonmonotone small-sample cash split does not establish that agents ignore future building opportunities.

## Scope of the next revision

Existing records support better tables, disclosed retrospective selection, carefully specified uncertainty summaries, a fuller account of prospective financing text, source corrections and substantial editorial compression. They do not establish transfer, learning, causal persuasion, optimal strategy or general model rankings. No engine run, replay execution, baseline, provider query, synthetic scenario or new game is authorized. Human validation would require actual human annotation; additional LLM readings cannot be presented as that validation.

The next revision should clarify the science already present. It should not continue searching the same corpus for a more impressive headline or promise that editing can guarantee a top-conference result.
