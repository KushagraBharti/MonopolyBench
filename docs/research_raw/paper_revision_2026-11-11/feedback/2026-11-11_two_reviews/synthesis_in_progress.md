## Fable's new proposal and Astra's response

Fable proposes leading with the necessary-collateral subset rather than the swap contrast. This is a substantive editorial proposal to weigh with the remaining responses. Astra agrees that the subset deserves emphasis but does not yet endorse another headline change.

Fable's crude list-price check is useful but does not eliminate the alternative explanation based on compensation. Astra reproduced median net list values of 170 for author-only swaps and 80 for neither-completing swaps. Recipient value also depends on mortgage status, existing holdings, blocked opportunities and the rival's portfolio. A list-price cut cannot establish that economically unattractive offers have been ruled out. The reported favorable-value counts also need their boundary specified: including zero net value differs from requiring positive net value.

Astra does not accept “refusal triggered by completion” as a causal replacement phrase. Completion was not manipulated. Nor does the reported building-cash cut establish an invariant categorical rule: the cells are small and cash alone omits financing options. A lack of a clear monotone pattern is not proof that agents ignore the rival's next action.

The finance result supports correct prospective statements and successful local execution in specific observed states. It does not by itself establish a general planning capability or make a baseline unnecessary for a difficulty claim. The hypothetical greedy score remains unverified.
