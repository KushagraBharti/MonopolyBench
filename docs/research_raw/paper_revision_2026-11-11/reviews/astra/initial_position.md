Its stated lack of a buyer premium cannot be accepted without system and mortgage cuts, since earlier evidence was heterogeneous. Different pre-estate asset measures need definitions, not a vote between their counts. I will not equate coder kappa with independent validation or 128 paid obligations with a model ranking.
