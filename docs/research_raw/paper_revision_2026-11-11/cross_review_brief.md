# Whole-paper cross-review

This stage follows the user's requirement that each core author writes the whole paper. Every reviewer must read all four complete manuscripts, assess the entire scientific argument, and propose the strongest integrated paper. Do not assign yourself a section or merely defend your own draft.

Root's independent draft was frozen before opening any new peer manuscript. Root may prepare provisional notes on delivered, frozen drafts while the remaining independent author finishes; no unfinished draft is read or steered. The final four-paper comparison and delegated cross-reviews begin after all four drafts are delivered and pinned.

## Questions to settle

1. What is the strongest supported scientific claim about LLM behavior? Distinguish an empirical finding, a new descriptive denominator, familiar game mechanics, and an unmeasured latent ability. Prefer explanations of what the agents attend to and execute over another inventory of defects.
2. Which bargaining comparisons survive meaningful alternatives? Check completion categories, opening versus later offers, configuration and seed concentration, exact cash-concession transitions, single-deed filters, mortgage status, and asking versus bidding. Reconcile 4/2/1 versus 5/1/1 same-seller/game/deed summaries by listing the actual rows and estimands. Do not select the more dramatic definition without a reason.
3. Does the new liquidation census close correctly on original payment or bankruptcy events? Reconcile all 188 episodes, 128 payments, 61 multi-action payments, and the definition of 131 payable episodes. Check whether success includes any defaults or incompatible channels, and distinguish executed local solvency from general strategy quality.
4. What can development exposure and pre-inheritance positions tell us? Different cash, book-value and building-level proxies can coexist. Do not turn an exposure normalization or eventual-winner conditioning into a causal effect.
5. Do quotations, source channels and delivered context support each interpretation? Preserve eight useful mechanisms, including competent correction and execution. Do not inflate severity, infer intent from silence, or count authorized bluffing as instruction violation.
6. Is the prompt/provider condition stated accurately and proportionately? Private-note removal retains deception permission; usage tokens are not cognition; public context remains; the historical refusal category is unrecorded. Conditions belong once in setup and where they change a result, with detail in the appendix.
7. What do the primary papers actually support, and what comparison matters? Do not pad the bibliography or make absence claims from abstracts. Assess novelty, scientific relevance and presentation against real prior work.
8. Which questions remain unanswered under the no-new-evaluation scope? Keep all eight plus A–C at supported levels. No rhetorical rewrite can supply a baseline, causal learning, persuasion, transfer or human validation.

9. Fable's liquidity-centered paper supplies a fourth complete argument. Assess its cash-band rejection contrast, refusal-to-sale sequences, and post-sale construction/win association with the same care as the other drafts. Verify the unit (offer, deed, group, player-game), temporal selection, mortgage/recipient/stage composition, chosen cash and eight-turn thresholds, and whether the title's "until" is warranted. Do not elevate an exploratory association into a causal liquidity effect or count several sales by one winner as independent victories. Consider whether this is the strongest central story or supporting evidence for bargaining over rivals' opportunities.

All four completed packages are now frozen in `four_draft_hashes.json` (235 source/data/document files). Do not modify them. ## Deliverables in each reviewer's own directory

- `initial_position.md`, before reading new peer reviews or root adjudication.
- `review.md`: full comparison of all four papers, strengths, major objections, own-draft corrections, dissent, and the proposed central claim.
- `findings.json`: stable IDs, severity, exact affected source passages and proposed resolution; separate verified evidence from inherited results.
- `integration_proposal.md`: complete final-paper architecture, title/abstract priorities, figures/tables, all eight incidents and the question map.
- `replacement_prose.tex`: finished scholarly replacements for the central argument and any material revisions, not only editing instructions.
- `verification.md`: sources, scripts, hashes, scope and remaining risks.

No new evaluations, model calls, games, engine execution, replay/branch runs, synthetic tests or baselines. Read-only reconstruction and arithmetic on saved events is permitted; call it event reduction, not a newly executed replay. No canonical/shared edits, commits, pushes, subagents, or messages to finished peers. Source and build verification are allowed in the reviewer's own directory. Material objections matter more than cosmetic perfection or review scores. Root also performs the full cross-review.
