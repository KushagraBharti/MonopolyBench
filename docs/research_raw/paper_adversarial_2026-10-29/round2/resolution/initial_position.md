The relevant question is whether the stated populations can be reconstructed from canonical decisions and saved HTTP records. I will reconstruct the construction-tool-listed decision denominator and split applied END by fallback, selected-attempt name conflict, and no detected name conflict. A row's presence establishes delivery to a prompt, not attention, belief, or causal influence. Absence of these known rows does not establish that a prompt was otherwise correct.
