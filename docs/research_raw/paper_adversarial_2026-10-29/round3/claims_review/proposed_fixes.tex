% Optional compact contribution clarification, suitable after the protocol table.
The audit returns different kinds of result. A permitted engine execution can
witness a local endpoint. A resource bound or exhaustive supported graph can
exclude that endpoint. A channel disagreement establishes a recorded discrepancy
without choosing an authoritative intention. A delivered message establishes
exposure, while an explicit correction establishes what the recipient recorded.
Claims about latent belief or causal persuasion remain unresolved without
additional evidence. These outputs are not interchangeable behavioral labels.
