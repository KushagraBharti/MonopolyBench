# Initial independent position

Recorded on 2026-10-29 before opening any prior review report. I read the complete frozen LaTeX manuscript, including its input tables and appendices. I have not yet executed or inspected the certificate implementation, consulted its internal review records, or inspected the rendered PDF. The parent task disclosed that a prior reviewer found an atomic-search completeness flaw and that this revision documents a guard. That disclosure is prior knowledge, not my discovery. All substantive impressions below come from the round-3 baseline manuscript.

## Provisional judgment

The revised title, abstract, and conclusion mostly fit a bounded methods-and-case-study paper. This manuscript does not currently claim a model ranking, a deception rate, an effect of capability on alignment, or a generic planning solver. A rejection based only on the absence of those experiments would evaluate a different paper. The contribution is also more concrete than a list of familiar precautions. It fixes a historical prefix, actor, intervention class, and endpoint; supplies replayed witnesses or restricted impossibility evidence; and reports full finite populations for elimination and fallback construction. The stated 56/3/1 elimination split and 63/66 reachability result could be useful reproducible findings if their implementation and population selection hold up.

The strongest remaining rejection argument is that the proposed general method is under-specified as a reusable claim adjudication procedure and under-evaluated as a measurement method. The executable portion tests selected feasibility predicates. The broader title concerns behavioral claims, while the most consequential interpretive step is deciding which predicate, provenance class, or recipient text warrants which behavioral label. No frozen external annotation target or comparative error study measures that step. The paper acknowledges this limitation, so it is a question of contribution adequacy rather than a concealed statistical error. A bounded fix is to make the deliverable explicitly a typed certificate audit of two finite populations, with selected social counterexamples, and show a compact claim-to-certificate ledger. It should not promise annotation improvement without measuring it.

## Questions to resolve in the artifact

1. What exactly are the 126 units? The manuscript calls them cases, prefixes, and certificates in nearby passages. They appear to comprise sixty elimination decisions and sixty-six fallback decisions, with 132 fallback attempts and multiple target-specific outcomes. These should not become 126 independent claims, successful repairs, or observed strategy errors. A case with a malformed or absent target is not an impossibility certificate for that missing target.
2. Does the portable package independently recover the census and the attempted construction targets from original records, or consume a hand-prepared manifest whose completeness and transcription need a separate check? Both are useful, but they support different reproduction claims.
3. Does the negative checker really reject every visited state that can offer a building sale, and does the historical hotel lock remain mortgage-only under the actual engine's action-name gate? This must be judged against the current restriction rather than the acknowledged superseded generic search claim.
4. Does construction expansion preserve the exact multiset and a single permitted action slot? Reachability of a reshaped target is not legality of the submitted encoding. The prose is careful on this point; the artifact must match it.
5. Are the construction and debt bounds admissible for the exact permitted inflows and endpoint? A successful witness is easier to certify than a negative result.

## Empirical and denominator assessment

The paper explicitly distinguishes 43/63 construction over all trade completions from 34/42 conditional on entry buildability. It retains zero cells and dependent seed blocks. It also separates applied END provenance from the fact that a construction tool was listed. These are appropriately bounded descriptive counts, not adjustment for selection or fair capability comparisons. The 34/42 condition is itself affected by acquisition and prior play; the manuscript says so. I do not currently see a false inferential denominator in these passages.

The recovery table remains potentially confusing. The narrative uses 20/25, 6/18, and 5/5 among successful construction resolutions, whereas the table also exposes 29, 19, and 5 rejected decisions and a separate 61-decision Opus condition. The conditional quantity is legitimate, but a general reader may read it as retry reliability. Showing the all-rejection denominators alongside it would make that error harder.

The social examples rebut observable inference rules, such as equating acceptance with explicit endorsement. They cannot establish that a recipient's latent belief is correct merely because its note corrects a premise. The main text mostly respects that distinction. I will look for residual wording that silently turns recorded correction into actual knowledge, intent, or lack of influence.

## Expected recommendation if checks pass

This could be a defensible focused artifact/case-study contribution, but not an unqualified strong accept for a broad empirical agent-evaluation paper. I expect to recommend tightening the method's target and making the certificate accounting explicit. I will not count acknowledged historical bugs as current falsifications unless the guarded implementation still makes the unsupported claim. No prior-review position has been adopted at this stage.
