# Independent initial position

Saved before opening earlier reviewer reports or the coordinator charter. Based on the frozen section sources and evidence map; PDF extraction has not yet succeeded.

The strongest contribution is a bounded procedure for falsifying interpretations of agent traces through historical legal-sequence tests. The paper is unusually careful about causal attribution, but caution is not itself a scientific result. Its principal risk is that the integrated descriptive account duplicates prior economic-agent benchmarks while the proposed procedure remains a collection of selected demonstrations rather than a reusable, evaluated method.

I would provisionally request major revision. The critical tests are whether negative feasibility results have exhaustive certificates, whether recoverable construction plans preserve the encoded action or merely an inferred intention, and whether denominator choices materially change conclusions across games. The rent comparison correctly disclaims ROI but still risks inviting that reading. Review overlap is explicitly acknowledged; independence must mean separately derived source checks, not multiple endorsements. Channel absence prevents comparable intent prevalence. I have not yet independently reproduced any central quantity and do not treat the evidence map as verification.
