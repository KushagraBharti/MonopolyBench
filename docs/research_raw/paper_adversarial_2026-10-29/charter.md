# Adversarial manuscript revision

The user requested repeated, independent adversarial reviews and substantive improvements to the complete first draft. The target is the strongest paper the evidence supports, not a promised score or an unsupported superiority claim.

## Protected baseline

The original 34-page draft is frozen under `baseline/`, with a hash manifest. The prior phase-3 outline and literature artifacts remain protected. Revisions will update the separate manuscript in `paper_draft_2026-10-29/`; original reviews and findings remain readable.

## Review process

1. Three fresh reviewers read the complete baseline independently before consulting prior peer judgments. They challenge contribution and conceptual organization, literature and external validity, and evidence and identifiability. Each must also attack the paper outside its specialty.
2. The coordinator checks objections against primary sources and records an explicit disposition. Major edits need evidence, not majority vote. Rejected objections retain a reason and source. New analyses use existing records; no engine, prompt, canonical game or experimental treatment changes are authorized.
3. Fresh reviewers receive the revised manuscript and challenge both surviving claims and the revisions. A final round tests scientific sufficiency, reproducibility, and presentation after integration. New reviews are new tasks, not restarts of finished threads.
4. Completion requires no unresolved demonstrated critical/high factual or inferential defects, explicit remaining limitations, all claims traceable at their stated scope, clean builds, and inspected figures/tables. Reviewer ratings remain advisory. Repeated positive reviews cannot prove a 10/10 paper.

## Questions that matter

- What is the one important problem this paper resolves, and what should change in evaluation practice after reading it?
- Which observations support a general method, and which remain illustrative cases in a small cohort?
- What is genuinely added beyond the strongest primary-source account of Vending-Bench/Arena, AgentMonopoly, FinBoardBench and DECE?
- Can apparent weaknesses become explicit, testable measurement distinctions without disguising missing evidence as a contribution?
- Which proposed improvements sharpen the argument, and which only lengthen it?
- What result would falsify the central interpretation? Is that test already possible in the retained record?

## Coordinator's initial hypothesis

The first draft has unusually strong source checks but risks reading as a catalog of findings and caveats. A stronger organizing contribution may be the identification of agent behavior from complete trajectories: a recorded endpoint must be decomposed into opportunity, reachable legal sequence, response resolution, delivered information and consequence. That hypothesis must earn its place by explaining several independent economic and social results and by comparison with prior work. It is not yet an accepted reframing.

No new paid games, provider experiments, commits or pushes. Reviewers write only within their assigned review directory; the coordinator owns manuscript edits.
