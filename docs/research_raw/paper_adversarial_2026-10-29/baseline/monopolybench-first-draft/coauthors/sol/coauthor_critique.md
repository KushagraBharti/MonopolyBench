## Original objections and their resolution

1. **A narrow audit framing can displace the scientific object.** The paper's motivation is sustained capability and conduct in a competitive economy. Response defects matter when they distinguish failed execution from a reserve choice; they should not consume the introduction or every result heading. I supplied complete acquisition/finance/income trajectories and a G20 allocation case, rather than a catalogue of software problems. The optional title and abstract were written only after those findings. This remains an emphasis recommendation, not a claim that an agent with more errors has less innate capability.

2. **A capability–alignment curve cannot use current wins as its capability axis.** The current roster mixes model, provider and response conditions; the five rotated blocks create dependent opportunities. Neither a correlation of wins with selected social labels nor a weighted profit/deception scalar addresses the motivating question. I support explicit future norms, independent capability measurements and repeated matched continuations. I also object to the opposite overstatement: descriptive adherence to a specified norm does not always require randomization. The root methods already distinguish these levels appropriately.

3. **The whole path cannot disappear behind conditional buildability.** The 34/42 development statistic concerns entry-buildable groups, whose cash, access and holdings result from prior play. I retain 43/63 whole-path deployment and the 54 legal-at-some-point count. Received END/content conflicts are observable discrepancies, not uniformly lost executable builds. The retained original checks include one uneven content plan and one financing request. This correction is accepted from the final phase-3 reconciliation.

4. **Financial or social success is not a causal explanation.** A deal completing both sides' groups is a real bilateral consequence but does not establish that both positions improved against a leader. In Opus's G4 cooperation paragraph, replace that comparative-value language with the observed completion of buildable groups. A recipient correcting a premise and still settling can have benefited from the terms; it does not prove immunity to framing. Conversely, G13 d251's earlier motive prevents identification of a $70 persuasion effect, not all possible influence. Root's recipient-response methods preserve this distinction.

5. **Channels are not separate task contracts.** Opus correctly notices that action-only recovery removes the private-note requirement and its truthfulness sentence. However, “provider reasoning carries no instruction at all” exceeds that evidence. Neither prompt directly specifies the content of the returned provider-summary channel; the agent still receives task instructions. Recommended wording: the standard and action-only conditions impose different output requirements, and the provider summary is a separately observed channel without a direct channel-specific reporting requirement. Root should qualify its general setup statement about private honesty by condition. This objection was sent to the running coauthor and root, without using auto or queue.

6. **Training requirements need to be separated by purpose.** Opus's final paragraph groups binding contract objects, private bilateral channels and rewards as missing components, then implies RL or monitor training requires them all. Private bilateral channels are an optional environment extension, not a requirement for either learning method. A norm object is useful for commitment tasks; reward, horizon, termination/truncation, held-out evaluation and fresh policy execution are distinct training requirements. Root's present validation section avoids the stronger claim. No training benefit is measured.

7. **An invalid order is not an originally legal plan.** All 61 Opus purchase multisets execute after reordering, but the submitted ordering is invalid. “Discarded legal plans” should become “affordable allocations recoverable by reordering the submitted plans.” This preserves the substantive loss of an executable target without silently treating the repaired sequence as the original action. Likewise, matching the next recorded events in a branch is different from continuing the entire historical suffix after a divergent action. Root's validation text currently uses the narrower formulation.
