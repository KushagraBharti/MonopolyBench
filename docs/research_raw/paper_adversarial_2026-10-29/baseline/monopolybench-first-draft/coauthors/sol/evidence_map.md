# Sol manuscript evidence map

The prose is in [methods_economics.tex](methods_economics.tex); [framing_conduct_bridge.tex](framing_conduct_bridge.tex) supplies adjacent prose, and [title_abstract.tex](title_abstract.tex) was written after the findings. Game numbers identify the frozen 20-game mapping. `dNNN` abbreviates a full decision suffix, not a turn. All dollar amounts are game dollars.

The fresh [verification](verification.json), produced by [verify_economic_claims.py](verify_economic_claims.py), independently reduces the original event streams and re-reads original initial payloads for negotiation opportunity counts. Its original-event paths and SHA256 values are the direct source index. It also reconciles retained proof/export counts. It **does not rerun the engine branches**, make new semantic annotations or independently validate every peer claim. Counts were known before checking. The original phase-2 proofs remain the authority for branch legality and channel readings.

Abbreviations below link to original audited sources rather than the displayed plot. P1, P2 and P3 mean the three dated research packages. Peer-authored analysis is attributed in each row.
