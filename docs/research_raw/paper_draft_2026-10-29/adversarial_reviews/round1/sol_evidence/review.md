# Evidence and methods review

Recommendation: major revision. The paper supports a careful descriptive account of this cohort. It does not yet establish the value of its adjudication method as a reusable research contribution. I found no independently demonstrated numerical contradiction in the quantities I recomputed. This is not certification of the remaining quantities.

## What knowledge changes

The useful result is that superficially similar outcomes have different executable explanations. A current menu can understate reachable liquidation, nominal collateral can overstate it, and END can originate in resolution rather than a stated hold. These distinctions matter outside Monopoly wherever actions change subsequent admissible actions. Their frequency, difficulty, and effect on evaluation remain unmeasured outside the selected cases. The strongest rival is a conventional trace audit with simulator access. The paper must show what exact-prefix execution adds over that rival, rather than claim novelty for replay.

The introduction's contribution passage, “apply an exact-prefix adjudication protocol,” is defensible as a description of work performed. It is insufficient as an evaluated method. A deterministic simulator plus a legal solution witness is a verification technique; the scientific result would be a reproducible account of which plausible judgments it changes, under a defined claim population and comparison procedure. No paid experiments are needed to make that result stronger.

## Findings

E01, major, missing evidence: Section 3.3 and introduction. Define a claim record with historical prefix hash, acting player, admissible intervention class, endpoint predicate, certificate, and exclusions. Separate existential witnesses from impossibility certificates. One successful route proves feasibility; failure to find a route does not prove infeasibility. The nominal bound supplies a valid negative argument only if every allowed own-asset liquidation inflow is bounded by its terms. Publish this argument and the hotel-lock search's action enumeration, state deduplication and stopping rule. The current prose names endpoints but gives no single runnable case suite. This is a reproducibility and method-evaluation gap, not evidence that the retained engine tests are wrong.

E02, major, scope and presentation: recovery table and Appendix B. The retained first Opus witness, G2 d75, submits three HOUSE operations with count 3; its repair splits them into nine count-1 operations. My independent algebraic check confirms matching deed/kind purchase multisets for all 61 retained witnesses. It does not confirm that the original ordered action is legal. “Reordered multiset” should explicitly permit splitting counts and reordering atomic purchases. Describe recoverable target allocations rather than valid plans or pure serialization defects. The benchmark expressly validates intermediate operations. Economic allocation competence and interface execution competence are different constructs; preserving one does not excuse failure of the other. The text generally respects this distinction, but the method should formalize it.

E03, moderate, denominator sensitivity missing: Section 3.2, abstract's 43/63 and 34/42. These answer different questions correctly. Neither is an independent-trial success probability. Add a per-game ledger containing completion, terminal inheritance, opportunity access, entry feasibility, later financing and applied build. Report pooled counts alongside distribution across games and seed blocks, retaining zero-denominator games. A few prolific games can dominate an episode-level statistic. This additional descriptive analysis is optional for correctness but materially strengthens interpretability. Do not substitute a decision-level confidence interval. A first actual management window also conditions on survival until access; explicitly count acquisitions lost before that window rather than let readers infer the denominator includes them.

E04, moderate, presentation: Section 3.2's post-spend receipts and development exhibit. The equation correctly excludes earlier receipts and explicitly disclaims incremental ROI. It still combines cumulative later spending with rent beginning at the first expenditure, inherited improvements and unequal ownership exposure. A later large purchase cannot explain earlier receipts in that window. Preserve a cumulative event-time spending/receipt ledger or add counts by inherited development and exposure turns. No factual correction to the reported totals is established here. Avoid using the scatter's balance against the diagonal as evidence of investment quality.

E05, major, release boundary: Appendix D says the package regenerates figures and PDF, while a full environment release remains future work. That disclosure is appropriate. It also means a reader receiving only this package cannot independently reproduce the primary behavioral claims from it. My raw recount used saved_games outside the manuscript package; source paths and hashes alone do not supply those files. A publication contribution centered on executable adjudication needs a minimal permitted raw-prefix bundle and environment lock, not only aggregate JSON. Include one positive liquidation, the hotel lock, one recoverable allocation, and a historical successful liquidation with commands and expected event hashes. Clean-environment execution is an acceptance criterion, not something this review performed.

E06, moderate, independence boundary: Section 3.5 says four streams “each considered all 614 catalog entries.” Appendix D separately calls the writing-stage recount independent. These are different claims. Initial judgments preserved before peer exposure are evidence of independence only for the judgments and inputs they actually covered. Shared extraction candidates and shared source packets can reproduce correlated omissions. Supply a compact provenance matrix listing claim, first source derivation, reviewer exposure time, independently executed check and inherited result. I did not inspect enough original review histories to verify the universal 614-entry claim. Do not cite this review as an independent replication of those histories.

E07, moderate, attribution: Section 6's observation defects and social cases. The paper rightly declines to infer latent intent from received tool/content conflict or persuasion from settlement. Make that same separation visible in each case record: emitted proposition, delivered prompt occurrence, recipient text, applied action, economic consequence, and unknown internal cause. The 149 omitted timeline occurrences cannot measure semantic nonexposure; duplicate statements and other fields matter. Likewise, 1,394 Opus decisions lacking authored private notes in memory do not identify the effect of note removal. A common-channel descriptive inventory can support public-action comparisons, not intent-rate comparisons. The existing caveats are substantive and should remain.

## Low-cost analysis that would change the paper

Freeze a finite set of mechanically decidable claims from the existing corpus before running a new audit. Use all 60 eliminations and all 66 defaulted construction decisions. For liquidation, classify by current-menu-only assessment, nominal bound and legal reachability. For construction, compare original ordered operations with allocation-preserving atomic expansion. Report a transition table of judgments changed by each information level, grouped by game; retain all failures and unknowns. Emit executable witnesses for positive cases and bound/search certificates for negatives. This directly tests the method's added information while avoiding a new semantic-label prevalence claim. A frozen case manifest and a small runner would be more valuable than another round of model endorsement.

## Literature boundary

Primary web verification accessed AgentMonopoly arXiv:2610.29330v2, especially Sections 4 and A.1–A.2, and Arena arXiv:2608.14825v3, Sections 2 and 4 and Appendix D. Their published designs already cover logged economic interaction and state-checked communication respectively. This supports the draft's restrained novelty positioning. I have not independently verified all 32 references, repository/paper game-count discrepancy, or the precise bankruptcy implementation in the rival repository. Those details remain inherited rather than newly certified.

## Advisory scorecard

Scores are on a five-point scale. Significance 3: consequential measurement problem, with little evidence of transfer. Soundness 3: careful bounded interpretations and supported recounts, but executable method evaluation and several source claims remain unreplicated here. Novelty 2.5: integrated adjudication may be useful; logging, replay and state-based checking have close precedents. Clarity 3: explicit units help, but many caveats and cases obscure the primary result. Reproducibility 2.5: rich local records and scripts exist; the manuscript package is not the behavioral reproduction package.

The three strongest rejection reasons are an insufficiently evaluated methodological contribution, an incomplete reader-facing executable release, and a selected descriptive cohort whose generalizable research conclusion remains weak.

The three changes most likely to improve the paper are a frozen all-eliminations/all-defaults method comparison, a minimal runnable positive/negative witness suite with certificates, and a shorter central argument organized around what historical execution changes about otherwise plausible evaluations.
