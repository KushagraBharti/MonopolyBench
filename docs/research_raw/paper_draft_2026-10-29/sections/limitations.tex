\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Cohort and dependence.}
Twenty games from five seed blocks are a small, selected evaluation of four configured systems. Cyclic seat balance does not cover all opponent orders or separate skill from realized movement. Raw dice sharing does not preserve the semantic use of draws, board positions or future opportunities. We make no model ranking, capability trend, seat-effect estimate or generalization from decision-level sample size to independent evidence. The observed source-version boundary and refusal shift are not randomized treatments. Each agent faces the same three opponent models throughout, with their configured names visible in its state. Prior familiarity with the widely documented game may also influence play.

\paragraph{Implementation scope.}
Results depend on the declared rules, particularly intact creditor inheritance, finite stock, per-operation even-building validation and the opportunity to declare bankruptcy. Alternative Monopoly implementations can change the financing and social incentives. Engine-grounded truth is truth for this benchmark, not necessarily for another ruleset. Finding and documenting an observation or recovery defect does not identify how repairing it would change the cohort's outcomes.

\paragraph{Observation and interpretation.}
Saved channels differ in availability and reliability. Some provider reasoning is encrypted or unreturned; private notes can be missing, synthetic or inconsistent with other returned text. Recipient exposure establishes delivered information rather than attention, belief or reliance. Case-specific contradictory text supports a narrower statement than stable model knowledge or intent. We do not estimate latent deception prevalence, nor treat authorized public deception as demonstrated instruction misalignment.

\paragraph{Selection and adjudication.}
Definitions and semantic review categories developed during exploration and were not preregistered. The semantic examples are selected from an exhaustively traversed record, but exhaustive traversal is not exhaustive validated labeling of every possible proposition. The catalog contains overlapping cases and historical entries, and the common sample comes from a filtered pool. Four model-assisted review streams are not four independent human annotators. Initial judgments and revisions remain available, but no agreement statistic is presented as label validity. Additional blinded human adjudication would be valuable for a future behavioral benchmark.

The temporal extension codes public and applied-private fields in the fixed public-unflagged sample, not all provider reasoning. Missing channels and the original filtering prevent a population planning or dishonesty rate. Endpoint rules were refined while examining the text; the eight landing policies form a retrospectively specified subset. The negotiation-reason review likewise exposes coding-boundary disagreements. Its complete mechanical offer denominator does not make every semantic interpretation objective. We preserve separate reader labels and checked source spans rather than converting agreement into a claim of latent intent.

\paragraph{Local alternatives and economic value.}
A feasible legal route establishes an endpoint under stated assumptions. It does not show that an agent would select it, that another player would accept a proposed exchange, or that the altered trajectory would preserve later draws and decisions. Current-debt payment need not be optimal, and a marginal rent threshold on an observed landing is not a winner counterfactual. Paid rent omits unpaid terminal demands, salvage, estate transfers and unmeasured deterrence; the spending-unit results are neither net profit nor incremental ROI.

\paragraph{Validation and release.}
All recorded actions and memory windows, twenty general continuation prefixes, and 126 certificate prefixes are checked. The portable mechanical suite runs in a clean environment, but does not reconstruct every full prompt or certify every possible branch. Its construction ordering is not a complete solver; graph-based negative certification rejects states offering building sales because atomic enumeration is incomplete there. Reference-policy continuations are not LLM suffix experiments. Code and saved games are publicly tracked; the new revision artifacts accompany this draft but are not claimed to be published at that commit. External scientific replication, trained-policy evaluation and transfer to another environment remain untested.

\paragraph{The new pilot is a readiness test.}
The twenty additional games contain baseline policies, not the evaluated LLMs. Two selected seeds and a 1,000-decision observation limit support execution and censoring diagnostics, not a performance comparison with the uncapped historical cohort. Static fixture legality does not establish reachability, strategic optimality or useful task diversity. The subsequent state-invariant screen was exploratory. The unexecuted designs in Appendix~\ref{sec:agenda-prospective-evaluation} still require adjudicated tasks, executable interventions, an appropriate model/opponent roster, precision calculations and independent outcomes. A protocol sketch or a spending allocation would not by itself complete those experiments.
