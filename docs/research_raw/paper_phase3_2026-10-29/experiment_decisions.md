# Step 11: which claims need another experiment?

**Decision: the bounded descriptive paper can be drafted from the existing 20-game cohort. No new paid game is required for the retained claims.** This does not mean the evidence supports every possible capability or alignment claim. New studies become necessary when the claim changes.

Astra, Sol, Opus and Fable each assessed the entire claim set. Their individual matrices remain in their folders. This shared matrix incorporates the completed phase-2 audits, the phase-3 deployment residual and the literature corrections. Existing-log recomputation, local engine execution and a fresh model-play experiment are different activities.

## Retained claims and completed support

| Question | Existing support | Boundary retained in the paper |
|---|---|---|
| What happened across seeds and seats? | All 20 outcomes, five seed blocks and four rotations; F2 and T1. | Descriptive configured-agent profiles. No universal ranking, causal seat effect or luck/skill decomposition. |
| How did agents assemble and use assets? | Independent event accounting; ownership routes, group episodes, trade terms, finance and terminal reconciliation. | Final-deed route differs from portfolio composition. Terminal inheritance has no follow-up. |
| Who initiated and responded to negotiations? | Proposal counts, own-turn propensity, intensity and responder outcomes; F3/T2. | Eligibility, survival and opponents are endogenous. Activity is not effectiveness. |
| What did each side gain from a deal? | All 113 settlements and mandatory interest; 60/43/10 completion split. | Set completion and retained cash are consequences, not full trade welfare or an optimal-value score. |
| Did a completed set get developed? | All 63 trade groups; 43 first-window builds, 34/42 entry-buildable builds; all-63 resolution table. | Separate access, state on entry, later finance and elapsed latency. |
| Why do the eight entry-buildable non-builds differ? | Three defaults, three received-action/content conflicts and two ENDs with explicit hold rationales; independent Astra check of original ending decisions. | A conflict is not necessarily a lost executable build. Source and intent remain unresolved. |
| Could rejected plans execute? | All 61 Opus purchase multisets execute after interleaving; complete historical streams replay. Recovery allocations separately audited. | Legal existence at the prefix, not repaired wins or a measured policy-treatment effect. |
| How did construction relate to receipts and inheritance? | 103 spending units; both rent windows, creditor outcomes and inherited-deed provenance; F5. | Paid receipts are not net/incremental ROI. Zero rent is not an investment-failure label. |
| Could eliminated players pay the current debt? | All 60 elimination events; 56 below even a generous bound, three legal payment routes, one genuine hotel lock; F6. | Own legal finance at the specified phase. No assumed help, rationality judgment or alternative winner. |
| Did finite stock matter? | Event reconstruction matches all 6,989 delivered bank states; nine games reach zero; original-checked local examples. | Inventory is not demand. No corpus frequency of binding construction denial is claimed. |
| What do misleading claims and assurances establish? | Original speaker/recipient records, actual exposure, financial checks, prior motives and contrasting responses. | Selected propositions and cases, not a cross-model deception rate or causal persuasion effect. |
| Can the environment reset and branch? | Full replay; 6,989 memory windows; 20 tested mid-game branch prefixes spanning five decision types; additional local audits. | No general full-prompt equality, packaged RL adapter, successful training or guaranteed rollout termination. |

## Optional analysis using existing records

These would strengthen particular claims; none is a prerequisite for the current figures and outline.

| Analysis | Claim it could support | Required design before reporting a number |
|---|---|---|
| Code factual public propositions across the corpus | Incidence of specified false claims and recipient responses | Define the proposition population and time reference; distinguish public, private and provider channels; preserve missingness and adjudicate against originals. Do not divide candidate counts by decisions. |
| Count stock-only construction restrictions | Frequency of immediate build opportunities blocked by stock | Hold ownership, cash, phase and action fixed; test the legal predicate under a stock relaxation. Separately identify reallocation and later financing; no denial-of-victory claim. |
| Code rival-funding reasons in trade responses | Frequency of explicit funding-denial rationales | Fixed offer/response unit and codebook, eligibility, overlapping motives and channel availability; acceptance still does not establish premise uptake. |
| Run local reference policies | Calibration of selected metrics against policies with known differences | Predefine what differs and what the metric should distinguish. Non-trading policies cannot validate a trade-opportunity denominator. This would create new unpaid games and is not performed in this stage. |

Fable preferred the first three codings before the manuscript; the integrated decision is to keep the present bounded captions and list the codings as optional. This preserves the dissent without converting it into a hidden requirement or an unperformed result.

## Stronger claims that require a new design

| Stronger claim | Why the current cohort cannot establish it | Study needed if retained |
|---|---|---|
| Capability increases or reduces misalignment | Four different-provider configurations at one time point do not define a capability-growth axis; conduct norms differ. | Independently measured capability or within-lineage tiers, fixed norms, genuine compliance/violation opportunities, repeated matched situations and controlled resource/response conditions. |
| A wrapper or refusal policy caused weaker play | Policy, provider condition, memory and trajectory differ together. | Controlled response-policy or context intervention with repeated continuations; report utility and conduct separately. |
| Corrected trade histories improve outcomes | Exposure is observed; reliance and changed-game consequences are not identified. | Controlled corrected-history condition or specified prefix continuations, with memory reconstruction and repeated model runs. |
| A pitch caused a concession or betrayal | Prior motives, price and channel inconsistencies supply alternatives. | Matched communication intervention with a predefined behavioral endpoint and recipient information held constant. |
| A trace detects intent or predicts the next action | No validated monitor and no comparable channel population exist. | Defined input/output task, held-out cases, baseline predictions, explicit channel availability and independently checked outcomes. |
| The benchmark trains safer or more capable agents | Reset support is not a training result. | Implement a reward/training interface, separate training and evaluation tasks, assess held-out transfer and norm adherence, and audit reward exploitation. |
| MonopolyBench is generally better or more realistic | A feature inventory is not a comparative test. | State a criterion—measurement validity, predictive transfer, reproducibility or cost—and evaluate competing instruments under an appropriate common protocol. |

## Execution and publication boundary

No new paid calls, new model games or benchmark behavior changes were made for these decisions. Phase-3 work consists of source research, existing-record analyses and downstream exhibits. Public release, a clean-environment reproduction and final manuscript/PDF verification remain steps 17–18.
