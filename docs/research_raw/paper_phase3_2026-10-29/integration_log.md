## Preserved disagreement and remaining gates

Fable favors three additional existing-log coding passes; the shared outline does not require them because it retains selected-case and stock-inventory claims rather than population prevalence or binding-denial claims. Optional reference-policy runs likewise remain a proposal, not completed validation.

The current evidence does not identify a capability-growth/alignment relationship, causal persuasion, intrinsic model rankings, intent from a privileged channel, general monitor accuracy, training benefit or real-world safety transfer. These are claim-dependent future studies, not reasons to discard the bounded observational paper.

The final paper, clean release and submission package have not been produced in this stage. The completed deliverable is a substantive research and drafting package for steps 11–14.
