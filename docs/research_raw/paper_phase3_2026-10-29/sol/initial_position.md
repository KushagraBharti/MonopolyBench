# Sol's independent initial position

Recorded 2026-10-29 before reading any new phase-3 peer proposal. Read the phase-3 README and phase-2 Astra ledger/reconciliation and Sol, Opus, Fable final syntheses. Some earlier peer prose is explicitly superseded by Astra's final ledger. No new game or model call is proposed for this authorized stage.

## Argument and research questions

The paper should ask how an agent sustains an economic objective across delayed consequences and strategic interaction, what its conduct reveals about adherence to the actual instructions, and how an environment makes those judgments auditable. These questions belong together. Money and property are observable stakes through which we study planning, social interaction, and the limits of safety inference. Finance alone does not state the scientific ambition; misalignment alone does not describe the present evidence.

My provisional contribution is a measurement protocol and a richly observed cohort in an executable strategic environment. The valuable distinction is between an applied action, a feasible intended allocation, a recorded explanation, an exposed message, a recipient response, and a causal effect. These distinctions can change the interpretation of exactly the same game. A generic claim to be the first multiplayer economic environment, the first Monopoly benchmark, or the first speech/action audit is implausible and unnecessary.

## Claim sufficiency

Twenty games, five seed blocks, four cyclic rotations, four configured players, 6,989 decisions and 8,679 attempts support within-cohort descriptions. The model, wrapper, retry, prompt, memory and response conditions jointly define the tested agent. Wins 8/7/3/2 are observed outcomes, not intrinsic provider ranks. Rotations balance seats but do not equalize trajectories or random opportunities. Neither decision-level p-values nor a latent capability ordering is justified by these data.

Already sufficient with final audits: acquisition/estate/development accounting; trade-unit descriptions; the 103 spending units and both paid-rent windows; 58 winner spending groups with 34 inherited-containing and 30 estate-completed; all 61 Opus default plans reorderable; 60 elimination classifications with three tested current-debt routes, 56 upper-bound failures and one stock lock; selected source-checked social cases and channel conflicts. These are descriptive or exact local feasibility claims.

Existing-log work can strengthen denominators for rule errors, public financial falsehoods, promise opportunities, shortage restrictions and multi-creditor prompt defects. It cannot identify a counterfactual winner, causal persuasion, an inheritance advantage, causal wrapper damage, or increasing misalignment with increasing capability. A controlled intervention would be necessary only if we insist on those stronger claims. No new paid games are necessary to make the bounded paper coherent.

Public deception is authorized. It can be studied as strategic conduct, but cannot by itself be labeled instruction misalignment. Private falsehood is also not self-authenticating evidence of dishonesty. The benchmark can expose mismatches among instructions, records and outcomes without revealing hidden intent. Returned reasoning is evidence of a recorded account, with incomplete access and component uncertainty.

## Literature position to verify

Read primary methods and appendices for the named benchmarks, original and current Vending-Bench variants, direct Monopoly competitors, longitudinal finance, negotiation and strategic-conduct work. Build a comparison by task structure, opponent incentives, temporal consequences, normative contract, observations, evaluation unit, reliability controls and artifact access. Do not make absence claims from abstracts. Treat author pages/software as such, and freeze exact versions.

The closest competitors should lead related work. AgentMonopoly challenges any first-Monopoly or first-explanation/action framing. FinBoardBench challenges a first-longitudinal-finance framing. Vending-Bench Arena challenges the generic multiplayer-economics gap. The stronger possibility is an integrated evidence methodology combining actual histories, phase-specific finance, response-condition diagnostics, event-replay checks and proposition-level social chronologies. This is a candidate positioning to test against sources, not an established novelty claim.

## Exhibits

Keep economic and social evidence in the main paper. Show an outcome profile with seat/seed blocks and response conditions; an acquisition-to-development-to-paid-rent trajectory; spending units including inherited improvements and censoring; actual versus accessible finance at elimination; selected message/exposure/recipient/action sequences with contradictory evidence. Put the response pipeline in the methods and use diagnostics to qualify affected economic findings. Avoid a figure program dominated by software defects.

Useful alternates are a two-window rent accounting exhibit and a claim-strength ladder contrasting facts, local feasibility and causal claims. Every plot needs a source manifest, units, checks and captions that state what is not identified. Static research assets should be reproducible from named inputs, not hand-drawn numerical summaries.

## Outline

Start with the capability/alignment question and why delayed competitive consequences matter. Establish what this implementation affords, the actual normative contract, and the cohort's design. Present outcome and trajectory profiles, planning/development/finance, and social conduct with counterexamples. Use interface findings at the point where they alter interpretation. Discuss evaluation validity, related work and the experiments required for larger claims. End with bounded conclusions and a research agenda for controlled safety and training studies. Do not imply that training or alignment interventions were implemented.

## Open objections I will press

1. Are proposed social claims actually about objective pursuit or instruction adherence? Name the instruction, opportunity, applied deviation and alternative explanations.
2. Does each proposed performance comparison condition on an endogenous opportunity or on survival? Retain the unconditional trajectory beside it.
3. Does any proposed counterfactual silently continue historical actions after changing the state? Local endpoints and full policy reruns answer different questions.
4. Does an exhibit conflate a deed's last acquisition route, a group's completion route, and inherited portfolio composition?
5. Is literature novelty narrower than the nearest competitor's actual methods, rather than narrower only than its abstract?
