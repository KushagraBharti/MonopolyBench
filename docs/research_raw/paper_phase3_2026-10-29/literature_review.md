## The argument to carry into the paper

MonopolyBench studies sustained economic capability and social conduct in the same competitive environment. The present contribution is an instrument, an audited cohort and a method for testing specific behavioral interpretations. Its strongest demonstrated method restores an actual decision state, executes specified legal alternatives and stops at a declared local endpoint. That can distinguish unavailable financing from overlooked financing, failed execution from a chosen hold, and plausible explanations from what the record establishes.

This is a narrower and more defensible argument than being the first economic sandbox, the first multiplayer benchmark or the first system with legal menus and replay. It also keeps the user's capability–alignment question central: the study explains what can be measured now and what a controlled test of alignment as capability increases would need. The current four-provider cohort does not measure that trend.

## Capability, alignment and the instruction contract

The relationship between capability and undesirable conduct depends on the task, norm and measurement. The Arena study reports a null relationship for its capability ordering; [Emergent Collusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24967v2) reports within-family differences under a protocol-violation opportunity. [MASK](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.03750v3) separates accuracy from honesty, while [Safetywashing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.21792v3) questions safety scores that mainly reflect capability. This motivates a controlled future study rather than a conclusion about our four models.

Compare three contracts explicitly: MonopolyBench permits public deception; AgentMonopoly encourages bluffing but prohibits public/action contradiction; the Arena communication study applies an external taxonomy rather than the same instruction. Keep an act's factual status, contractual status and external ethical assessment separate. [MACHIAVELLI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03279v4) is a precedent for measuring task reward and ethical conduct separately. The [normative-contract table](opus/exhibits/normative_contracts.md) supplies the expanded comparison.

## Bargaining, assurances and explanations

[NegotiationArena](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/bianchi24a.html) motivates explicit tactic comparisons. Our transaction following a pitch is not a measured persuasion effect. [Cheap Talk, Empty Promise](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04782v1) and [When Agents Lie](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05132v2) are workshop papers with explicit stated-action settings; free-form standing offers require additional conditions and expiry definitions.

[Empty Commitments v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.01045v2) is an empirical study with mocked tools, distinct from its earlier protocol-focused v1. [Who May Release an Agent's Promise?](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.21203v1) studies authority over commitments. These sharpen the interpretation of a feasible standing offer later withdrawn, but do not establish that our G18 case was an irrevocable promise or induced costly reliance.

[Turpin et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.04388v2) and [Lanham et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.13702v1) motivate caution about explanations as causal accounts. [Chain of Thought Monitorability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.11473v2) motivates oversight research, not treating our channel-conflict count as a validated monitoring score. We report the channels, their disagreements and the limits of attribution.

## Corrections carried into the shared outline

- AgentMonopoly v2's table totals **140 games**; the inspected repository README totals **120**. Neither is the earlier 160. Keep the source discrepancy visible.
- FinBoardBench already has an acquisition-to-conversion argument. The early phrase “longitudinal acquisition and finance” was too broad for the inspected summaries and has been narrowed.
- DECE already provides substantial legal-interface and replay infrastructure. Generic infrastructure firstness is removed.
- Arena has a full communication paper; it is not only a collection of website anecdotes.
- Empty Commitments **v2** supplies the current empirical comparison. Preserve v1 separately as historical source provenance, not evidence for v2's results.
- Use **Gemini (Argon)** at first mention, **Gemini** thereafter, and seats **1–4** in the shared manuscript. Preserve original names and numbering in archival peer records.
- Citation metadata and abstract inspection do not imply full methods verification. Where a stronger comparison depends on an uninspected section or release capability, the registry keeps that boundary explicit.

The resulting paper can overlap prior work and still contribute. Its claim is the demonstrated combination of a persistent economic environment, full-player consequences, auditable action provenance and carefully bounded legal-alternative analysis. Comparative superiority, a capability–alignment curve and successful training remain separate empirical questions.
