## Closest comparisons

- [Vending-Bench v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.15840v1), methods §§2–3 and abstract: sustained economic operation and failure traces motivate a capability-and-safety question. Its simulated business, supplier interaction and net-worth endpoint differ from player-to-player asset competition. Borrow the structure linking outcome profiles to concrete failures; do not borrow its horizon units or call our runs millions of tokens without accounting.
- [Vending-Bench 2](https://andonlabs.com/evals/vending-bench-2) and [Arena](https://andonlabs.com/evals/vending-bench-arena) are first-party evaluation pages, not interchangeable versions of the original paper. The former's year-long setup and adversarial suppliers extend the problem; the latter already includes competition, communication and transfers. Label mutable pages by access date and avoid comparing current leaderboard values to our frozen cohort.
- [Arena communication study v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.14825v3), §§2,4,5 and limitations: deterministic claim verification, reasoning-based labels and operational follow-through already exist in this close competitor. Its external communication taxonomy is not our instruction contract. The paper explicitly limits its human-reference validation. Our candidate addition is precise legal-sequence testing and per-attempt, delivered-context adjudication tied to longitudinal consequences—not the first state-grounded speech analysis.
- [AgentMonopoly v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.29330v2), §§3–4, limitations and A.1–A.4: legal-action validation, speech/action logging and explanation skepticism are explicit. Appendix A.3 totals 140 games. Peers independently found 120 in the repository README; preserve the discrepancy. Bluffing is encouraged while public/action contradiction is prohibited. The inspected repository also reports deterministic execution and wealth trajectories. Our debt-time financing analysis concerns different mechanics, not a missing analysis they necessarily should have performed.
- [FinBoardBench v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.27896v1), §§3–4 and figure captions: already links acquisition, financing, monetization and deployment; Monopoly trade evaluation distinguishes proposer and receiver. Its cross-game stage argument and Monopoly summary profiles overlap our framing. Fable correctly narrowed my early “longitudinal” wording: inspected sections report liquidity, control and return summaries, not our exact per-group post-trade windows. We should explain our accounting unit rather than claim to invent acquisition-to-use analysis.
- [DECE v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.35773v1), §3.2 and related method passages: legal interfaces, persisted states, replay/resume and complete model–harness configurations are already explicit. Peer-checked §4.3 adds matched harness comparisons. This is a direct instrument comparator. Our social analysis and historical-prefix legal-sequence audit are narrower additions; an unsupported feature checklist cannot establish superiority.
- [Cattle Trade v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14537v1), abstract and metadata personally checked; detailed methods attributed to peer reads: auction/bargaining integration, complete action logging, code policies and 242 games already support analysis beyond wins. Workshop status is explicit. Reference policies can calibrate some behaviors, but a policy without substantive trading cannot validate our trade-specific measurement.

## Norms, commitments and capability growth

[MACHIAVELLI v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03279v4) separates task reward and ethically assessed conduct. [MASK v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.03750v3) separates accuracy from honesty; [Safetywashing v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.21792v3) challenges safety measurements that mainly track capability. [Emergent Collusion v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24967v2) studies a protocol-violation opportunity and within-family differences. These purpose-level descriptions were verified in their primary abstracts. Together with the Arena study, they motivate an independently defined capability axis and norm. Their findings are design-dependent; our four different-provider systems do not estimate a capability-growth curve.

[NegotiationArena](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.05863v1) motivates explicit tactic comparisons rather than attributing a concession to the last utterance. [Cheap Talk](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04782v1) and [When Agents Lie](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05132v2) use stated-action protocols; both are workshop publications according to their primary metadata. Our free-form offers require additional conditions, expiry and exposure definitions before promise-breaking rates exist.

[Empty Commitments v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.01045v2) and [Who May Release an Agent's Promise? v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21203v1) distinguish feasibility from authority over a promise. I verified the former's empirical v2 abstract and introductory formalization, resolving the stale v1 “proposed protocol only” reading; deeper v2 methods were independently read by Sol. These are preprints. Neither establishes that G18's changed standing offer was a binding promise, and runtime-infeasible promises differ from later withdrawal of feasible terms.

## Positioning decision

There is substantial overlap and that is acceptable. The paper's value is an integrated environment, audited cohort and analytical protocol with consequential examples. The comparison should say what each study measures and what our evidence adds. “Our benchmark is better” requires an explicit criterion and a suitable test; a feature inventory is insufficient. Broad firstness, blanket competitor absences and claims of superior realism are removed.

The strongest proposed differentiator is the demonstrated protocol: restore an exact historical decision, execute specified legal alternatives through the authoritative engine, stop at a local endpoint, and connect that diagnosis to the recorded economic/social trajectory. This is a contribution with measured scope, not an exclusive-infrastructure claim. Public artifact release is a completion gate in steps 17–18, not an accomplishment to infer from files existing locally.
