# Astra's initial full-scope position

This position precedes the new coauthor proposals. It is a starting argument to challenge, not the final outline.

The paper should ask how sustained economic capability and strategic social conduct can be evaluated together when agents change one another's future opportunities. The contribution should combine a replayable competitive environment, a source-linked longitudinal study, and evidence about what evaluations can and cannot attribute to an agent. Safety and alignment motivate the study, but the present permissive game instruction does not supply a clean alignment score.

My proposed argument begins with ordinary competent actions accumulating into long trajectories. Acquisition, liquidity, development, negotiation, memory and execution jointly matter. Then show that a terminal win or a plausible explanation cannot distinguish those mechanisms. The environment lets us test narrower claims against actual menus, settlements and legal action sequences. Finish with the implications for designing controlled long-horizon evaluations and future training environments, with implemented and proposed capabilities labeled separately.

## Experiment decision

The descriptive paper does not require a blanket new tournament. Its strongest claims use current complete games, independent event accounting and exact-prefix local checks. Any claim that communication caused concessions, corrected recovery changes winning, agents become less aligned with capability, or house retention caused victory needs a separate design. The outline should make those optional claims easy to remove rather than imply the existing cohort tests them.

The closest practical weakness is construct validity, not raw game count. We should retain the hand-specified accounting tests, menu-grounded financing sequences, channel counterexamples and recovery-allocation checks. A few baseline games without meaningful trade cannot validate every social or trade measurement. Reference policies and future interventions can be described as extensions with exact purposes.

## Proposed exhibit sequence

1. Environment and evidence path: choices, public communication, economic state, attempts and canonical events. Distinguish model information from analyst-only records.
2. Seed-by-seat winner grid paired with cohort design, not a general leaderboard.
3. Opportunity creation, bilateral completion and management access. Show absolute counts beside conditional rates and mark defaults/channel conflicts where attribution matters.
4. Longitudinal construction, receipts and inheritance, with zero-rent units and censoring visible rather than a misleading ROI.
5. Financing and finite-house examples: legal payment, inaccessible nominal equity, and earlier management workaround.
6. Social chronology: G13 omission and pre-existing buyer rationale, paired with explicit premise correction or transient echo. Show exposure separately from reliance.
7. Compact response-processing/measurement validation exhibit that explains discarded plans and history discrepancies.

Tables should cover rules/configuration, definitions and units, primary-source comparison, and claim-to-experiment boundaries. Every chart must come from the checked records rather than numbers transcribed into drawing code.

## Initial literature checks, 2026-10-29

- Vending-Bench v1, https://arxiv.org/html/2502.15840v1, sections2–3. Economic tasks composed over long runs and trace-based explanations are useful structural inspiration. Its original score is net worth and its environment includes simulated supplier communication. We should not claim identical realism, horizon length or failure mechanisms.
- Vending-Bench2 and Arena, https://andonlabs.com/evals/vending-bench-2 and https://andonlabs.com/evals/vending-bench-arena. The author's current pages add competition, trading and collaboration. These are first-party eval pages, distinct from the 2025 paper. Multi-agent economics alone is not our differentiation.
- AgentMonopoly v2, https://arxiv.org/html/2610.29330v2, sections3–4. Its behavioral/persona comparisons already connect language, actions and outcomes; generated explanations are explicitly not direct intent. Our comparison must center exact financing, attempt resolution, temporal exposure and consequences, not claim those broad ideas are new.
- FinBoardBench v1, https://arxiv.org/html/2605.27896v1, sections2–4. It evaluates Cashflow, Acquire and Monopoly, with repeated active decisions and financial consequences. A simple acquire-then-develop narrative overlaps; exact legal sequencing and applied-versus-explained distinctions may be stronger additions.
