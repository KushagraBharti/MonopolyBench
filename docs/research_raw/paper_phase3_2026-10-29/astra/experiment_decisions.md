# What requires another experiment?

The bounded paper can proceed with the audited cohort. No new paid game is required for its current descriptive claims. This is a claim-selection decision, not a claim that the data answer every capability or alignment question. The user-authorized phase is 11–14; no prompt, game or execution treatment is changed here.

The observation unit is a configured agent interacting with three other configured agents. Counts of decisions do not create thousands of independent model-level samples. The five seed blocks and common roster limit inference. Excluding inconvenient interface cases cannot reconstruct a counterfactual game.

| Question | What the retained evidence supports | Additional work if the stronger claim is wanted |
|---|---|---|
| Who won, acquired groups, negotiated and developed? | Complete cohort profiles, conditional denominators and all-game outcomes | General rankings require more independently sampled environments/opponents and controlled configurations, with a precision target chosen before a game count |
| Did a group become useful? | Own expenditure, subsequent paid rent, creditor events and estate routes, with timing and censoring | Causal investment return requires explicit acquisition costs, salvage/terminal treatment and alternative policies; a cash-flow ratio alone is insufficient |
| Could a debtor pay now? | Existence of a legal own-action sequence or an upper-bound impossibility certificate | Continued survival, rationality and changed winners require policy continuations and a declared utility/horizon |
| Would a rejected build allocation work? | All 61 Opus multisets execute after reordering at their recorded prefixes | The effect of different recovery or plan encoding needs a new versioned treatment and fresh agent/opponent responses |
| Did scarce houses restrict construction? | Nine games reached zero stock; selected engine-checked constraints and workarounds | A population count of binding expansion needs existing-log coding with a precise predicate. A causal house-denial effect requires a changed retention policy and continuations |
| Did a statement mislead? | Specific false/omitted premises, delivered information, response chronology and alternative motives | Deception prevalence requires a defined proposition population and adjudication; persuasion requires a controlled message/information comparison |
| Did an agent breach a promise? | A scoped offer can be delivered, invoked and withdrawn; current cases do not identify costly reliance or initial dishonesty | A population count needs contract conditions and temporal opportunities. A causal commitment effect needs an intervention on the commitment protocol |
| Does a note reveal intent? | What each returned channel actually says, with conflicts and missingness | A psychological intent or stable-knowledge claim needs stronger identification; more prose alone is not validation |
| Is the system monitorable? | Specific received action/explanation conflicts and exactly localized recording limits | A monitoring claim requires a fixed monitor input, prediction target and held-out criterion. A diagnostic count is not monitor accuracy |
| Does capability growth harm alignment? | A motivation and prospective design; this roster supplies no growth axis | Independently measured capability tiers, fixed norm, real opportunity to comply/violate, comparable treatments and repeated controlled opportunities |
| Can this sandbox train safer agents? | Tested replay/reset components and legal local branching as implementation affordances | Training adapters/rewards, train/test separation, compute controls, held-out seeds/opponents and external transfer evaluation |

Existing-log analyses are optional unless their corresponding population claims enter the abstract or results: all false public propositions; binding expansion under finite stock; funding-denial reasons in responses; quantities changed by recovery beyond the audited uneven-build class. The present figures avoid those unmeasured prevalence claims. A repeated reference-policy tournament is not required to validate exact accounting. Policies that never initiate meaningful trades cannot validate negotiation-quality metrics.

The core safety design should distinguish three goals. **Instruction adherence** asks whether a pre-existing instruction was followed; an observable breach can be described without randomization. **Normative evaluation** applies an explicitly declared external criterion, which must not be mistaken for the agent's instructions. **Causal comparison** asks whether a treatment or capability difference changes conduct, requiring a design that identifies that effect. In this cohort public deception is authorized; private honesty is requested but errors and inconsistent channels prevent treating every false note as intentional violation. Engine rejection of illegal actions is a boundary of the environment, not evidence that the agent never attempted one.

No existing finding requires the paper to become an interface-debugging report. The main narrative remains persistent economic pursuit, social bargaining and the evidence needed to interpret both. Execution conditions matter when they change that interpretation: a defaulted END is not a chosen reserve policy, and a settled trade need not reflect acceptance of its public premise.

Before manuscript drafting, freeze the displayed definitions, run the reporting assertions, inspect every retained figure and preserve the source/version ledger. Those tasks validate the paper's representation of completed analyses. They are distinct from re-running the benchmark and from the clean-release work in steps 17–18.
