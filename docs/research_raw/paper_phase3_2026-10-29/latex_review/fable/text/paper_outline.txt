        Workingtitleandcentralargument
        Preferred workingtitle: MonopolyBench: Studying Long-Horizon Agent Capability and Social Conduct in a
        Competitive Economy.
        Alternative emphasizing the research program: MonopolyBench: A Replayable Sandbox for Studying Agent
        Capability and Alignment. Use this only if the abstract immediately distinguishes the current descriptive
        results from future alignment experiments, and the release package is complete.
        Thepaperaskshowagentssustaingoal-directedeconomicbehaviorandhowtheyconductthemselves
        towardotheragentsoverthesamelongtrajectory. MonopolyBenchprovidespersistentresources,com-
        peting interests, consequential negotiation, constrained financing and inspectable state. The current
        cohort supports economic profiles and source-grounded social explanations. Exact historical-prefix tests
        exposewhennominalassetsareinaccessible,anapparentlyskippedinvestmentwasadiscardedplan,or
        ageneratedbehaviorallabelwaswrong. Thesefindingsinformhowtodesignandauditlong-horizon
        safety sandboxes. They do not establish how alignment changes with capability growth.
        Keepthreeresearchquestionsthroughout:
         1. Capability: Howdotheevaluatedsystemsacquire,negotiate,finance,develop,liquidateandpreserve
          economicpositionacrosscompletegames?
         2. Conduct: Whatdotheyclaim,conceal,promise,coordinateandrevise,relative to their actual instruc-
          tions and counterparties’ delivered information?
         3. Evaluationdesign: Whichrecordsandactionableopportunitiesallowthesebehaviorstobeinterpreted
          reliably, and what additional experimental design is needed for capability–alignment comparisons
          andtraining?
        Contributions should be concrete: the environment and replayable research records; bilateral and longi-
        tudinal analysis of a complete cohort; exact-prefix legal-alternative auditing with demonstrated scope;
        andnorm-explicit social chronologies that preserve conflicting channels and counterexamples. Shared
        infrastructure is acknowledged rather than claimed as first. Release status must be stated honestly until
        steps 17–18 finish.
        Abstract plan
        Openwiththejointcapability/conductquestion. Introduce the persistent four-player economy and the
        20-game, five-seed, four-rotation cohort. Describe the analysis method in one sentence. Include one
        economic result and one social contrast, plus the result that applied non-building can arise through
        different execution paths. End with the supported evaluation-design implication and the boundary of the
        current study.
        Candidateeconomicnumbersarethe60-eliminationfinancingsplitortheeight-casedevelopmentresidual;

        becomemisalignedastheyimprove. Finaltitleandabstractarewrittenafterthemanuscript,instep15.

            1   Introduction: capability and conduct with delayed consequences
            Develop the motivation from Vending-Bench’s economic autonomy question and the user’s broader
            capability/alignment ambition. Show why competing agents create endogenous opportunities: an offer
            changes another player’s assets and financing, a cash payment can fund a rival’s development, and a debt
            cantransfer a portfolio. The environment’s value is controllable complexity and inspectability, not a claim
            to reproduce the whole economy.
            Introduce the inference problem with two compact contrasts. An applied END can represent a reserve
            preference, a rejected build followed by default, or a received action that disagrees with its accompanying
            text. A settled deal can coexist with explicit rejection of its sales pitch. These examples connect economic
            andsocial evaluation from the beginning.
            Place the work alongside AgentMonopoly, FinBoardBench, Vending-Bench Arena and its communication
            study, Cattle Trade and DECE. Capability/alignment evidence from Arena, MASK, Safetywashing and
            EmergentCollusionmotivatesadesignedfuturecomparison;itisnotoneuniformtrendthatourwin
            counts can extend. End with the three RQs and the specific contributions.
            Evidence/exhibit: F1 evidence chain, the residual audit and G13 chronology. Alternative: game familiar-
            ity, declared variants and wrapper conditions may explain behavior. Transition: specify the environment,
            instruction contract and evaluated configurations before interpreting any result.
            2   Environment,instructions and evaluated agents
            2.1  Economyandrulevariant
            Explainpurchases,auctions,trades,completecolorgroups,evenbuilding,finitehouses/hotels,mortgages,
            jail management and bankruptcy in student-readable terms. Describe the actual variant: developed
            property can pass intact to creditors; debt-time sales can unlock mortgages; a hotel downgrade requires
            bankhouses. Separate mortgage interest on a traded deed from the creditor-inheritance rule. Avoid an
            unqualified claim of classic or standard Monopoly.
            Acompactrule-and-implicationtableshouldconnecteachrelevantmechanictotheanalysisitenables.
            For example, selling buildings before mortgaging makes finance a legal sequence; inherited development
            complicates attribution of rent to own construction; jail limits access to management. State rules from the
            frozen engine and saved configuration rather than recollection of tabletop play.
            2.2  Informationandnormativecontract

            Include the three-environment normative-contract table developed by Opus: our contract, Agent-
            Monopoly’s blufÏng-plus-say/do constraint, and Arena’s externally coded communication taxonomy.
            Keepcontractual permission distinct from an external normative judgment. An explicitly declared norm
            cansupportdescriptive adherence without randomization; causal comparison needs more design.

            3   Studydesignandanalysisprotocol
            3.1  Frozencohortanddependence
            Report 20 complete games, 80 player-games, five seeds with four rotations, 50,918 events, 6,989 decisions

            alongside conditional responsiveness because prior play creates access and funding.

        6  Whatthesandboxmakesmeasurable—andwhatremainsuntested
        Combineinstrument validation and the alignment agenda in one substantial section. Summarize the
        response-policy mechanics, the 2,054 initial prompts carrying at least one trade-history defect manifesta-
        tion, and timeline omissions. State their demonstrated consequences and scope without attributing every
        later decision to them. Explain why original-record and engine checks also corrected the researchers’
        labels.
        Useathree-layer affordance ledger: implemented environment features; analyses actually performed;
        unimplemented experimental/training interfaces. Exact reset and local branching are implemented
        and tested at stated prefixes; full-prompt equality beyond memory, provider sufÏxes, general rollout
        termination, RL rewards/training and real-world transfer are not thereby demonstrated. One reference-
        policyrolloutrequiredtruncation,sobankruptcyterminationisanobservedpropertyofthiscohortrather
        thanauniversalguarantee.
        Specify a prospective capability–alignment study: fixed norms with genuine opportunities to comply
        or violate; an independently measured capability axis or within-lineage tiers; controlled prompts, mem-
        ory, budgets and response handling; matched situations and repeated policy continuations; held-out
        assessment of utility and norm adherence. Examples include truthful financial disclosure, protected
        information, transaction authorization and commitments with a defined release authority. No scalar
        shouldsilently trade all violations against profit.
        Explain what new experiments would answer and keep them outside current results. The current corpus
        can motivate and test the measurement machinery; it cannot supply a capability-growth trend or training
        benefit. See experiment decisions.
