The vertical axis counts paid rent at developed rates from that unit's first own expenditure onward; it excludes $450 received earlier on an inherited developed group. Total outlay is $103,450 and these receipts total $92,550. Whole-game developed receipts are $93,000. Diamonds identify inherited deeds at first own expenditure, not homogeneous estate-only portfolios. ## F6 — Current-debt finance at all eliminations

[Figure](exhibits/f06_elimination_financing.png), [60-row table](exhibits/t06_eliminations.csv). Each point is one applied elimination, including four automatic eliminations after mortgages. Horizontal position is cash accessible through the acting debtor's own legal liquidation sequence at that debt, without assuming help from another player. Vertical position is debt owed. In 56 cases even a generous full-access upper bound is insufficient. Three have engine-verified payment sequences; their mechanisms differ, including a contradictory received declaration in G12. In G7 the horizontal segment separates $440 accessible from $1,310 nominal under a $750 debt because the hotel-sale rule binds. The greedy legal route reaches the full upper bound in 59 cases; maximality in the remaining case follows from exhausting both permitted mortgage orders and the empty sale menu. No claim is made about rational surrender, future survival, an alternative winner or the effect of earlier house retention. Source: Opus's all-elimination reconstruction with Astra/Sol exact-prefix checks and Fable's independent bounds.

F6 encoding note: circles are the 56 declared eliminations; squares are the four automatic eliminations after mortgages. All 60 observations are retained, including overlapping values. Highlight labels use leader lines without opaque boxes; the $748/$500 Sol point remains visible.

## F7 — A legal build after retry need not preserve the proposed allocation

[Figure](exhibits/f07_build_recovery.png), [table](exhibits/t07_build_recovery.csv). Population: decisions with an uneven-intermediate-building rejection, not all build opportunities. A successful recovery means some legal construction was applied within the same decision. It preserves the original purchase multiset in 20/25 Sol, 6/18 Gemini and 5/5 Grok recoveries; the others change it. All 61 Opus defaulted plans can execute after interleaving identical purchases, but its refusal/recovery condition leaves no corrective attempt after the invalid action-only retry. One chain has the reversed order of invalid attempt then refusal. These are configured-system mechanics; the chart neither imputes repaired outcomes nor proves a causal loss of wins. Eventual same-turn target attainment is separate from within-decision preservation. Source: Sol's exact-plan and full-event replay audit, independently challenged by all peers.
