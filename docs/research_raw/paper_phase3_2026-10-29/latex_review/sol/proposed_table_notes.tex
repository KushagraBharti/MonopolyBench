% T2: do not mix percentage and ratio units.
\paragraph{T2 units.}
Own-turn propensity is proposing turns divided by eligible own turns;
eligible-decision rate is proposals divided by eligible decisions. If shown
as percentages, multiply these two columns by 100 and mark their headers
with \%. Proposals per proposing turn and eligible decisions per turn are
ratios, not percentages. Counts are exact; any displayed rounding applies
only to rates and ratios. State a single rounding rule for values computed
from the integer numerator and denominator. In particular, 18/16 is exactly
1.125: half-even rounding to two decimals gives 1.12; half-up gives 1.13.
The CSV/JSON retain their full recorded precision.
Preserve the exact source \texttt{response\_condition} strings in row-linked
native notes: Sol and Gemini, ``standard corrective-retry policy''; Opus,
``refusal recovery to action-only requests; retry budget affected''; Grok,
``required tool choice; standard corrective-retry policy''.

% T5: game currency, not provider costs; preserve both rent windows.
\paragraph{T5 units and flags.}
Each row is one game/player/color group with any own construction expenditure.
All monetary entries are in game dollars. Outlay and sale proceeds are gross
flows. Paid developed rent is shown both for the whole game and after first
own expenditure. The first-own-spend event is an event sequence number.
Winner and inherited-deed fields are Boolean flags; the creditor field counts
eliminations on that group. These amounts do not measure net or incremental ROI.

% T6: explain raw ``avoidable'' without implying future survival.
\paragraph{T6 units and scope.}
Owed, cash and all three finance bounds are in game dollars. Bank houses is
a count. Menu-mortgage-only cash includes current cash plus the mortgage
values of deeds mortgageable on the initial menu, without building sales.
Accessible cash follows the legal own-action liquidation sequence, including
building sales and mortgages of newly unlocked deeds. A hotel downgrade
requires at least four houses in the bank. The generous full-access upper
bound is current cash plus half the building cost per level and all
unmortgaged mortgage values, ignoring the bank-house constraint. This is the
retained analysis's definition of the source \texttt{full} field, rather than
a new financial estimand. As F6 states, the greedy legal route reaches that
bound in 59 cases. In the remaining case, maximality follows from exhausting
both permitted mortgage orders and the empty sale menu. The four
automatic-elimination rows use the triggering decision state before its
applied action.

Display the source \texttt{avoidable} flag under a header such as ``Own legal
sequence pays current debt''. A true value means that an engine-verified
own-action sequence can pay that debt. It does not predict later survival or
a changed winner. Preserve all 60 rows' ordered liquidation steps, applied
actions and elimination event sequence numbers in a companion native table.
The paired CSV/JSON remain the exact machine-readable source.

% T8: no reconstruction/aggregation hidden by compact display.
\paragraph{T8 ordering and units.}
These are the 631 retained event-level cash records for G14, G15 and G19,
in their source order. Event sequence numbers and game turns are distinct
indices. Cash is the balance after the event, in game dollars, not a cash
change, net worth or liquidatable wealth. The figure's initial balances come
separately from saved turn-zero states and are not rows of this event table.

% T9: source None must not be conflated with zero.
\paragraph{T9 missing fields and provenance.}
A dash denotes an absent selected-content name or selected-attempt index;
0 denotes attempt index zero. Categories classify the received records,
not intention or a proven lost build. A shortened decision ID is unique only
with its game key. Preserve the full ID, source path and source line in an
adjacent native provenance table, together with all attempt outcomes in
their source order. The paired CSV/JSON remain the exact machine-readable
source; a pointer alone does not fully represent these omitted fields.

% Opus cost table: the currency differs from game-money tables.
\paragraph{Provider cost and token units.}
Cost entries are known OpenRouter-reported USD, not game dollars. Opus's cost
is a lower bound because refused attempts lack cost records. Missing cost
counts and token accounting semantics remain visible. Completion and reasoning
tokens have provider-specific semantics and must not be silently summed into
a cross-provider total. Label latency in wall-clock seconds per decision;
it is observational metadata. Display the source's Argon participant as
``Gemini (Argon)'' to match the other exhibits.
