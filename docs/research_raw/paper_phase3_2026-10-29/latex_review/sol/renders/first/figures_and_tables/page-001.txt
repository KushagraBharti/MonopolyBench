This document collects the research exhibits for the venue-neutral paper outline. It is an exhibit package,
not the full manuscript. Figures retain their source identifiers F1–F9. Tables T1–T9 reproduce all 837
source records as native LaTeX tables; longer tables have repeated headers. Additional coauthor exhibits
follow separately. Exact data and source hashes accompany the editable sources.

Money in economic tables is game dollars. Provider spending is explicitly labelled USD. Rounded ratios
use half-even rounding to match the existing figures; exact counts and fractions remain visible. Gemini
denotes the Gemini 4 Argon participant. Seats and shared rotations are numbered 1–4.
