6  What the sandbox makes measurable—and what remains untested


Combine instrument validation and the alignment agenda in one substantial section. Summarize the
response-policy mechanics, the 2,054 initial prompts carrying at least one trade-history defect manifesta-
tion, and timeline omissions. State their demonstrated consequences and scope without attributing every
later decision to them. Explain why original-record and engine checks also corrected the researchers’
labels.

Use a three-layer affordance ledger: implemented environment features; analyses actually performed;
unimplemented experimental/training interfaces. Exact reset and local branching are implemented
and tested at stated prefixes; full-prompt equality beyond memory, provider suffixes, general rollout
termination, RL rewards/training and real-world transfer are not thereby demonstrated. One reference-
policy rollout required truncation, so bankruptcy termination is an observed property of this cohort rather
than a universal guarantee.

Specify a prospective capability–alignment study: fixed norms with genuine opportunities to comply
or violate; an independently measured capability axis or within-lineage tiers; controlled prompts, mem-
ory, budgets and response handling; matched situations and repeated policy continuations; held-out
assessment of utility and norm adherence. Examples include truthful financial disclosure, protected
information, transaction authorization and commitments with a defined release authority. No scalar
should silently trade all violations against profit.

Explain what new experiments would answer and keep them outside current results. The current corpus
can motivate and test the measurement machinery; it cannot supply a capability-growth trend or training
benefit. See experiment decisions.


7  Related work and limitations


Use the literature review throughout the introduction/methods and collect a concise comparison here if
the manuscript reads better that way. A compact table should compare environment, norm, economic
mechanics, observation, evaluation unit and verification method. “Not reported in inspected sections” is
not a negative feature assertion. Keep version and repository discrepancies visible in the supplement.

Limitations: one roster and five blocks; declared game variants and possible prior Monopoly familiarity;
endogenous opportunity and survival; unequal response/channel conditions; no human gold-standard
semantic annotation; exploratory definitions; dependence among groups/decisions; selected case studies
without population deception rates; no direct real-world safety transfer, human baseline or RL result.
Discuss these limits alongside what the environment genuinely allows a researcher to check.


8  Conclusion


Answer the three RQs at the demonstrated level: full economic paths reveal different acquisition, financing
and deployment patterns; social chronologies connect bargaining to consequences without collapsing ex-
planation into intent; precise records and legal-sequence tests materially change interpretations. End with
a concrete research direction for controlled capability/alignment work, not a claim that the relationship
has already been measured.
