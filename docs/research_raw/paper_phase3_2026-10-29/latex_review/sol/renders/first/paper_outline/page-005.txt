2.4  Artifact and replay contract

Explain snapshots, events, legal decisions, applied actions and per-attempt prompt/response records.
Applied actions remain authoritative. Distinguish full event replay, memory reconstruction, exact state
reset, local legal-alternative tests and fresh policy continuation. The system’s existing reset/recovery

result is claimed.

Evidence/exhibits: rule table; condition/channel table; Opus affordance ledger. Citations: Agent-

3  Study design and analysis protocol


3.1  Frozen cohort and dependence

Report 20 complete games, 80 player-games, five seeds with four rotations, 50,918 events, 6,989 decisions
and 8,679 attempts. Each participant occupies each seat five times. Show all outcomes in F2. Shared raw
dice do not give identical opportunities because the uses and recipients of draws diverge.

Two treatment fingerprints coincide with games 1–4 versus 5–20. The inspected commit diff concerns
concurrency plumbing, UI, documentation and tests, with no engine, prompt, resolver or model-client
file change. Neither a behavioral effect nor its absence follows from the source diff. The refusal-share shift
at that boundary is a coincidence in the data, not an identified cause.

Avoid independent-decision significance tests and chance-model p-values that ignore block structure.
Descriptive counts and a transparent outcome grid are more defensible than a universal ranking from
five blocks. Report horizon as game turns, decisions, attempts and available token accounting; METR’s
human-time horizon is a different construct.


3.2  Economic units and time windows

Define deed acquisitions separately from group completions; distinguish final acquisition route from
the routes of constituent deeds. Specify trade author, original initiator and responder. Define first
actual management turn, entry buildability, later buildability and elapsed delay. Keep whole trajectories
alongside conditional responsiveness because prior play creates access and funding.
