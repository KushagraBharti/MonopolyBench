3.3  Social evidence and review

For each proposition or assurance record: subject/time, factual status, speaker observation, available
channels, prior and current recipient exposure, applied action, later consequence and alternatives. A
logged utterance is not necessarily delivered; a past exposure can matter even after it leaves the current
window. Keep intent unresolved where error, conflict or missing evidence remains. Do not turn a fluent
summary into privileged ground truth about the agent.

Describe full first-pass extraction, overlapping 614 catalog items including 21 historical entries, all 20
trajectories and the fixed 240-decision original-bundle sample. Preserve Sonnet/Haiku attribution for the
public pass. Four model coauthors are not human annotators, and iterative debate is not independent
blind replication of every assertion. Definitions developed during exploration are not preregistered. The
sample frame limits prevalence inference.


3.4  Exact-prefix legal-alternative audits

Restore a saved decision prefix, verify the historical event match, execute a specified legal sequence for
the acting player, and stop at a defined local endpoint. Report whose action opportunity is used and any
altered condition. A legal existence proof is not an optimal policy, a predicted opponent response or an
alternative winner. Historical suffixes cannot simply be replayed unchanged after a divergent state.

Show validation of both model claims and our own extracted labels: reordered builds, accessible financing,
the genuine hotel lock and a fabricated redemption restriction refuted by the engine. Separate structural
integrity, accounting correctness, semantic validity and causal identification.

Evidence/exhibits: method diagram, definitions table, phase-2 source/hash ledger. Citations: Agent-
Board, HAL, Arena communication study, MACHIAVELLI, SOTOPIA, explanation-faithfulness literature.
Transition: follow the economic life cycle before interpreting social tactics within it.


4  Economic behavior across complete games


4.1  Acquisition, bargaining and winning routes

Begin with deed routes: 475 direct, 72 auction, 207 trade and 397 inherited acquisitions. Then distinguish
209 complete-group episodes, including 69 terminal inherited completions with no future opportunity.
Do not mix these denominators. G14 Sol and G15 Gemini demonstrate actual wins without accepted
trades.

F3 reports proposing-turn propensity and proposals per proposing turn, with absolute counts and
eligible-decision rates in its table. Gemini’s low initiation coexists with recipient bargaining. Across 113
settlements, group completion benefits neither/one/both sides in 60/43/10 cases. In 44 the final terms are
authored by the other party than the thread initiator. These are consequences and roles, not a complete
welfare score.
