4.2  Development and execution

F4 shows 43/63 trade-completed groups developed in the first actual management turn and 34/42 among
entry-buildable groups.  Its visible residual is essential: three defaults, three received END/content
conflicts and two consistent ENDs with stated reserve rationales. These are record classifications, not
three causally lost builds or a repaired willingness rate. The all-63 resolution table distinguishes never-legal
windows as well.

Use F7 or a compact table to explain all 61 Opus defaulted multisets that are executable after interleaving.
Successful retry may change the purchase multiset (within-decision allocation)1: 12/18 Gemini and 5/25
Sol recoveries do; 0/5 Grok do. Preserve valid chosen holds as counterexamples. Access choices, defaulted
actions and stated restraint all belong in capability analysis, but they must not be assigned the same
mechanism.


4.3  Investment, income and inheritance

F5 follows 103 spending units: $103,450 own outlay, $93,000 whole-game paid developed rent and $92,550
after first own spend. Forty units collect zero developed rent; 26 also cause no creditor elimination.
Explain exposure, salvage, deterrence, unpaid debts and inherited development before interpreting this
pattern. Do not label the ratio ROI.

Use Sol’s G19 successive-owner exhibit as the concrete trajectory; F9 cash paths provides trade-free
contrasts. Of 58 winner spending groups, 34 contain an inherited deed at first own expenditure, but only
30 first complete through an estate. Eventual winners are creditors in 54/60 eliminations, including 35/40
nonterminal ones. These characterize concentration under the declared variant, not a causal inheritance
advantage.


4.4  Financing, liquidation and finite stock

F6 shows all 60 eliminations: 56 below even a generous upper bound; three with a legal current-debt
payment sequence; one hotel lock with $440 accessible against $750 owed despite $1,310 nominal equity.
G12’s received declaration contradicts its accompanying sales recommendation, so the three payable debts
do not share one reasoning mechanism. Include successful staged liquidation as a counterexample.

Fable’s stock grid records nine games reaching zero houses and reconciles all 6,989 delivered bank states.
Zero stock is not proof of thwarted demand. Distinguish direct build restrictions, costly reallocation,
earlier management workarounds and the later G7 debt phase. Local finance statements never become
claims about a changed winner.
