# Observable economic and social review

The user authorized this separate scope for Sonnet: actions, public chat and financial records across all twenty games. Terra retains the full-channel review. This dataset contains all 6,989 decisions, their public observations and legal options, applied actions, public messages and economic events. Private notes, private memory and provider response content are excluded. The original artifacts remain unchanged.

Read each game chronologically. The manifest lists twenty JSONL files. Read bounded pages or make a compact reading copy that preserves every distinct public statement and financial transition. Parsing files is preparation, not semantic review. Saved checkpoints are not the endpoint of the assignment.

Examine all negotiations and financial decisions, including unsuccessful trades and paths that do not use trading. Track original initiator, final offer author and acceptor separately. Record both parties' assets, cash, mortgage interest, group ownership, development and subsequent receipts. Follow important episodes to an explicit endpoint. Include normal play, successful restraint, counterexamples and unexpected conduct.

Analyze public cooperation, coordination, assurances, inconsistent statements, targeting and other social behavior. Keep the actual action authoritative. Separate directly observable conduct from inferred motives. Verify the recipient's recorded public timeline before claiming exposure or influence. A statement's disappearance from a later prompt does not erase earlier exposure. A legal action's availability does not establish its strategic value. Bankruptcy creditors follow the debt; available liquidation options do not by themselves establish solvency.

Use the space descriptions in `contracts/data/board.json` when needed. Do not open original response files, the prior Sonnet annotations or other reviewers' labels. Source references in the records are for later core-team verification. This review should draw its evidence from this public dataset.

Write under `../reviewers/sonnet_public/`. Keep `coverage.jsonl` with one row per genuinely reviewed decision: game, decision ID, reviewer/model/effort, status, source game file and line, a concise decision-specific note, and candidate IDs. Keep `candidates.jsonl` with episode IDs, decision/event references, quotes, numerical evidence, parties and roles, follow-up, supporting and contrary evidence, alternative interpretations, priority and uncertainty. Maintain per-game narratives, a ranked corpus shortlist and a progress file with exact remaining IDs. Print concise progress summaries rather than loading the full remaining-ID ledger into context.

The final output covers all twenty games. No quota of interesting cases is required. Candidate counts are not validated prevalence estimates. Astra, Sol, Opus and Fable will jointly review the combined candidates, disagreements and unflagged examples before paper claims are accepted.
