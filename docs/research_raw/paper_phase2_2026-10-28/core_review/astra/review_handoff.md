## What changed our interpretation

- Several apparent choices not to build are rejected affordable plans or contradictory received calls. All 61 Opus defaulted purchase plans execute after legal reordering. Genuine chosen holds also exist.
- Paying a debt often requires selling buildings before mortgaging the newly cleared group. Three of sixty applied eliminations admit a tested payment route; one other estate is inaccessible because of the hotel rule. Those four cases have different mechanisms.
- A trade, a completed set and a profitable investment are different outcomes. The accounting distinguishes own spending, paid rents, unpaid debts, sale proceeds and estates, with no-trade winners and zero-realized-rent groups retained.
- Misleading financial framing is observable. Successful persuasion is not established merely because a deal closes. Earlier recipient motives and explicit corrections materially narrow the strongest cases.
- Generated histories, response handling and generated research labels can all introduce errors. They are checked against the same canonical records rather than trusted as explanatory truth.
