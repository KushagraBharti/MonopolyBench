# Ready claims and evidence limits

This ledger closes Astra's phase-2 contribution. It incorporates the latest source-backed corrections from Sol, Opus and Fable. It does not certify every sentence of the earlier catalog or coauthor drafts. Initial judgments and later corrections remain separate.

Ready means the bounded descriptive statement below has evidence. It does not mean preregistered, causal, population-representative or human-annotated. The twenty games contain repeated players and five shared-seed blocks.

The refusal condition and timeline-delivery omissions are carried from independently checked phase-1 inventories. The full synthesis states their units. Opus's 1,605 refused attempts are not 1,605 first-attempt recoveries; the latter count is 1,604. No refusal reason establishes moral opposition to the game.

## Interpretations retained as unresolved

- An offer's price or framing caused a recipient's belief, concession or later action.
- A player deliberately sacrificed itself to select a winner, or formed a binding coalition.
- House retention was optimal, necessary for an elimination or decisive for victory.
- More retries would preserve later messages, memory or the actual winner.
- Received tool-name discrepancies originated in a specific provider or processing component.
- Four configured systems in this cohort establish increasing capability or changing alignment with capability.

These statements are not prerequisites for the bounded findings. A claim about causal performance, persuasion or corrected prompts would need its own design. No such experiment was run.

## Representative case set

The strongest economic exhibits are the G19 light-blue acquisition and later same-deed dark-blue contrast, G14/G15 trade-free wins, G7's hotel lock with its earlier management workaround, G20's actual $1,400 rescue, and the corpus-wide default-plan test. The strongest social exhibits are G13's omission with prior buyer motive, G4's public concession with private correction, G6/G19 correction with acceptance, G11's transient echo then rejection, and G18's exposed offer withdrawal.

These exhibits should carry their negative cases. G1's trade-to-development sequence can illustrate applied consequences only with the upstream rejection/acceptance mismatch disclosed; it should not serve as an unqualified persuasion success story.
