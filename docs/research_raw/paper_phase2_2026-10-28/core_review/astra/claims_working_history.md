# Astra's proposed claims and open evidence

These are working claims for the economic/social section. They are not the completed four-coauthor synthesis. Quantitative script checks and semantic adjudication remain different forms of evidence.

## Supported bounded statements

1. **Attempted deployment differs from applied deployment.** All 61 Opus defaulted build allocations can be executed at the recorded prefixes by interleaving their operations. Seven of the eight attempted plans behind four Sol defaults are unaffordable; one initial plan was feasible before its correction changed the allocation. Unit: attempted allocation within a defaulted decision, not independent games. Evidence: `check_default_build_plans.py`. This diagnoses immediate feasibility and recovery mechanics, not performance under a repaired policy.
2. **Legal financing and physical inventory both limit access.** The G4 immediate-build and G14 post-redemption examples separate cash, collateral and bank supply. The G7 final liquidation provides an exact hotel-lock case. Evidence: `check_supply_constraint.py`, `check_g07_hotel_lock.py`. Artificial inventory changes in the former are predicate diagnostics, not legal historical branches or winner counterfactuals.
3. **Negotiation initiation has distinct frequency and intensity.** `check_negotiation_units.py` independently reproduces all proposal counts and both offered-decision and eligible-own-turn denominators from canonical records. Use any-proposal turn propensity and proposals per proposing turn in the main descriptive profile; retain menu frequency and menus per turn for granularity. Neither eligible turns nor received offers are policy-independent populations. Response ratios need received-offer context and cannot establish skill on matched offers.
4. **Recorded speech can mislead without identifiable persuasion.** G13 d252 omits available collateral despite a recorded explanation acknowledging it, and the buyer accepts a higher offer. Its insurance rationale already exists at d251. Evidence: `g13_review.md`, `check_g13_claims.py`. Supported: delivered omission, recorded rationale, settlement. Unsupported: identified belief change, causal $70 premium, or instruction misalignment.
5. **The decision record can expose an inconsistent model-facing history.** One formatter-role assumption appears as 44 accepted-transfer direction reversals, 26 rejected-offer author reversals and 232 self-referencing counteroffer partner labels. The latter preserve the actor and offer bundles. Their union appears in 2,054 of 6,989 initial recorded prompts. Evidence: `check_negotiation_units.py`; Opus independently reproduces counts. This is exposure, not proven reliance, confusion prevalence or a causal capability loss. G14 d140 explicitly reconstructs its Mediterranean sale correctly despite the history line, while retaining unrelated state errors.
6. **Some apparent model errors are extraction errors.** G13 includes correct house-financing arithmetic, board distance, conditional budgets and set-blocking language that the public extractor flagged as erroneous. Evidence: `g13_review.md`. These targeted corrections do not estimate a corpus-wide error rate. The common unflagged sample and further adjudication remain necessary.
7. **Trading and inheritance have distinct roles in winners' observed development.** Eighteen winners participate in an accepted trade; G14 Sol and G15 Gemini do not. Thirteen first winner-developed groups contain a deed whose latest acquisition was a trade. Of 58 winner groups with any own construction, 34 contain inherited deeds at first own construction. The winner receives 54 of 60 estates as creditor. Evidence: `check_development_aggregation.py`. These are trajectory descriptions, not necessary routes or causal effects of trading or inheritance. HOTEL_BUILT counts as construction even without an earlier own HOUSE_BUILT.
8. **Construction and realized receipts are different accounting measures.** Across 103 player–game–group spending units, construction costs $103,450; whole-game developed-property receipts total $93,000, including $450 before first own construction. Forty units collect no developed rent; 26 also trigger no elimination. Evidence: `development_aggregation_check.json`. Neither those units nor the $92,550 after-first-construction total identify investment failures or incremental return. Follow-up, inherited improvements, retained assets, salvage and within-game dependence remain separate.
9. **Scarcity occurs, but a bank count alone does not identify strategic denial.** Bank houses reach zero in nine games, with event-derived stock matching all 6,989 initial decision states. Evidence: `check_development_aggregation.py`. This does not count how many desired builds were blocked or show that a particular retention decision caused scarcity. Selected exact-prefix supply and hotel-lock checks establish narrower local constraints.

## Claims requiring further existing-data work

- Whole-trajectory rent, cash, financing and two-sided trade summaries must be independently joined before choosing representative cases. A rent schedule, a paid rent and an estate transfer are different quantities.
- The three financing-feasible applied declarations do not share one established mechanism and are not automatically deliberate kingmaking. G12 d249's content requests a legal sale and its applied note ultimately warns against bankruptcy, while the applied function declares it. An independently replayed five-house sale sequence pays the debt without mortgages (`check_g12_channel_liquidation.py`). G17 d234 instead omits mortgages unlocked after sales from its stated maximum. Recipient identity follows the debt; stated preference, alternatives, transfer timing and later use remain separate.
- Anti-leader appeals need chronological recipient exposure and competing prior motives. A matching later action is not sufficient evidence of influence.
- Channel inconsistency, unavailable notes and failed requests must remain attached to social cases. An absence of extracted deception is not an absence of deception in unobservable channels.
- Cross-model counts describe configured systems in this cohort. They do not identify a capability-growth trend or a causal relation between capability and alignment.

## Claim-dependent experiments

No new game is needed to report these bounded descriptive and instrument findings. Claims that a recovery policy changes performance, communication causes cooperation, or an omission changes a buyer's belief require a designed comparison. Do not present immediate legal-branch checks as those experiments.
