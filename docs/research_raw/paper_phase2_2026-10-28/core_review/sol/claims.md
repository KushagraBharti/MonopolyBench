## Proposed refinements to the phase-1 contract

1. Record proposition truth separately from omitted finance, implied necessity, forecasts and conditional threats. Preserve plausible alternative readings.
2. Record action authorship. Model-selected END, valid received END contrary to content, rejected plan and deterministic fallback are different processes.
3. Require actor, phase and next management access for every financing or construction alternative. Separate entry menu, reachable sequence and nominal upper bound.
4. Record the recipient's earlier rationale before claiming message influence. A later repeated motive may predate exposure.
5. Distinguish transient echo, final correction, explicit premise rejection and action in reliance. Never infer general immunity from one rejection.
6. Define a public assurance and its conditions before labeling withdrawal. A prior counter is not always a standing legal offer.
7. Keep channel availability and response condition in comparisons. An explanation-selected rate is not latent intent prevalence.
8. Preserve initial labels and corrections. Adjudication is exploratory research, not preregistration or independent human annotation.
