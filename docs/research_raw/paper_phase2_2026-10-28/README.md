Each extraction output keeps neutral chronology, proposed cases, contradictory evidence, economic consequences, exposure evidence, and separate completion statuses for actions, events, public text, private notes, provider text and attempts. A screen hit does not establish intent, irrationality, collusion or kingmaking. No fixed quota of interesting cases is imposed.

The four researchers retain overlapping responsibility for all interpretation and writing. Their independent initial judgments and later adjudication are separate from the extraction records.
