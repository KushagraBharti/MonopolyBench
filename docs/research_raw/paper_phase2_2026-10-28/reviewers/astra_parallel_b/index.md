Saved private notes and readable provider explanations are dataset evidence. Encrypted, opaque and unreturned content is unavailable. Applied actions and canonical events determine conduct. Explanations alone do not prove intent, successful persuasion, collusion or kingmaking. The candidates distinguish stated intentions, exposure, actual choices, channel disagreement, arithmetic errors, failed threats and inference. No source `semantic_review` values were accessed for labeling or verification.

Game 13 supplies a particularly clear contrast for the final review: Opus wins with a naturally completed orange monopoly despite 39 construction fallbacks, while an actual misleading bargaining omission appears at decisions 251–253. Other recorded channels also invent construction, mortgages and rejected offers that never occur. Its final Sol bankruptcy is financially unavoidable, unlike premature liquidations identified in other games. The per-game candidate lists supply the source references and limits for those comparisons.
