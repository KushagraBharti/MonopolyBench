# Sol's whole-paper cross-review

## Scientific judgment

The best integrated paper asks how agents state changing economic opportunities and what they subsequently do. It should lead with the whole economic setting and complete populations, then give the strongest repeated positive observation its due. Fourteen of sixteen debts requiring mortgages released by prior sales reach payment. Thirteen successful entry statements name the additional mortgages or an aggregate budget encompassing them, and the remaining one raises the possibility. Both failures omit that resource and declare immediately. Those facts connect prospective language to dependent execution under a known local goal. They are more informative than either an isolated impressive transcript or the overall 128/131 payment fraction.

Bargaining provides a complementary scientific account. The agents do more than accept or refuse: they counter, retain disputed deed bundles while changing prices, correct false premises, compare building budgets, and discuss who benefits if a rival survives or fails. These are positive observations of economic assessment and negotiation. The selected swap/cash contrast remains descriptive because terms and proposers differ. Neither an economically plausible refusal nor an eventual win establishes that the refusal was effective.

I recommend Astra's restrained narrative and concise overall architecture, with Sol's complete first-decision fields and explicit evidence distinctions, Opus's close reading of factual amounts and counteroffers, and Fable's separately identified cash asks and concrete additions to the case histories. This is a substantive integration, not a vote for one draft. My own Sol version loses on the completeness of its offer-form presentation and contains a mistaken finance example. Opus and Fable add useful science, but their strongest generalizations exceed the checks reported.

## Fresh checks and their implications

## Narrative, limitations and dissent

Use four main cases: the G20 payment, G15 refusal, G19 correction and G13 selective pitch. They show successful execution, rival-focused reasoning, positive factual correction and economically situated omission. Keep G2, G7, the G20 coordination appeal and G18 as complete appendix cases with short body references. G7's two-sided budget is especially useful as a positive strategic continuation and deserves a substantive body paragraph even if its longer transcript is in the appendix. Eight cases remain eight incidents, not eight independent findings.

Preserve brief construction and inheritance context from Astra/Sol. It explains why building cash, blockers and survival matter. Move asking-price medians, cash-selected histories and realized receipts to one supporting appendix rather than deleting the economy around the negotiations or treating every association as a contribution. Use the source-linked G13 buyer text from Fable and Opus, which makes uptake visible while avoiding a persuasion-effect claim. Preserve G18's earlier survival explanation and the later redemptions; they explain changed terms without an invented inheritance motive or original-insincerity claim.

I dissent from Fable's explicit claim that initial-menu sums calibrate difficulty, and from Opus's “Most debts were easy” heading. I also dissent from the external inline review's unexecuted 131/131 loop score, its suggestion that a current provider query could establish a historical refusal category, and its authorship inference. Neither review authorizes new evaluations or invented humans. The other external review is right that necessity, expressed anticipation, factual assessment, execution and effectiveness differ; those distinctions should organize the science rather than become a repeated defensive checklist.

I favor Sol/Astra's empirical whole-block interpretation. Opus's second-stage resampling draws rotations with replacement within a shared seed, although the design deliberately includes every rotation once. That is an additional hypothetical reweighting assumption. If retained, name it and avoid calling it the uncertainty of the same fixed-rotation design. Five blocks are too few to make any resampling variant a decisive headline.

One consolidated limitations section should cover the interacting roster, five seeds and cyclic opponent order; unequal provider paths and corrupted historical summaries; retrospective selection; model-assisted interpretation; and absent effectiveness and difficulty references. Local qualifications still belong next to a specific number, such as the tentative statement or the selected numerical check. The question map should preserve all eight questions plus A-C without treating memory, learning, hidden-belief calibration or general capability as measured negative results.

This handoff offers a defensible observational paper, not a claimed model ranking, review score, acceptance forecast, independent replication or account of hidden cognition. The remaining substantive limits require future study; they cannot be removed by stronger wording. The available records already support a clearer and more positive scientific account than a catalogue of failures.
