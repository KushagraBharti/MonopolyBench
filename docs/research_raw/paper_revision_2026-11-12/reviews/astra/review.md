# Astra: all four complete manuscripts

I read the four complete manuscripts and appendices, handoffs and evidence maps before reading the other November 12 reviews. My own version was frozen first. The comparison below is independent of those reviews. No manuscript should be adopted unchanged.

## Scientific judgment

The strongest common argument connects prospective economic assessment with executed local goals. The financial subset has a particularly clear endpoint: the initial mortgage menu is insufficient even after all building salvage, and payment requires collateral released by sales. The text precedes the changed menu; thirteen successful texts include the resource or a budget incorporating it and one raises it tentatively. The two failed sums omit it. This is substantive evidence of expressed anticipation followed by local execution, with the support supplied by refreshed menus stated plainly.

Bargaining asks a different question because agreement is not a measure of decision value. A complete response population, explicit rival concerns and economically situated incidents are useful findings. The swap contrast remains descriptive and composition-sensitive. It should not carry the title, and neither a weak proxy nor an imprecise estimate warrants a new claim that agents ignore the magnitude of a rival's opportunity.

The best paper is an observational account of financial planning and strategic interaction. It is not a validated planning benchmark, a general audit method, or a census of internal reasoning. A stronger narrative cannot remove the same-cohort selection and interface conditions, but it can explain the behavior much better than a catalogue of label corrections.

## Checks that matter for the integrated paper

I independently reread the original first resolved action for every one of the 188 debt episodes. The new Fable first-action count reproduces: 50 of 56 above-bound debts start with declaration; the other six start with four mortgages and two sales. Among 131 demonstrated-payable debts, three start with declaration, 73 with a mortgage and 55 with a sale. The one menu-restricted episode starts with a mortgage. None of those 188 first actions is an engine fallback. These are applied-action counts, not accuracy scores: continuing an unpayable episode is not necessarily an error, and a received call can disagree with its own explanatory text.

I reread all resolved responses for the narrow named-action screen and recovery-note accounting. The content syntax screen yields 1,068 Grok responses naming an action and 139 mismatches; no other system yields that syntax. This does not establish that other systems never disagree with their prose. Opus has 1,604 recovery decisions, none with a model-authored private note; sixty have a policy-generated note after fallback. Say “model-authored note” when describing the prompt's effect. Do not equate the 1,604 recovery decisions with the 1,394 delivered memory windows lacking notes.

The apparent Fable 14/15 accuracy rate has a more important problem than one incorrect yellow mortgage total. Its checker marks the two false maximum-liquidity claims as equal by comparing $408 and $530 with the incomplete initial subtotal. The full bounds are $748 and $905. The arithmetic of the subtotal is right; the claim that it is the maximum is false. The fifteen checks also select one heterogeneous scalar per case and omit the tentative case. They cannot establish that fourteen complete financial assessments are accurate. Print the fields and the resource table; use specific verified examples rather than a new accuracy headline.

I also read the original G15 sequence. The three Grok offers are d168, d172 and d174; d170 is a separate offer to Gemini. The second removes the cash request and adds Kentucky simultaneously; the third adds $250. The public last offer directly addresses Grok's stated cash concern. That improves the negotiation narrative: it shows an actual answer to a stated objection, followed by continued refusal, without supplying a treatment effect.

All 234 frozen files remain unchanged. `check_cross_review.py` and `checks.json` preserve these checks and source hashes. Engine witnesses, replay executions and semantic labels remain inherited.

## Material objections

The Opus claim that stated plans generally guide actions, with Grok the exception, exceeds the selected debt/incidents. Earlier construction records include feasible allocations that end in invalid encoding and default. The current study has not coded plan/action correspondence over every decision. It is enough to say which initial budgets and resource dependencies were followed through in the declared subset.

Both Opus and Fable classify observations with a numbered or ordered evidence ladder. Accuracy and consistency are independent: a false resource assessment can consistently produce a declaration. Sensitivity likewise needs an estimand and a comparison, not merely a higher evidence number. Use the useful distinctions in plain prose, without a highest-level badge on every incident.

The house-budget bins do not establish that rejection ignores rival opportunity. Cash excludes mortgage capacity; complete ownership does not imply immediate development legality; proposer/recipient composition changes across bins; and the post hoc cells do not isolate an effect. A non-monotonic table is admissible as an exploratory appendix diagnostic, with exact definitions, but the headline “no response to size” is not supported.

The resource partition identifies one financing dependency. It does not calibrate easy versus hard tasks or prove that declaring requires foresight. A simple policy could continue whenever finance is offered without computing a global bound. That policy has not been evaluated, and the actual first-decision texts are what add prospective evidence.

The two-stage Opus resampling breaks the fixed four-rotation design inside each sampled seed. It can be presented as another composition diagnostic, but it is not the primary distribution for the stated repeated complete-block design. The integrated main figure should retain intact blocks and explicitly call the bands empirical reweighting ranges. Neither the narrow whole-block range nor the wider game/two-stage range is independent confirmation.

## Integrated structure

Lead with the economic question and a concrete financing example. Define the cohort, prompts and response conditions briefly. Present the complete debt partition, first actions and sixteen resource-dependent episodes with full initial text in an appendix. Then present complete bargaining populations, proposer composition and relevant sensitivity, followed by the strategic incidents in their economic context. Keep eight incidents in the package and at least five in the main paper; avoid fifty quotation cards. The abstract should focus on the positive finance finding and concrete strategic behavior, without a second exploratory percentage headline.

Retain one concise synthesis of current-source facts, prospective statements, actual actions and outcomes. The result is an LLM behavioral study supported by reproducible records. Put detailed inherited checks, prompts, seeds, source paths and the full question map in the supplement. Human byline and publication status are administrative handoff items, not claims about the science.

The manuscript remains a small observational study. A clear paper can state useful positive findings without claiming optimality, cognition, effectiveness, generalization or venue acceptance.
