# Astra initial all-four review position

Written after freezing the complete independent Astra draft and before opening the other November 12 manuscripts or any cross-review.

The paper should explain observed economic action. Prospective financing offers the strongest direct connection between a stated assessment and a later objective outcome. It should not become a broad reasoning score: the resource menu changes after each action, the sixteen-case subset is retrospective, and its text is model-read rather than independently human validated.

Bargaining needs a complete descriptive account, proposer and offer-form composition, and contextual reasons. The 61/74 reasons belong to a different population from the headline swap comparison. The eight incidents should retain their economic context and distinct evidential roles. Permitted bluffing and omission must not be sold as forbidden misalignment; absence of a causal persuasion estimate does not erase the observed tactic.

I will prefer a manuscript whose actual sentences explain what the agents assessed, what they did and what happened next. I will not choose a dramatic title because it yields a cleaner slogan, nor favor the Astra draft by default. The key integration tests are coherence, population discipline, source fidelity, readable context and a clear distinction between direct arithmetic, retrospective semantic reading and earlier executed checks.

I expect all four drafts to need some synthesis. A successful revision will still be an observational study of this particular cohort, with the same limits on effectiveness, model ranking, cognition and transfer. Review volume is not validation.
