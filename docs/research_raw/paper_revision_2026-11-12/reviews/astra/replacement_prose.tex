\paragraph{Prospective financial assessment.}
Payment gives an agent a local objective with a directly observable endpoint. Sixteen encountered debts cannot be covered by cash, all building salvage and the mortgages offered initially. Fourteen are paid after sales make additional mortgages available. Thirteen successful initial texts name that resource or a budget incorporating it; one raises it tentatively. Both immediate declarations omit the released collateral. These records connect expressed anticipation to subsequent dependent actions. They do not require treating returned text as internal computation or execution length as lookahead depth.

\paragraph{Initial choices across the complete population.}
At the first financing menu, the applied action is bankruptcy in 50 of 56 debts exceeding the generous own-asset bound and in three of 131 demonstrated-payable debts. The other six above-bound episodes begin with a sale or mortgage. None of these first actions is an engine fallback. The table describes how episodes begin; continuing an unpayable debt is not scored as an error, and a received call can disagree with its accompanying text.

\paragraph{Economic value and local completion.}
Bargaining has a less directly observable endpoint. A payment can finance the seller's construction, preserve a blocker against a third party, or prolong the last opponent's survival. The same cash transfer therefore has different strategic meanings in G7, G13 and G18. Agents state these relationships explicitly. Whether they weigh them well remains separate from whether the calculation is correct, the message is received, or the stated local plan is carried out.

\paragraph{Interpreting variation in refusals.}
The opening-swap rejection contrast changes with proposer composition. The reasons coded in the seventy-four recipient-completing refusals belong to a different population, so they cannot explain that contrast. Additional cash-budget bins show no simple monotonic pattern in this cohort, but they omit collateral and change counterpart composition. They supply no identified estimate of how strongly agents respond to the size of a rival's opportunity.
