## Best material to carry forward

Sol's argument is coherent and positive. It gives the strongest weight to prospective resource inclusion and subsequent payment, then explains why bargaining has a less directly checkable endpoint. The discussion's account of cash as relational is especially useful: a payment may fund the seller, sustain a blocker, or prolong the final opponent. This connects the three incidents without inventing a common causal effect.

Printing all sixteen initial fields is a valuable addition. The reader can see the successful budgets, the tentative G20 Opus response and the two omitted-collateral sums without trusting an aggregate semantic label. The channel-selection rule is explicit: applied note when available, otherwise provider-returned explanation. Integration should keep this appendix even if the main paper stays shorter.

The interpretation of block resampling is precise. The three standardized proposer summaries (16.0, 21.5, 18.1 points) make the weighting choice visible and prevent a unique adjusted effect from being implied. The manuscript treats a hypothetical simple-policy result as unmeasured. The complete historical replay description names both report files and explains their different canonicalizations.

## Material integration decisions

1. Sol puts three incidents in the body and five in the appendix. The user has repeatedly asked for five to eight prominent transcript incidents. The complete package meets the charter, but I prefer at least five main incidents or a clearly linked substantial case section. We should decide this on scientific coherence and readability, not simply minimize pages.
2. Sol's offer table retains all rows but combines pure cash asks and other bundles. The recent referee specifically asked to expose selling a recipient its completing deed. Astra's four-form table separates cash asks and remaining bundles, which is the clearer integrated choice. The distinction is descriptive, not another hypothesis.
3. The main discussion has substantial scientific synthesis worth preserving, but some paragraphs repeat qualifications already stated next to results. Keep the cash/ownership relationships and the separation of goal completion from strategic value; compress repeated no-cognition and no-causal-effect sentences.
4. Show the sixteen resource comparisons as a figure or table in addition to the aggregate 62/53/16 partition. Sol's per-episode figure better displays what the key inequality means. The integrated choice should allow the reader to inspect both denominator and heterogeneity.
5. No new central claim is needed merely because the bargaining point estimate weakens. Both complete drafts support a local financial-planning observation plus contextual strategic behavior. Neither supports a general reasoning or bargaining-effectiveness score.
