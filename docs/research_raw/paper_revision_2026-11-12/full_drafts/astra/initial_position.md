# Initial scientific position

Written before opening any new peer draft. The scientific subject is the relationship between expressed prospective assessment and executed economic behavior. Financing dependencies offer a verifiable local objective. Bargaining supplies observations of rival-sensitive language and varied decisions whose effectiveness is unmeasured. Neither should become a sweeping claim about reasoning or alignment.

I will lead with the financing sequence as a concrete example, report the complete debt partition and sixteen necessity cases, then explain how bargaining asks a harder evaluative question. Preserve all eight conduct/competence incidents in the package. Place explicit normative framing near the conduct, and carry the configuration conditions into the relevant result without making them the subject of the paper.

The new analyses will make uncertainty and population choices visible: full completion-by-form counts, proposer composition, exact five-seed-block resampling of a fixed descriptive contrast and leave-one-block-out sensitivity. These are retrospective stability descriptions. No resampling converts the selected contrast into independent confirmation.

The paper should say what happened confidently, explain what it suggests, and put remaining unanswered questions in a concise scope account. I oppose another dramatic title centered on a newly selected denominator. I also oppose removing the social observations because they do not estimate persuasion. Each has a distinct observational claim.
