# Findings and limits

## Positive results

The most informative finance observation is 14 payments among 16 obligations that require mortgage principal absent from the initial menu. Thirteen successful entry fields include the released mortgages or a full budget encompassing them; one asks whether those mortgages could suffice. Both failures omit them in stated sums and immediately declare bankruptcy. The package prints all sixteen selected fields and their arithmetic, including the different private/provider channels.

The complete debt context is 188 episodes = 128 paid + 56 above the generous asset bound + 1 menu-restricted + 3 payable bankruptcies. The conditional 128/131 is valid for demonstrated-payable encountered obligations. It is not a difficulty calibration. Sixty-seven payments use one action and 62 are covered by initial cash/mortgages alone. Nineteen payments use new collateral, of which five are outside the necessary subset. No policy score is guessed.

G20 Sol states a $1,414 budget, completes two sales and eight mortgages, pays $1,400, and retains $14. Gemini's earlier aggregate is also right but includes offsetting $25 component errors. The result supports a stated local endpoint sustained through execution, not ten levels of lookahead or cognitive faithfulness.

The opening swap gap is 30.5 points with every proposer, 22.6 after deleting conflict-initiated threads, and 16.7 after removing every Grok-authored offer. Proposer reference weights produce 16.0/21.5/18.1-point standardized summaries. The full table retains all forms, completion cells and stages, including both-party swaps and cash asks. The 61/74 semantic population is explicitly disjoint.

Five-block variation and exact resampling are reproducible existing-record arithmetic. Swap gaps are positive in all observed blocks; the cash comparison varies in sign. The central block-resampling bands are [24.2,35.5], [-18.0,3.2], and [-3.8,31.9] for swaps, cash bids and non-Grok swaps. These are conditional empirical reweighting diagnostics, not new-seed confidence intervals or confirmation of a retrospectively selected contrast.

Eight contextual incidents preserve refusal, pricing, bluffing, correction, omission, an attempted financing boycott, successful execution and assurance withdrawal. Every displayed quotation matches original fields and PDF text; reception is claimed only after a stored-request match. G18 is explicitly survival denial against the last opponent, with a terminal estate. The explicit inheritance pitch is G13.

## Remaining limits

- One roster, five seeds, cyclic relative ordering, unequal provider response conditions, changing random consumption and unreplicated seed/rotation cells prevent model-level causal attribution.
- Offer quality, immediate own benefit and proposer behavior are not separated from rival opportunity. List price is not economic value. A negative cash point difference is not evidence of a population null.
- Semantic readings are LLM-assisted. There is no human annotation panel or independent semantic validity claim. Thirteen inclusive texts do not mean thirteen wholly correct calculations.
- Updated menus support execution. Neither complexity nor search depth is measured. No comparison calibrates liquidation quality or tactic effectiveness.
- Three historical payment witnesses, the menu search and earlier execution/default audits remain inherited. Source/quote checks are fresh but do not rerun those evaluations.
- No durable learning, memory benefit, calibrated hidden beliefs, unfamiliar-opponent transfer, general/multi-agent capability relationship or causal skill/chance split is established.
- The repository URL and frozen source commits are given. This pass has not verified public hosting of every local artifact. Human byline and responsibility decisions are outstanding for the human owner before submission, not invented in the paper.

## Dissent

The useful result is not that a large action count proves deep reasoning, or that a selected bargaining contrast establishes sensitivity to a competitor's next move. It is that prospective statements, resource requirements and executed choices can be joined at a concrete local level. I favor this balanced paper over either an error catalogue or a newly inflated finance headline. Review ratings and venue forecasts have no evidential status here.

No new games, engine executions, replay executions, baseline evaluations, rollouts, synthetic tests, provider probes or model calls were made. No branches, commits, pushes or subagents were created. The November 12 Sol directory was initially absent. No other author's new draft was opened.
