## Research-question mapping

All eight questions and A–C appear in the manuscript's Appendix D. The main contributions concern local economic assessment/execution and descriptive bargaining. Memory, learning, tactic benefits, general-capability relationships, transfer, causal skill/chance decomposition and corrected-interface performance remain unmeasured. They are not reported as negative findings.

## Scientific dissent

I disagree with making the swap contrast the title claim, because proposer composition and unequal terms remain unresolved. I also disagree with treating 14/16 as a general-planning score or implying a hypothetical bot score. Descriptive resource necessity, prospective text and payment are meaningful scientific evidence at a narrower level. That level should be the paper's center. Three main cases carry the narrative, with five complete additional incidents in the appendix; eight cases are not eight equal discoveries.
