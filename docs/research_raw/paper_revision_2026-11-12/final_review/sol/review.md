## The scientific argument

The paper's strongest sentence is the introduction's description of the financing result as "a localized observation of prospective assessment followed by dependent actions." This is supported more directly than a general claim about planning skill. The full 188-episode population remains visible, and the sixteen necessary-collateral episodes have an independently defined resource requirement. They are not sixteen handpicked impressive successes. Fourteen pay, thirteen successful initial texts name subsequent mortgages or a budget incorporating them, and one asks about the resource tentatively. Both failed initial sums omit released collateral. These are distinct outcomes and distinct kinds of text, rather than a single inflated accuracy score.

The full initial fields matter scientifically. G20 Sol states an aggregate target, not an ordered ten-call plan. G20 Opus asks whether the pink mortgages could close its gap. G2 Sol overstates yellow mortgage value by $30. G6 Opus distinguishes cash raised from cash already held. The failures' $408 and $530 are correctly added incomplete inventories, rather than accurate maximum-resource assessments. The paper now lets readers see these differences instead of asking them to trust a compressed score.

The full population gives the right context. I reproduce 188 episodes, 128 payments and sixty eliminations, plus the 62/53/16 resource partition among the 131 debts treated as demonstrated payable. Payments in those categories are 62/52/14. Historical legal-route validation for the three failed payable debts remains inherited. I did not substitute arithmetic sufficiency for a witness. I also reproduce the nineteen payments using 33 mortgages absent from the first menu. Requirements, use, feasibility and difficulty remain different concepts throughout the paper.

The first-action paragraph is appropriate as written. The first actions reproduce, including 73 mortgages, 55 sales and three declarations among payable episodes. The paper expressly says these are not a payability-classification score. Its actual liquidation instruction makes clear that continuing can be reactive and menu-supported. I would not add an accuracy statistic, rename the categories easy and hard, or reframe the two omission failures as a general capability boundary.

The bargaining contribution is complementary, not another equally identified capability result. It preserves all 920 responses, separates the 918 opportunities allowing acceptance, prints every offer form and completion category, and acknowledges who supplies the offers. All 32 stage/form/completion table cells reproduce. The swap contrast is 30.5077 points, 16.6667 without Grok authors and 22.5706 after excluding conflicting-opening threads. The within-recipient cash-bid differences also show why the pooled negative cash estimate cannot be read as a uniform behavioral direction. The paper does not present list value as economic utility or infer insensitivity from coarse capacity bins.

I independently reproduce all three empirical reweightings, the five block points and leave-one-block-out values. The main figure and its caption describe the displayed objects correctly. The intact-block procedure holds the four rotations together; individual-game and two-stage sampling alter their representation. All three no-Grok empirical ranges include zero. None remedies retrospective discovery. The paper says this at first presentation and in the appendix. More uncertainty calculations would not resolve the economic composition problem, and none is needed for this review.

The positive LLM-related conclusion is therefore bounded but concrete. These configurations produce prospective budgets, descriptions of newly available finance, rival-focused calculations, factual corrections and conditional bargaining messages. Some stated budgets are realized across dependent actions. This remains useful evidence even though the paper cannot establish strategic effectiveness, cognitive transparency or superiority to a reference policy. The related-work account presents this as a connection among populations, statements and execution, not as the discovery of negotiation, trade withholding or Monopoly-playing agents.
