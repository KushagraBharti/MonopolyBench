# Integrated findings and limits

The evidence supports a complete observational study of the historical cohort.

- Sixteen debts require mortgages initially unavailable; fourteen are paid. Initial text includes the resource or an aggregate budget in thirteen successes, considers it tentatively in one, and omits it in both failures. This applies a published Monopoly rule to live holdings, rather than discovering an unfamiliar rule.
- All 188 financing episodes are partitioned by resource requirements. Successful payment, not an arithmetic bound alone, establishes 128 feasible routes; historical legal witnesses establish three additional routes.
- All 920 responses are retained, with the 918 legally acceptable offers shown by stage, form and completion. Proposer and recipient composition materially affect comparisons.
- Eight contextual incidents show economically situated statements and tactics. Their outcomes and channels are shown, without converting selected acts into a deception prevalence or effectiveness estimate.
- G18's public renewal accompanies a returned reservation before withdrawal; G2 and G15 blocking policies predate the shown warnings. These chronological distinctions are preserved in the final incidents.

The manuscript does not claim difficulty calibration, cognitive transparency, optimal decisions, causal persuasion, learning, a model ranking, an identified completion premium, or a general statement/action prediction rule. It supplies no baseline performance that was not run. Human coding remains absent. All interpretation is scoped to the declared populations and selected cases.
