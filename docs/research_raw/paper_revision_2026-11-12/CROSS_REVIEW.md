# All-four whole-paper cross-review

Each reviewer reads every complete November 12 manuscript, including their own, and proposes the best single integrated paper. This is not a division by topic. All independent drafts are frozen in `four_draft_manifest.json`.

Read `CHARTER.md`, both entire reports in `../paper_revision_2026-11-11/feedback/2026-11-11_two_reviews/`, all four complete manuscripts and their handoffs/evidence maps. First record an initial position before opening any other review. Do not read the other November 12 review folders or any integration files that may appear during the review.

The purpose is to strengthen the science and clarity. Identify the most consequential supported finding, the best explanatory narrative, useful positive results, and which qualifications can be consolidated. Preserve eight distinct incidents in the package and the full question map. Distinguish resource requirements, observed planning statements, factual correctness and actual execution. Do not equate a first-menu arithmetic partition with validated difficulty. Do not equate prospective text with internal cognition. Assess the complete negotiation populations, composition, uncertainty, source conditions and retained provenance.

Compare all four drafts; do not defend one's own by default. Check genuinely new claims against existing records where feasible. Identify errors that materially change an interpretation; do not turn this into a cosmetic-error catalogue. Give exact passages and evidence, including errors in one's own version. State what was freshly checked and what was inherited. No review score or acceptance guarantee.

Only write in `reviews/<author>/`. Required outputs: `initial_position.md`, `review.md`, `findings.json` with stable IDs/severity/exact locations/evidence/recommendation, `integration_proposal.md`, finished `replacement_prose.tex` and `verification.md`. A complete rewrite is unnecessary in the cross-review; replacement prose should express the best integrated sections and claim, with compile verification in the reviewer's own directory if practical. Check frozen hashes before and after.

No new evaluations: no games, engine execution, replay execution, baselines, synthetic tests, branches, rollouts or provider probes. Only source reading, arithmetic and resampling existing records, quotation checks and document builds. No human-coding claim or invented byline. No subagents, changes outside the assigned directory, commits, pushes or messages to finished peers. Stop after the handoff; further rounds receive separate assignments.
