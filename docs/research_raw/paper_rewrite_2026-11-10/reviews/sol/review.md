# Sol whole-paper cross-review

## Judgment

The strongest paper is a finite-cohort behavioral study of bargaining, development and creditor redistribution. Its positive finding is that these agents explicitly negotiate over rivals' ability to build and over who would receive a distressed player's assets. The complete transaction record supplies scale and heterogeneity; contextual records supply the stated considerations. Ownership, requested investment, executed investment and accessible finance then explain different stages of the observed economic sequence. This is useful empirical knowledge about these configurations. It is not a validated strategic-quality measure or an identified explanation of winning.

No independent draft should become the integrated paper unchanged. Astra is the most economical complete architecture, Sol has a continuous economic argument and careful local finance, Opus contributes counteroffer transitions and G2, and Fable connects cases to aggregate results and carries response conflicts into negotiation. The synthesis needs their evidence, not their strongest adjectives. In particular, completion is not an identified premium, feasibility is not sound investment, and a later bankruptcy does not measure purchased survival.

I read all four complete source trees, including included appendices, generated tables, transcript definitions and handoffs. I extracted all 130 PDF pages and inspected every page in contact sheets. I wrote `initial_position.md` before opening Astra's report. I then read its all-four and in-progress reports, check scripts/results, literature follow-up and render report. `performed_checks.json` records my own retained-data and original-field work. No engine result was newly executed.

## What survives and deserves main-text space

The complete 918-offer table is informative when combined with response styles, thread position, counter contents and actual reasons. Author-only offers have 159 rejections and 19 acceptances in 289; recipient-only offers have 38 and 24 in 140. The completing side pays net cash in 373 of 429 one-sided offers. That describes different bundles and selected encounters. It does not isolate relative-gain preferences. The explicit exception matters: Opus accepts 7/52 author-only and 5/42 recipient-only offers, while the other three have the opposite acceptance ordering. Thread position reduces the pooled contrast and belongs in the main account, not only a robustness appendix.

I independently joined all 394 counters with actor reversal and exchange continuity. In the 111 author-only counters, 92 preserve the same original author's newly complete group, 88 leave only that party completing, and 85 of those improve the counterer's cash. The 79 preserving the entire noncash bundle all improve its cash. Those are genuine repricings of unchanged bundles. They do not identify the marginal price of completion. Other cells also usually increase the counterer's cash; the correct comparison is neither universal resistance nor universal completion pricing. Explicit notes can state a completion premium, as G2 does, while also making a financial error.

The development and current-debt results are stronger than the descriptive language sometimes suggests. The full-window 43/63 and entry-buildable 34/42 populations resolve different questions. The twenty full-window residuals are nine without a recorded construction option, four defaults, four received-call conflicts and three valid-end windows. These categories describe observed access and response resolution, not mutually exclusive psychological causes. The 61 reachable Opus purchase multisets show missed execution of affordable requested allocations under the retained checks. They do not estimate lost rent or prove that investment was prudent. The 56/3/1 debt partition gives a complete, sharply defined historical finance finding, with successful actual staged liquidation beside the errors and stock lock.

Inheritance belongs in the central argument. During continuing play, estates complete 52 groups, compared with 63 trade completions. Another 69 estate completions occur only when the game ends. The 202/227 nonterminal inherited deeds received by eventual winners describe concentration, while creditor identity and eventual victory emerge from the same trajectory. Show that population before the more dramatic 121-versus-63 total. The G2 and G13 paths make inheritance a concrete economic stake without claiming an effect on winning or survival.

## Errors and objections by draft

### Astra

The draft correctly treats the study as behavioral and is the clearest length model. Its sparse exhibits, however, omit information needed to understand its leading bargaining contrast. Add the Opus exception, thread position, counter contents and Grok's negotiation-conflict join. The sentence inferring from cash medians that recipients do not respond to cash alone is not identified by those medians. Use the actual stated reasons for that conclusion.

Its G13 language still attributes time to the transaction: "The purchase bought immediate liquidity" is defensible as a cash-flow statement, but "an insurance sale can postpone" the later transfer suggests a changed continuation that was never evaluated. Write the settlement balance and later bankruptcy separately. The first-window phrase "never become buildable" needs "on the recorded menus" in headline locations. Its disclosure and G3 controls are good appendix material, not expendable evidence. Conditions should be compressed by removing repetition, not by reducing every confound to two generic sentences.

Astra's cross-review catches many real errors but does not flag Opus's G2 zero-rent wording. The source episode has $44 paid rent, whereas the spending unit has zero developed rent. That distinction must survive integration. I also disagree with treating the seven-case selection as settled; see dissent below.

### Sol, including my own earlier claims

My whole-paper sequence is useful, but my case section is detached from the population evidence. Move incidents beside bargaining, development and finance. Add the counteroffer and conflict analyses that were absent from my independent draft. Drop the duplicate response figure/table and move detailed reference-policy readiness to the appendix.

I retract "payment purchases time" in G13. We observe a $200 receipt and forty-three subsequent turns, not forty-three turns caused by the payment. Replace "induce counters" with "receive counters." Replace "causal order" with "economic sequence" unless discussing a specific rule transition. My description of Empty Commitments' "validated ontology" is too broad: the primary source reports human checks of specified detector and judging components, not universal validity of an ontology or its transfer here. My statement that a private-note contradiction means a purchase cannot be a deliberate abandonment is too categorical; the record does not establish deliberate abandonment, and does not reveal a single intention.

I suspected my portable-artifact relative path in the initial note. Direct resolution confirms it exists. That suspicion is cleared, not a finding. My draft's local quote checks are substantial but do not newly validate delivered prompts or engine witnesses; the existing separation between performed and inherited evidence should remain. The integrated research-role account must include all four independent whole-paper authors and Fable 5.2, while retaining Fable 5.1 as earlier history.

### Opus

The new G2 narrative is valuable, but contains two material errors. Its quoted Sol note says $520 can buy nine yellow houses; nine cost $1,350. Keep the exact quote and state that $520 alone buys three. The outcome then says Sol "collects no rent on them." The completion ledger and original G2 event 1566 show $44 Atlantic rent at turn 77. The correct statement is zero developed yellow rent, $44 total yellow-group rent after completion, $300 construction outlay and $150 salvage. Deed inheritance later in the trajectory should not imply that these sold houses passed intact.

The abstract's "most of the shortfall" due to defaults is false for the full-window Opus population. Five of its eight unbuilt groups lack a build option in the recorded window; three end in defaults. The entry-buildable subset is different. Feasible allocation is not "sound," and a failed encoding can be expressed by another legal order. An immediate default does not establish zero later income.

G12 and G19 do not contradict every text channel. Their public messages agree with the received bankruptcy or proposal. Content and the attached note oppose it. A mortgage-only subtotal is not the whole displayed menu, which also offers sales. The refusal count is 1,605 affected decisions but only 1,604 first-refusal recovery chains; G2 d129 is invalid then refused. The counter table's claim to omit cells smaller than two is contradicted by its omitted five- and six-counter cells. "Nothing" means no group completion, not no economic benefit. Population conduct concentration and advice to prioritize such cases are unsupported by purposive selection. The 43-page treatment can be shortened without removing any of these distinctions.

### Fable 5.2

Fable has the best integration of contextual cases with economic results and adds useful thread-position and conflict joins. Its headline rhetoric is less reliable. "Mostly" cash-poor is wrong at its own threshold: 21/48 is below half, while 21/30 reverses the conditioning set. Retain the seller-cash medians, $194 versus $571, and exact denominators. Cash direction does not prove a premium, and bank list prices differ across deeds. No controlled change establishes that a deed was released because its holder ran short of cash.

The nine no-build-option rows establish recorded access, not every possible financing route. Arriving mortgaged or holding less than one house's price is an immediate condition, not a proof for the full management window. Defaults and conflicts likewise cannot account for an identified amount of lost early value. These restrictions apply even when total observed receipts differ sharply.

G15 is a strong four-player case because both sides explicitly discuss development capacity. Its $830/$233 cash consequences are correct. But $600 buys one house on each green; it is not the minimum legal start. A single green house costs $200, and source reading of the evenness rule permits unequal levels differing by one. The quoted note describes inability to fund a complete first layer, not inability to build anything. My check establishes arithmetic, pretrade holdings and bank stock, not an executed post-acceptance route, a maximum financing budget or optimal refusal.

Delete the causal "protection bought lasted 43 turns," the claims that plans were "sound," and the categorical exclusion of deliberate surrender. A false private note can faithfully report a mistaken belief. Related-work sentences grouping all comparators as utility-scored or endorsing their rankings need narrowing. The first-completion outcome table is descriptive context and belongs in the appendix; the main argument should not become a ranking by rent with disclaimers attached.

## Strongest dissent from Astra

I favor eight distinct main incidents: G2, G15, G7, G19, G20 financial denial, G12, G13 and G18. Astra proposes seven and moves G7 to the appendix. G2 contains a bluff followed by refusal, mixed with an incorrect capacity calculation and a much longer ownership trajectory. G7 supplies the clean settled public/private limit discrepancy and an explicit recipient reason. Keeping G7 in a short card preserves an outcome contrast the selected G2 does not replace. Neither proves persuasion. If length forces seven, moving G7 is defensible, but its loss should be a conscious evidential choice rather than an assumption that G2 dominates it.

I agree that G15 is the better main reciprocal-completion refusal than G11. The four-player setting and later third-party estate are useful; the source mistakes must still be visible. G11 remains the appendix control for a recipient correcting a false completion claim and then refusing a genuinely completing replacement. G3 remains the stock/finance control. G19 and G12 must stay main, because competent correction and severe contradictory execution prevent the paper from becoming a collection of coherent-strategy anecdotes.

I also resist compressing the early conditions to two paragraphs if it removes the affected populations or the refusal/recovery distinction. Around 700–850 words, one concise configuration table and an appendix pointer are realistic. They protect the results' meaning without recreating the long audit preamble.

## Whole-paper recommendations

Use an economic title and a roughly 220-word abstract with at most four headline quantities. Introduce one question, how bargaining over rival development relates to execution and creditor redistribution, rather than eleven promised studies. State implemented rules, roster, dependence and observation/recovery conditions before interpretation. Explain units and source status once. Lead bargaining with the census, then heterogeneity and actual counter changes. Place G2/G15/G7/G19 beside it. Present development using the full-window and entry populations, give valid holds and successful repairs, and place G20 beside financing rival construction. Keep current-debt finance with G12 and a successful liquidation contrast. Follow with nonterminal inheritance, G13 and G18. Give temporal evidence a compact balanced section with G9 attainment, G16 conflicting execution and feedback recurrence under unequal conditions.

Keep one explicit supported/unestablished table for Q1–Q8 and A–C. Strategic reasons, contextual opponent-capacity deductions, negotiation actions, targets and revisions, realized flows and collective transfers receive evidence. Optimality, calibrated hidden-belief prediction, causal persuasion, durable learning, memory benefit, general-to-multi-agent capability, unfamiliar-opponent transfer and skill/luck shares remain unestablished. An unanswered measurement is not a negative capability result.

The closest work deserves specific credit. AgentMonopoly v2 studies actions, language and personas; its §4 and Appendix A.2 specify its prompt norms. Arena v3 has state-grounded analysis and full cross-judge pipeline replication; do not call it generic text labeling. My fresh primary-source reading confirms these claims. I also inspected Counterparty Modeling v1 §3.3 and Empty Commitments v2 §§3.4/5.6; the former varies supplied ranked preference summaries, and the latter reports component-level human comparison. These are scoped readings, not full-paper reviews or comparator executions. Version-specific implementation claims not re-inspected here remain inherited.

## Three likely reasons for rejection of the best integration

1. The historical cohort is small, dependent and heavily affected by information and response conditions. Describing complete configurations is correct but does not supply a clean strategic-quality comparison or corrected-interface result.
2. The added scientific contribution may remain too narrow relative to existing Monopoly, economic-agent and bargaining studies. Connected complete trajectories are useful, but a reviewer may still demand a measured generalization or mechanism intervention. More careful prose cannot meet that demand.
3. The semantic evidence is retrospective, selected and LLM-assisted, with family overlap, uneven channels and partial unblinding. Repeated agreement and mechanical reconciliation do not independently establish semantic validity or prevalence.

These objections remain after the factual corrections. The integration can make a coherent, source-faithful observational paper; it cannot promise acceptance or resolve every research question.
