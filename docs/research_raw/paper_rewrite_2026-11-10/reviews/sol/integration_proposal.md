# Complete integrated-paper proposal

## Identity and editorial decisions

Proposed title: *Bargaining, development and creditor inheritance in MonopolyBench*.

Central question: How do configured agents bargain over development opportunities whose value depends on rival liquidity, execution and creditor redistribution? The answer is descriptive and positive. Some records explicitly weigh relative development and estates; transactions encounter different access and response outcomes; creditor transfers connect them over time. Do not claim that the paper identifies rational prices, the effect of a tactic or a complete-system skill ranking.

Target about 8,300 main prose words, excluding quote bodies, tables, references and appendices. This is an editorial target, not a measurement claim. Use the existing evidence without new evaluations. Preserve all independent drafts, the original outline and research archives.

## Exhibits and appendix

Use three principal figures or tables: bargaining responses with a compact counter transition inset; development resolution with the two denominators; current-debt finance with accessible G7 clearly separated from nominal bounds. A small route table should show 63 trade, 52 nonterminal inheritance and 69 terminal inheritance. It need not become a fourth large figure. Keep absolute counts accessible without reproducing the same data in both a table and a full chart.

Put the full cohort, per-configuration/seed/game offer cells, thread-position counts, pure-versus-changed-bundle counters, exploratory seller-cash thresholds, typed flows, episode rent, spending units and all sixty eliminations in the evidence appendix. Do not put a leaderboard-looking win grid or implied ROI scatter in the main paper. An episode-rent column must say total paid rent; developed-rent columns must say developed. The G2 discrepancy shows why this matters.

The methods appendix should define event canonicalization and the engine's version-dependent rules; separate the 56 nominal-bound negatives, three positive witnesses and one mortgage-only graph result; retain the bundled-sale counterexample's implication without narrating the entire historical debugging process. The portable 126-case package reproduces fixed supplied cases, not population extraction by itself. Historical readiness policies/scenarios get one short paragraph and a source link, not a capability baseline.

The research-provenance appendix should name the actual human direction and LLM research instances, including family overlap, Fable 5.1's historical role and the four complete independent rewrites including Fable 5.2. It should identify two Astra reason readers, partial exposure, single-reader temporal coding, quote fields/hashes, raw source paths and exact checkpoints. Frozen drafts and disagreements remain archived. Do not call the four AI authors independent human annotators or consensus a validity study.

## Material boundaries on the integrated interpretation

Cash is negotiated compensation in heterogeneous bundles; an estimated completion premium is unavailable. No-build-option means absent from recorded menus, and leaves unvisited financing paths unresolved. Reachable construction establishes executability, not investment quality. Historical current-debt impossibility concerns own assets in that phase, not earlier rescue or wise play. Later bankruptcy is chronology, not purchased-survival duration. A recipient's correction is direct textual evidence on that premise, while delivery and settlement alone leave belief unresolved. Neither benchmark replay nor a document build validates semantic coding.

These are not optional caveats to append after a stronger headline. They define the positive findings themselves. `replacement_prose.tex` supplies a coherent set of replacement sections at those levels, while the four frozen drafts retain the longer unchanged material available for integration.
