## Central judgment

Use a behavioral paper about how bargaining, development, debt and inheritance interact in this recorded economy. The audit is the evidence discipline. It should neither replace the scientific subject nor be advertised as a generally validated measurement method.

The strongest integrated paper will not be any author's draft copied wholesale. Sol provides the clearest continuous economic argument. Opus provides the counteroffer transitions and useful longitudinal incidents. Fable connects bargaining conditions to realized construction and rent, carries Grok's response conflict into the negotiation counts, and gives inheritance its proper place. Astra provides the shortest architecture and careful current-debt and channel distinctions. Each draft contains claims that must be weakened or corrected, including mine.

The guiding distinction is between an observed economic sequence and its causal explanation. We can explain the implemented mechanism, identify a stated consideration, and report what happened next. Those three achievements do not identify what would have happened without a message, refusal, default or inheritance rule.

## What the integration should argue

1. The cohort contains substantive bargaining over completion, liquidity and rivals' ability to develop. Responses differ across configurations; a common pooled rejection contrast is not uniform across configurations. Counteroffers often preserve the rival's proposed completion while demanding more cash. That is negotiation over terms, not proof of a uniquely rival-sensitive price premium.
2. Acquisition, legal availability, submitted action and applied construction are separate observed stages. The 63 trade-completed groups have 43 first-window builds. The 20 residual windows comprise nine with no construction option observed, four defaults, four received-call conflicts and three valid ends. The nine never-legal windows are not nine demonstrated impossibilities under every possible financing sequence.
3. Most current elimination debts are unpayable from the debtor's own assets, but three were payable and one nominally wealthy position was locked by the hotel rule. These are local financing findings, not retrospective optimality labels.
4. Creditor inheritance redistributes productive assets and blocking deeds. The nonterminal comparison is the meaningful main result: 52 complete groups and 227 deeds transferred through estates while play continued, against 63 trade completions and 207 traded deeds. Terminal transfer can be shown separately. Selected bargaining accounts explicitly concern who will inherit, but their effectiveness is not identified.

This is a stronger, more accurate thesis than either an audit-method novelty claim or a broad finding that agents are rational, misaligned, agreement-resistant or capable of learning.

## Fable findings requiring correction

### F1. “Mostly cash-poor” reverses the conditioning set

The abstract says completing deeds change hands mainly when their holders are short of cash; the introduction, bargaining section and conclusion repeat it. At the draft's defined threshold, 21 of 48 paying-side-completing settlements have a seller below $100, which is 43.75%, not a majority. The 21 of 30 figure answers a different question: among cash-receiving settlements with a seller below $100, how many complete the payer's group? That proportion is 70%.

My independent ledger re-tabulation confirms both counts, the $194 versus $571 seller-cash medians, and all four threshold cuts. Keep the association and explicit units. Suggested text: “Sellers held less cash in settlements that completed the paying party's group: median $194, compared with $571 in cash settlements completing neither party's group. Twenty-one of 48 had sellers below $100.” Do not say that cash-poor sellers released deeds that otherwise liquid holders refused; that is an unobserved counterfactual.

### F2. Cash terms do not identify a completion premium

The 373 of 429 cash-direction result is correct and useful. “No list price asks for this premium” and “the systems price the completion itself” go beyond it. Bundles differ in deeds, cards, cash, strategic position and mortgage charges. Even the claim that a completing deed costs “the same ... as any other” is false literally: bank list prices vary across deeds. The intended statement is that the bank does not condition a deed's listed price on who completes a group.

Use “The completing side usually pays net cash.” Combine this observation with explicit stated reasons and Opus's counter analysis, while distinguishing each evidence type. My counter join finds 79 of 88 counters that retain only the original author's completion preserve the entire noncash bundle and increase the counterer's cash. These are true cash-only repricings, but cash increases also occur in other categories. They do not identify a causal rival-completion premium.

### F3. A false private note is not automatically a breach of honesty

Setup says a false private note “departs from a request, but it can also be an error.” The request is for true reasoning, not guaranteed factual accuracy. A mistaken belief honestly expressed need not depart from it at all. Say: “A false private note does not by itself establish dishonesty or instruction violation.” Retain the standard/action-only distinction.

### F4. “Lost most of its value” is not a measured result

The introduction, development section, discussion and conclusion attribute most of Opus's early economic value loss to refusal recovery. In the first-window population, five of Opus's eight nonbuilds are never-legal windows and three are defaults. The 61 reachable allocations demonstrate missed execution at particular decisions, not foregone rent or total value. Rent after completion is observed, while the rent a corrected action would have earned is not.

This is the same substantive error as Opus finding O2. Replace it with the precise action-stage finding. Preserve the first-window rent totals as realized receipts with unequal exposure and survival, not returns caused by response policy.

### F5. Unbuildable on recorded menus is not an exhaustive financing impossibility

The nine never-legal windows are empirically established. Seven have a mortgaged completing deed and two leave less cash than one house costs. Those facts explain the absence of an immediate build option. They do not prove that no legal sequence of mortgages, redemptions, sales or later management choices could make a build possible within the window. Fable's appendix caption mostly preserves this distinction, but headings such as “could not legally be built on” and the causal attribution of all seventeen residuals to purchases or response path should be scoped to recorded windows. Grok's notes in several such acquisitions also express an intention to wait. Opportunity and preference are not mutually exclusive explanations.

### F6. Refusal and recovery require the G2 d129 exception

Fable correctly states 1,604 first-attempt refusals and 1,605 decisions with a refused attempt. The paragraph then says the harness resends an action-only request after them, which is false for the second-attempt refusal at G2 d129. Its default discussion likewise makes all 61 defaults follow refusal before invalid construction, although its table note correctly says sixty. State the ordering once and use it consistently. “Every system gets one corrective retry after an invalid action” should make clear that the two-attempt budget is shared with refusal recovery.

### F7. Feasible plans are not “sound,” and conflict does not resolve intent

The discussion calls the 61 Opus plans “sound as plans.” They request reachable purchase multisets, not proven good investments. G12's reading says the bankruptcy “cannot be read as a decision to surrender,” while the temporal section rules out a change of mind. The channels conflict; that prevents a clean attribution, not every possible intentional interpretation. Use “does not establish a deliberate surrender” and “does not identify a change of mind.”

### F8. The G13 transaction does not establish 43 turns of purchased survival

The exact later bankruptcy is valuable context, but “the protection bought lasted 43 turns” implies a causal survival duration. The seller already had a mortgage route under the lower cash offer, and no alternate continuation was run. Say “Grok remained in the game for 43 more turns before bankruptcy transferred the yellows to Sol.” The observed $70 price difference remains unassigned to persuasion.

Likewise G18's explanation that paying prolongs a likely estate recipient's opponent is a stated rationale. The paper should not endorse “cash ... only delays an estate the payer may receive anyway” as a general economic fact. Buying the green completion also changes both parties' positions.

### F9. Rival attention, relative-gain preference and quality remain separate

The three evidence forms in the discussion are not “independent places”: they arise from overlapping offers, states and texts. The new G15 incident is a strong example of explicit rival-development reasoning, but exact cash arithmetic alone does not validate all collateral and timing claims in its notes. My source checks establish all fourteen new quote fields and all eight G15/G11 public-message deliveries. They do not establish optimal rejection, hidden-belief inference, or a maximum build calculation.

### F10. Avoid new sweeping comparator or negative claims

“These studies score outcomes against a defined utility” groups rank-based game studies with bilateral utility experiments. “These designs ... support the rankings theirs can” endorses all named leaderboards beyond the evidence read. Narrow each citation to its verified design. A late source review checked most new sources only at abstract level. The existing literature record should remain explicit about that.

The sentence “No trade ... or bankruptcy is shown to be good or bad play” should say no ex ante optimality claim is established. An observed local financial or execution error can be described directly without proving the optimal complete-game policy.

## Proposed integration and unresolved decisions

Keep the compactness of Astra/Sol with the economic connections of Opus/Fable. Main results should begin earlier than pages 6–7. A short configuration table and two paragraphs of conditions precede the results; exhaustive implementation, code-reading and provenance details belong in the appendix.

Proposed main sequence:

1. Introduction with a concrete behavioral thesis, not a promised answer to every broad construct.
2. Environment, cohort and response/information conditions.
3. Measures and evidence: ledgers, exact-prefix feasibility, channel-specific text and LLM-assisted coding.
4. Bargaining: response population and heterogeneity, actual counteroffer changes, stated rival consequences, exploratory seller-cash association.
5. Development: full-window and entry-buildable denominators, reachable allocations, realized receipts. Keep a distinction between a missing menu option and impossibility under financing.
6. Debt and inheritance: 56/3/1 finance census, a successful liquidation contrast, nonterminal estate flows and sustained bargaining trajectories.
7. Compact temporal evidence, discussion and question map; limitations consolidated with each relevant result.
8. Conclusion and evidence appendix.

I favor seven main contextual incidents placed beside their economic results: G2 pricing/bluff/reversal, G15 repeated rival-development refusal, G19 correction with acceptance, G20 financial-denial appeal, G12 bankruptcy conflict, G13 omission/estate framing and G18 standing-offer withdrawal. G11's two-player correction/refusal, G7's clean price bluff, G3's false obstacle and the G20 disclosure remain appendix contrasts. This replaces my earlier provisional G11 main selection with G15, because it shows four-player rival reasoning and a third-party estate transfer. G2 must visibly correct the quoted $520/nine-house error. If seven cases make the main text too long, shorten the commentary and quoted spans before dropping entire distinct mechanisms.

The four candidate figures are bargaining response/counter content, development with explicit response conditions, current-debt finance, and nonterminal inheritance. Do not duplicate the same complete table and chart in the main text. Rent totals can be integrated with the development exhibit, but should not be placed as implied causal returns. Keep exact values in source-linked tables.

Potential title: “Bargaining, Development, and Inheritance in MonopolyBench.” It names the empirical subject without promising a general capability test or an audit-method contribution. The final title and abstract should be written after the integrated claims settle.

I dissent from treating every positive framing as automatically better. “Mostly,” “sound,” “lost value,” “premium,” “protection lasted” and “only delays” make several peer drafts punchier by adding inferences the record does not support. The integrated paper can be direct without those substitutions. I also dissent from reducing the social material to two methodological curiosities. The user requested contextual conduct and the data contain real bargaining tactics, which deserve careful description in the economic narrative.
