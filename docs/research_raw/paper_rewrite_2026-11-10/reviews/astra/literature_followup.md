# Primary-source follow-up during complete-draft comparison

Checked 10 November 2026. These checks follow Sol's and Opus's leads; they are not blind literature discovery. They do not modify the frozen literature review or bibliography.

- [MERIT / AgoraBench, ACL 2026](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.1155/), official [PDF](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.1155.pdf). Read metadata, abstract, §§3–4 and the limitations passage. The "monopoly" setting is a single-seller market, not the Monopoly board game. The environment is bilateral buyer/seller product bargaining with specified willingness to pay and cost. The metric combines consumer surplus, movement from the initial price, and embedding-based acquisition similarity; its weights are fitted to human preference judgments. The paper is a useful contrast for having explicit preference and valuation assumptions. It does not give a portable utility function for this four-player game. Do not borrow its metric names as though our completion counts measured surplus or negotiation power.
- [Counterparty Modeling is Not Strategy, arXiv:2605.16575v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.16575v1). Read the abstract and inspected design/information, outcome and belief-accuracy passages, including §§3.3–6 and Appendix B summaries. The informed conditions supply a ranked summary of the partner's preferences, not the full utility weights. The paper separates accurate expressed preference modeling from resulting bargaining gains. It is relevant to Q2 and the speech/execution distinction, but saying simply that agents infer hidden preferences accurately loses the information-treatment condition. Keep any summary specific to its measured setting and information access.
- [Supply-chain bargaining, arXiv:2608.07538v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07538v1). Abstract and version metadata checked; full methods not read in this pass. Its buyer has private demand information and negotiates a quantity/payment contract against an equilibrium benchmark. This supports a narrow contrast with the present public-cash, evolving-portfolio game. Do not import its surplus, reliability or capability findings as evidence for our four configurations.
- [CEO-Bench, arXiv:2606.18543v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18543v2). Abstract and metadata checked. It concerns simulated startup operation over 500 days, with a Python interface and a rule-based comparator. It is appropriate long-horizon context, not evidence that the systems studied here are already negotiating real commercial contracts.

The first sentence of Opus's introduction says agents act on behalf of people and firms but cites simulation papers. Reframe the motivation around what economic-agent evaluations ask systems to do unless a source establishing actual deployment is supplied. The related-work additions should clarify the inference target rather than become a broad citation inventory.
