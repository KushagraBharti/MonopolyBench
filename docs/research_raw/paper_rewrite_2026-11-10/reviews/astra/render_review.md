# Visual inspection of the completed peer drafts

Rendered the frozen Sol PDF, 25 pages, and Opus PDF, 43 pages, with PyMuPDF. Inspected every page in contact sheets under `render/`. Automated page geometry found no text span outside the displayed page in either document. This is a page-layout review, not a new build or verification of the figures against original games.

Sol's continuous layout is compact. The two figures and the management table support the narrative, and the seven transcript cases occupy about five pages without visible clipped boxes. Opus's tables expose the counteroffer result and the configuration exception better, but the document devotes many extra pages to full condition, counter, per-game and provenance tables. Several large blank regions and full-page floats increase the page count. The first result does not start until page 7. These are presentation tradeoffs rather than numerical defects.

For integration, keep readable native transcript cards and substantive table content, but move exhaustive re-tabulations to the evidence appendix. Avoid inheriting all figures just because they already exist. The proposed core is a bargaining figure/table, a development-resolution exhibit and a compact finance display. A winner grid and spending scatter are optional appendix material.

I subsequently rendered and inspected all 39 Fable 5.2 pages in five contact sheets. The geometry check found no out-of-page text. Its bargaining and development figures place conditions beside consequences effectively, and the inheritance figure distinguishes the 69 terminal completions from the 52 with further play. Its main results nevertheless start on page 6, and the abstract is overloaded with numerical sequences. The estate table is dense and should remain appendix material. These are presentation observations, not source-data verification of every plotted mark.

This review did not recompile peer PDFs, inspect every page at full resolution, or validate all figure marks. Astra's own independent document has its separate build and render report under `full_drafts/astra/qa/`.
