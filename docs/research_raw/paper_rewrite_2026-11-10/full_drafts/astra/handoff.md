## Intellectual choices

The thesis is economic interdependence: agents bargain over rival development, survival and eventual estates, while action access and response handling determine which plans execute. I give the stronger offer-response contrast and configuration-specific styles full main-text space. I connect acquisition, construction, liquidation and inheritance, then show seven distinct contextual conduct incidents. I retain the eight-question map near the discussion rather than promising eight capability studies in the introduction.

I dissent from a generic auditing-method identity without external validation, from agreement-bias claims based on low acceptance, from financially justified bankruptcy language, and from treating correlated refusal changes as an identified provider-policy intervention. I keep three payable debts and the hotel lock precise. I retain G13's d250-before-d251 framing and later d311 estate outcome. Explicit correction with acceptance and contradictory bankruptcy are main-text contrasts, not evidence of malicious intention.

## Self-critique for the four-way discussion

1. The observational bargaining contrast is stronger than the old sufficiency-rule headline but is still confounded by offer selection and configuration composition. The final paper should consider a compact per-configuration display if another draft supports it cleanly.
2. The setup still takes substantial space. Compress only after retaining the conditions necessary for each result; deleting them would falsely simplify the cohort.
3. Seven contextual incidents occupy five pages. This preserves the user's request, but case selection and figure layout remain open to better proposals.
4. I use one figure and a four-case financing table. A good debt/access figure could be a stronger main exhibit than the table without reviving annotation collisions.
5. The bibliography is deliberately focused. Two newer methodological papers are cited only at abstract-supported scope; the complete literature updates from peers may sharpen comparisons.
6. This is a complete independent candidate, not a final accepted or integrated paper. It does not satisfy the external referee's request for held-out experiments, and I do not claim otherwise.
