## Important distinctions

- The 272 frozen baseline-source hashes remain unchanged.
- These document checks do not constitute a fresh independent scientific replication.
- Quote validity establishes the text and source identity, not the truth of an explanation, actual cognition, or causal influence.
- Retained-ledger recounts check correspondence with the published inputs, not how all raw rows were selected.
- No new author draft was read to produce this version. Peer completion notices may have arrived while writing; their drafts were not inspected.

## Scope decisions

The full referee critique was considered. Its focus/length/conditions/semantic-transparency objections are accepted. Its request for new controlled evaluation is outside the user's authorized scope. Seven contextual incidents remain because the user explicitly requested five to eight; the paper does not claim that adding examples validates persuasion or prevalence. A behavioral identity is a deliberate scientific choice, not a claim that editing satisfies the referee's experimental demands.
