# Primary-source checks for the independent Astra draft

Checked 10 November 2026 using primary web pages. This is a targeted update, not a new exhaustive literature review. Prior detailed paper/repository inspections remain inherited and are not represented as newly repeated full-text reviews.

- Arena: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14825 resolves to v3, 21 August 2026. Abstract verifies 2,583 emails, 20 year-long runs, state/reasoning-grounded classification and replication with two other judge families. The independent draft makes no unsupported generic first-state-grounded-speech claim.
- AgentMonopoly: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29330 resolves to v2, 22 October 2026. Abstract verifies model-family/size and persona studies and language/action comparison. Rule and repository distinctions in the draft rely on retained full-source reviews, not this abstract alone.
- DECE: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35773 resolves to v1, 26 October 2026. Exact title uses singular 'Eurogame'. Abstract verifies ten games and matched execution-framework comparisons. No claim that our observational cohort identifies equivalent intervention effects.
- FinBoardBench: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27896 resolves to v1, 27 May 2026. Abstract verifies three financial board games and dynamic wealth-management target. Retry and bankruptcy details are inherited from the earlier appendix readings.
- NegotiationArena: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/bianchi24a.html verifies ICML 2024, authors and controlled behavioral-tactic work. Saved-game modification details remain from prior source inspection.
- Measurement-First Auditing of Agentic Leaderboards: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05830v1, 5 October 2026. Abstract verifies matched controls and scorer validation; title/authors copied into supplemental.bib. Our sentence uses only that abstract-supported scope, not an invented comparative performance claim. The page reports a workshop association; the draft cites the arXiv version and does not infer main-track acceptance.
- Agent Evaluation Reliability: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00651v1, submitted 30 September 2026. Abstract distinguishes complete-system from underlying-model reliability and uses a Bayesian decomposition. Supplemental bibliography added; the paper does not borrow its numerical reliability estimates for MonopolyBench.

Additional search surfaced MERIT/AgoraBench, a Monopoly rule-changing research project and DealBench. These are leads, not fully inspected comparators. They do not justify a statement that no other work studies a feature. No novelty claim in this draft relies on their absence. Later full-team comparison may add inspected sources.

Version status above refers to the pages returned on this date, not a claim that no other version or project exists. Existing bibliography notes remain preserved in references_source.bib. No evaluation was run in response to these papers.
