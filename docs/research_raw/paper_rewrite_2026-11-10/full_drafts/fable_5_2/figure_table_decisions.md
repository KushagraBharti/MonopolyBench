# Figure and table decisions (Fable 5.2 full draft)

## Principles

1. One exhibit per step of the argument: bargaining (Figure 1), conversion (Figure 2), insolvency (Figure 3), inheritance (Figure 4).
2. Every figure has the same numbers in a table or in the caption, so nothing depends on color.
3. No exhibit shows winners first. The 29 October Figure 1 was a winner grid, and the critique is right that it primes a leaderboard reading. Wins appear in one sentence of Section 2.4 and in Appendix Table 8.
4. All table bodies in the appendix are generated by `tools/make_tables.py` from the JSON outputs. Main-text tables are short and are covered by `tools/check_manuscript.py`.

## Palette and style

All four figures were redrawn for this draft with `tools/make_figures.py` (matplotlib from the repository virtualenv). I did not reuse the shared `assets/*.pdf`. The 29 October palette (`#286787`, `#766392`, `#a66a35`) fails the dataviz validator: blue against purple has a protanopia ΔE of 2.1 and a normal-vision ΔE of 10.1. The new figures use slots 1 to 5 of the validated reference palette in fixed order (blue `#2a78d6`, orange `#eb6834`, aqua `#1baf7a`, yellow `#eda100`, magenta `#e87ba4`). The validator passes the three-slot and five-slot sets on a white surface for adjacent pairs. Three slots are below 3:1 contrast, which the rule allows only with visible labels, so every segment carries its count and every figure has a table. Text is in ink colors, never in a series color. Bars have a white gap between segments and no outline. Hover layers do not apply to a print PDF.

Rendering was inspected page by page at 100 dpi (`qa/render/`, regenerated by the commands in `handoff.md`). Two title collisions in the first render were fixed.
