I independently chose the full-paper architecture. The main story begins with bargaining for development, continues through execution and finance, and ends in creditor redistribution. The two figures are new renderings of existing retained rows, not new evaluations. All manuscript assets are local under `assets/`.

Seven contextual transcript incidents remain in the main paper. I dissent from the critique's proposed limit of two: the seven demonstrate different economically meaningful mechanisms, and G19/G12 provide correction and execution controls. They occupy about five pages as exact searchable LaTeX, with surrounding contextual prose. The disclosure incident moves to the appendix because its primary subject is channel integrity.

I omit the old spending scatter. Forty zero-receipt units are better stated explicitly than hidden by overplotting or presented as investment failures. Spending and receipts remain fully quantified, with inherited-development and ownership examples. I also omit network diagrams, which would invite interpretation of dense exchange as collusion. Typed cash/deed totals and terminal/nonterminal partitions carry the supported collective finding more directly.

No figure depicts a counterfactual winner, message effect, latent motive, model-ranking confidence interval, ROI or learning treatment effect. The chart generator is `check_existing_data.py`; input hashes are in `data_checks.json`.
