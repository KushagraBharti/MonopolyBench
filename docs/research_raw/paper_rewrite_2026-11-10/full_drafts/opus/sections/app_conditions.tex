\section{Response and information conditions}
\label{app:conditions}

\paragraph{Refusal chains.} The recorded Opus condition has 1,604 chains in which the first attempt is refused and one additional refusal on the second attempt of G2 d129. These are 1,605 refused attempts in 1,605 decisions. The action-only retry succeeds in most chains. Sixty action-only retries submit an invalid construction plan and end in an engine default, and G2 d129 reaches a default by a differently ordered chain. A default writes a synthetic private note, which then enters later memory. The configured fallback model was never invoked. Because the retry changes the prompt, the tool schema and the private-note requirement together, its success says nothing about which feature of the first request triggered the refusal.

\paragraph{Action-only prompt.} The action-only retry shortens the deception sentence to ``Deception in public chat is allowed.'' and instructs ``Do not include private\_thought or a reasoning transcript.'' This is a response-format instruction. It does not add an honesty requirement for provider-returned explanations, and the records do not establish which provider fields it controls. Opus therefore played most decisions without the private-honesty clause, not only without a private note.

\paragraph{Grok conflicts.} The 139 selected conflicts comprise 138 strict JSON name comparisons and one malformed-content case. Reading all attempts rather than selected ones adds nine conflicts in discarded attempts, which reconciles an earlier count of 147 strict-JSON conflicts. The raw non-streamed response bodies received from OpenRouter contain all 139 selected disagreements, and their content and tool names match the canonical attempt records.

\paragraph{Message delivery.} Of 6,882 nonempty public messages, 94 are dropped when consecutive building summaries keep an earlier message and 55 are consumed by a bankruptcy cash event without a timeline summary. Of the 6,733 that enter the timeline, 6,720 are observed in at least one opponent's later timeline. Repetition, private notes and trade-thread history mean that timeline omission is not the same as complete non-exposure.

\paragraph{Explanation availability.} Readable provider reasoning exists on at least one attempt in 4,834 decisions, and opaque (encrypted) reasoning artifacts in 4,016; the populations overlap, and no encrypted content was read. Availability on the selected attempt differs from availability anywhere in the chain. These inventories describe which channels exist; they do not correct for selection into refusal, recovery or survival.
