\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Economic simulations and board games.}
Vending-Bench measures long-horizon coherence in running a simulated vending business, and CEO-Bench asks agents to run a simulated startup for 500 days; both connect decisions to cash over long trajectories~\cite{backlund2025vendingbench,chen2026ceobench}. The Vending-Bench Arena communication study analyzes 2,583 emails among competing agents, checks extracted claims against simulator records, and reports that misaligned messages rise under low-inventory conditions~\cite{li2026arena}. FinBoardBench evaluates financial reasoning in Cashflow, Acquire and Monopoly with a focal model against fixed opponents, and reports that models favor immediate asset purchases over keeping enough cash~\cite{hu2026finboardbench}. Its Monopoly rules return buildings to the bank on creditor transfer, whereas ours pass them intact, which changes the stakes of a rival's insolvency. AgentMonopoly studies behavioral profiles, model size and persona instructions in the same game, records language and actions together, and finds that persona instructions change wording and some actions, though not consistently across decisions~\cite{wu2026agentmonopoly}. Its inspected trade interface accepts or rejects a single offer and its prompt forbids public messages that contradict the chosen action, so our counteroffer analysis and our authorized-deception contract have no direct counterpart there. DECE evaluates complete model--harness configurations across ten Eurogames and finds that the same model can perform differently across harnesses~\cite{zhang2026dece}, which supports treating response policy as part of the evaluated system. Earlier Monopoly work trained reinforcement-learning agents against fixed-policy opponents~\cite{bonjour2022monopolyrl}.

\paragraph{Negotiation and bargaining.}
Learned negotiation dialogue agents were studied before large language models~\cite{lewis2017dealornodeal}, and CICERO combined a language model with strategic planning to play Diplomacy at a human level~\cite{metafair2022cicero}. NegotiationArena varies behavioral instructions in bilateral games and shows that some tactics change negotiation outcomes~\cite{bianchi2024negotiationarena}. LLM-Stakeholders defines scorable multi-party, multi-issue negotiations~\cite{abdelnabi2024stakeholders}. Cattle Trade studies bluffing, bidding and bargaining among several LLM agents~\cite{muller2026cattletrade}. Two recent bilateral studies are close to our bargaining results. One finds that agents model a counterparty's preferences accurately but that agreements follow opening anchors more than true utility~\cite{cosentino2026counterparty}. Another benchmarks supply-chain negotiation against a Bayesian equilibrium and finds that the provider of each model predicts surplus division better than capability rank does~\cite{liang2026supplychain}. Our setting differs in the respect that drives the main result: a bilateral Monopoly trade changes the positions of two players outside it, and a completion strengthens its owner against everyone, a cost that a two-party utility does not capture.

\paragraph{Strategic reasoning benchmarks.}
GTBench and $\gamma$-Bench evaluate LLMs on game-theoretic tasks and multi-agent games with defined scoring~\cite{duan2024gtbench,huang2025gamabench}, and SPIN-Bench combines planning, board games and negotiation~\cite{yao2025spinbench}. They offer cleaner strategic targets than a complete Monopoly game, which instead lets bargaining, investment, liquidity and insolvency interact.

\paragraph{Deception, commitments and collusion.}
Park et al.\ survey deceptive behavior in AI systems~\cite{park2024aideception}, and Scheurer et al.\ show a model deceiving its overseer under business pressure in a simulated trading task~\cite{scheurer2023pressure}. MASK separates honesty from accuracy~\cite{ren2025mask}. Studies of public promises and repeated games examine when agents break commitments or lie~\cite{shi2026cheaptalk,shi2026agentslie}; Empty Commitments separates promises a runtime cannot fulfill from feasible promises not acted on~\cite{empty_commitments_v2}; and work on standing over commitments asks who may release a promise in multi-party chat~\cite{commitment_standing}. LLM pricing agents can reach supracompetitive prices~\cite{fish2024collusion}, and collusion can emerge in long-horizon cooperative tasks~\cite{shi2026emergentcollusion}. Our task authorizes public deception and assigns no standing-offer norm, so our incidents describe conduct and recipient responses rather than violations. Work on explanation faithfulness and monitorability explains why we treat private notes and provider explanations as fallible channels~\cite{turpin2023unfaithful,lanham2023faithfulness,chen2025cotfaithful,korbak2025monitorability}.

\paragraph{Evaluating complete systems.}
Agent evaluation increasingly treats the model, scaffold and policy as one system. AgentBoard argues for process-level measurement~\cite{ma2024agentboard}; ``AI Agents That Matter'' and the Holistic Agent Leaderboard emphasize cost, reproducibility and harness effects~\cite{kapoor2024agentsthatmatter,kapoor2026hal}. Hardy et al.\ find that rankings of fixed model--scaffold systems are far more reliable than rankings of underlying models~\cite{hardy2026reliability}, consistent with our decision to describe configured systems. Yang et al.\ audit leaderboard contamination claims with matched controls and scorer validation and decline conclusions their evidence cannot support~\cite{yang2026measurementfirst}. Agarwal et al.\ show how few-run evaluations can mislead without attention to variability~\cite{agarwal2021precipice}. We do not claim novelty for replay, failure logging, state-grounded claim checking or counterfactual restoration, all of which appear in the work above. What this paper adds is an account of how one cohort of configured agents bargained over rival completion, converted completion into construction, financed or failed to finance debts, and passed assets on in bankruptcy, with each link checked against the legal options and the information actually delivered.
