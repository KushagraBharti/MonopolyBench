\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Design.} Twenty games from five seed blocks are one observational cohort of four configured systems. Cyclic rotations balance seats but cover four of the 24 seat orders, every configuration faces the same three opponents, and shared seeds stop matching opportunities once actions diverge. Decisions and offers within a game are dependent, so their counts are not independent trials. We report finite-cohort counts and do not attach confidence intervals that would imply a sampling design the cohort lacks. Nothing here ranks the underlying models.

\paragraph{Conditions.} The Opus refusal condition, Grok's required tool choice, the trade-history formatter, message delivery gaps and unequal text channels are part of what was evaluated. They limit comparisons across configurations, and their effects cannot be estimated from these games. The cause of the refusal shift between games 1--4 and 5--20 is unresolved. The origin of Grok's content/tool conflicts is localized to the received response but not further.

\paragraph{Rules.} Results about financing and inheritance hold for this implementation: intact creditor inheritance, per-entry even-building checks, finite stock, declaration permitted while payable, and a one-dollar redemption overcharge at some principals. Engine-grounded facts are facts about this ruleset.

\paragraph{Measurement.} Mechanical ledgers reconcile with the records, but the completion cells are ownership facts, not values, and the counter analysis describes terms, not intentions. Legal-sequence witnesses prove feasibility of a stated endpoint and nothing about optimality, survival or what any player would have done next. Negative search is certified only where no building sale is offered. Semantic results (the 74 rejection reasons and the 240-record temporal coding) come from LLM readers from one model family, with a partly unblinded second reader and no human annotation. Several definitions were refined during exploration and were not preregistered.

\paragraph{Selection and authorship.} The incidents are purposive and several involve Grok, whose text channels make conflicts visible. They support no prevalence estimate or moral ranking. LLM research-assistance runs from model families that overlap with evaluated participants performed earlier extraction and review, and this draft was written by an Opus-family run. Other drafts written in parallel will check this one.

\paragraph{Scope.} The paper does not establish causal persuasion, intent, durable learning, opponent-model accuracy, transfer to unfamiliar opponents or games, optimal play, the relation between general and multi-agent capability, or a decomposition of skill and luck. An unpayable debt at elimination does not justify or condemn the choices that led to it, and concentration of estates in winners does not measure an effect of inheritance on winning.
