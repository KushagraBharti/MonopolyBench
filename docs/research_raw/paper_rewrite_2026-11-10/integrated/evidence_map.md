# Evidence map

Paths below are relative to the parent rewrite package unless stated. The manuscript uses the same game and zero-based decision mapping as the retained twenty-game cohort.

## Status vocabulary

- **Retained:** an earlier recorded audit or semantic coding, not rerun for this rewrite.
- **Re-tabulated:** recomputed from an existing ledger, without returning to all canonical events.
- **Original-field checked:** a named raw source field or event read and matched to the excerpt or statement.
- **Document checked:** text, tables, figures or PDF inspected; not an independent scientific verification.

## Presentation and source preservation

`tools/verify_document.py` reloads the original fields for every quoted card used in the integrated text, checks exact excerpt spans and their rendered presence, checks 272 protected historical files and 81 frozen independent-draft files, and tests log diagnostics, page bounds and PDF Launch actions. It renders contact sheets for all pages. `qa/document_checks.json` stores the checked PDF hash. Its result is document correspondence, not validation of the semantic labels or gameplay claims.

`source_manifest.json` records the hashes of original copied inputs. The integrated preamble and supplemental bibliography are intentionally modified derivatives. Twenty-five metadata labels (eight Fable and seventeen original transcript definitions) are corrected to full field by `tools/prepare_quote_labels.py`; the verifier checks this exact transformation and complete original content apart from unprinted surrounding whitespace. The DECE title follows the plural paper-body title, with singular abstract metadata recorded in the adjudication. All other reference-library text and quoted content are preserved. A final-file manifest is separate so a source-input hash is never mistaken for a hash of edited output.

`tools/check_retained_tables.py` independently re-tabulates the offer categories, recipient response styles and completion-route/winner cells printed in the main text. It also reloads original G2 event 1566. Its report is `qa/retained_table_checks.json`; it does not recompute the entire economic census from events.

`tools/check_opus_followup.py` additionally checks the two directions of bankruptcy-related received-name conflicts, the thirteen Opus low-cash completing sellers, the fifty-one rent-earning trade episodes and the nineteen winner-recipient terminal estates. G13's terminal estate goes to the bank. These are retained-ledger checks, not fresh canonical replays.

`tools/check_fable_followup.py` independently checks the same-predicate counter comparison (92/111 versus 87/110 requested-deed retention; 79/111 versus 81/110 identical noncash bundles with increased cash), 309/313 buyer payments without completion, and the 31/31/8/4 configuration composition of the seventy-four reason-coded rejections. It reads original responses for all three opening author-only acceptances: G3 d194 names acceptance in content and call; G16 d247 and G18 d083 are retained conflicts. It checks the overlapping subsets of nineteen accepted author-only offers. None of these checks validates the semantic sixty-one-code agreement.

The same script reads original G2 d197 cash and d215 debt, the turn-72 bankruptcy cash and estate-acquisition events, G13's turn-111 next movement and turn-114 auction purchase, and retained group episodes for the block-4 orange paths. G13's green completion at event 1911 is a realized consequence of acquiring Pennsylvania, not evidence of an optimal trade. No trajectory is simulated.

`qa/literature/sources.json` records new primary-source verification: AgentMonopoly's pinned engine at commit `2434f4278bf2970237ba82399db191e0116f3d35` (with archived raw source and hash), and FinBoardBench v1 §3.2, Appendix C.3 and Appendix E.
