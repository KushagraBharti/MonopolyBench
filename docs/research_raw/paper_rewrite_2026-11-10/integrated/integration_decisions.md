## Corrections to the new Opus analysis

`tools/check_opus_followup.py` independently reads actual prompts, the offer ledger and exact board keys. It does not run the engine or construct a hypothetical trajectory.

1. The review script maps `B_O_RAILROAD` using the first board name beginning with B, which is Baltic Avenue. B&O's price is $200 and mortgage is $100, not $60/$30. This changes G16 Opus's available outside collateral from $130 to $200.
2. The review sums an unbuilt deed even when another deed in its group has buildings. The actual mortgage menu excludes Atlantic in the two G1 windows, reducing outside-group collateral from $335 to $205 and from $260 to $130. Both corrected budgets still cover the stated target. Using the current menu also avoids asserting legality from individual deed level alone.
3. The review's “pure cash-for-deed” filter includes jail-card transfers. The integrated comparison excludes those fifteen offers and uses correct board prices. Its four pooled sample sizes are 131, 176, 87 and 113, rather than 134, 178, 93 and 117. This is a disclosed definition correction, not a silent disagreement over medians. Board prices remain denominators, not strategic values.
4. The two budgets that fall short are not certified as having no own-asset route. The new analysis does not execute all sale, mortgage, redemption or trade sequences. Its negative statement stays within the displayed mortgage-only budget.
5. The terminal estate count is nineteen to winners and one to the bank, as directly recounted from the retained flow ledger. “Twenty terminal winner creditors by construction” is not valid under this ruleset.

The aggregate two/seven financing counts survive the corrections. They do not license promotion of every no-option window to a proven deliberate hold. The main result remains a decomposition of observed menus and resolution records, supplemented by stated financing deferrals.
