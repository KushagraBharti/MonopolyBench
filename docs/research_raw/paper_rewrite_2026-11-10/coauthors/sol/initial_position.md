# Independent initial position

Written before reading any new coauthor output, 2026-11-10. I read the rewrite contract first and the full supplied critique. This position is independent of the new peer drafts.

The behavioral paper should explain the passage from bargaining over rival development to executable economic control. Monopoly makes a deal valuable through its effects on both parties' complete groups, remaining reserves, and subsequent construction. Those gains can later move again through liquidation and creditor inheritance. The retained offer ledger can describe how responses differ with those stakes. It cannot estimate the effect of an isolated rival benefit, because prices, holdings, responders, and histories differ at the same time.

I agree with the critique that the recipient-completion result is too weak as the headline. I disagree with redirecting the whole paper toward an unvalidated auditing method. The stronger existing-data result is the joint distribution of author and recipient completions, accompanied by configuration-specific response styles and state-grounded examples. This deserves prominence only if the stronger contrast survives re-tabulation and is presented with its selection boundaries. I will check each configuration rather than inherit consistency claims.

Economic execution belongs in the same argument. A completed group does not automatically become a rent-producing position; legal building opportunities, submitted allocations, representation errors, response resolution, and realized construction intervene. Financial distress does not establish that earlier investment was wise, and three payable eliminations do not establish that their players could have won. The first-management and current-debt denominators must remain separate. Creditor inheritance describes a consequential change in control, with terminal transfers separated from usable opportunities.

I would cut most learning material from these sections. A short realized target chronology can demonstrate the connection between prospective reserves and later execution. It cannot establish learning or theory of mind. I would retain configuration differences as descriptions of deployed systems and avoid a model ranking. The 61/74 rival-reason statistic must explicitly identify LLM-assisted semantic coding and its dependence on prior definitions and partial unblinding.

Planned contributions are finished bargaining and economics prose, compact native tables generated from the retained rows, a brief methods insert, source and arithmetic checks, and a handoff. I will not run engines, models, games, reference policies, or new controlled evaluations. I will write only in this directory and will not alter any baseline or canonical evidence.
