# Assessment of the additional referee feedback

11 November 2026. The [complete supplied review](user_review.md) is archived unchanged in substance. Its scores and venue predictions are opinions, not verified outcomes. This assessment builds on the preceding critique and the existing whole-paper reviews. It does not revise the manuscript or authorize new evaluations.

## The issues that matter

1. **The paper needs a clearer scientific finding about agents.** Bargaining, building and inheriting is a useful narrative, but familiar game mechanics cannot do most of the work in the abstract. The stronger material concerns bargaining over a rival's future options, cash constraints, withholding completing deeds, third-party resale and creditor incentives. The next analysis must distinguish those observations from ordinary haggling and the rules' mechanical consequences.
2. **Considering a strategic issue is not the same as handling it well.** A refusal mentioning rival development establishes a stated consideration, not an optimal decision. A settled bluff establishes a bluff and a settlement, not successful persuasion. Current-debt feasibility can be checked precisely; overall economic judgment needs a different reference. Keep those levels distinct while stating the observed conduct directly.
3. **The headline economic comparisons need comparable opportunities.** Early-built groups have different time to earn rent, acquisition dates, property composition and survival. Estate recipients may already have strong positions before the transfer. The preceding plan to examine timing, exposure, all debt episodes and pre-estate positions remains appropriate using existing records. Its results, not a predetermined narrative, should determine the headline.
4. **The effective participant conditions change what behavior means.** Opus's refusal recovery removes private notes and a corrective opportunity. Grok's executed calls sometimes contradict its text. Missing board parameters and misleading history affect the information available. These conditions also affect the opponents interacting with those systems; deleting one participant's rows does not recover an unaffected experiment. Report the essential conditions briefly before the results and preserve the actual exchanges.
5. **Reliability and generality remain limited.** Five seed blocks, a fixed roster, repeated offers and LLM-assisted labels do not support stable capability rankings, calibrated theory of mind or learning claims. Quantitative uncertainty must match a stated target and the dependence structure. Naive offer-level significance tests cannot cure these limitations. Existing coding definitions and agreement should be made accessible without claiming human validation.
6. **The presentation makes the answers harder to see.** Give the paper a finding-led abstract, explicit contribution, clear prompt example and economic trajectory figures. Introduce the user's questions before the coverage table. Keep eight contextual incidents, but cut repeated caveats and duplicate interpretation. Human authorship and submission packaging must be settled before publication; neither is supplied by an LLM review.

## The most important new evidence

The Opus refusal explanation is a substantive reporting addition. Root's fresh read-only census confirms that all 1,605 refusals carry the same restriction message concerning reverse engineering or duplicating outputs. Anthropic's [current Opus documentation](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/opus-5-5/whats-new-opus-5-5) describes refusals of requests to reproduce internal reasoning. The archived standard prompt requests honest private thoughts. This supports a likely prompt/provider incompatibility; the saved responses lack the specific classifier field, so it does not prove the precise trigger. The chronology also changes within G1–G4, not only across the code-version boundary.

This should replace vague treatment of the refusal explanation in the next revision. It should not become a claim that Opus refused deception, lost all memory, or that all its trades and failures were caused by the private-note requirement.

## What we should not import from the review

- **A predetermined rejection or acceptance verdict.** The scores and venue table are a referee's judgment. We have not independently checked the entire new literature and venue comparison.
- **The $218 cost interpretation.** It matches adding overlapping OpenRouter cost fields. The known upstream inference subtotal is approximately $141.71, with unpriced calls; a BYOK-aware field selection yields approximately $141.39. Neither establishes invoiced total spending or why twenty games were chosen.
- **Reasoning tokens as cognition.** The reported means reproduce, but zero reported tokens do not contradict a medium request setting or prove no reasoning. The 69,595 maximum is completion tokens, not reasoning tokens.
- **No coding protocol.** Definitions, a semantic population and row-level labels exist in the archive. Their presence does not solve the absence of independent validation.
- **A deterministic engine as an optimal-policy oracle.** Historical replay and local feasibility are narrower than the value of an alternative strategy against responding opponents.
- **All economic findings as tautologies.** Voluntary bankruptcy need not mean insolvency, and asset concentration is not fixed in advance. The issue is their scientific prominence and interpretation, not that every such count is meaningless.
- **Permission to deceive as proof that no interaction pattern can emerge.** The prompt prevents presenting permitted bluffing as spontaneous instruction misalignment. It does not prescribe every specific negotiation, withholding tactic or coordination appeal.
- **A compulsory auditing-paper pivot, two-transcript limit, or new experiments.** Those do not follow from the user's instructions. Prompt changes, repairs, reruns, ablations, new baselines, rollout evaluations and new labeling campaigns remain unexecuted.

## What remains from the previous plan

Keep the behavioral identity and all eight research questions plus A–C at their supported levels. Existing-record priorities remain recipient-by-stage bargaining, ask-versus-bid differences, development timing and rent exposure, successful as well as failed debt episodes, and pre-estate strength. Add concise corrections for refusal explanation, information supplied, compute reporting and cost accounting. Do not promise that these analyses will establish a premium, a tactic effect or a general capability relationship.

The paper's strength should come from explaining strategic interaction. Audits support those explanations; they should not take over the paper. Material limits stay visible, while repetitive diagnostic detail moves out of the main argument.
