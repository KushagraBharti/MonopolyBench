# Astra whole-paper integration review

**Historical scope:** this judgment precedes the final peer reports. Its closing statement about unresolved defects was superseded by those reports. The original PDF pin is archived in `../pinned_before_final_edits/`; the final integrated revision and all dispositions are now complete. See the Sol, Opus and Fable adjudications beside this file.

11 November 2026. Written before the three fresh integrated-paper reviews.

The integrated manuscript is a complete observational paper, not a proposal for future evaluations or a generally validated auditing method. I wrote and read the full synthesis, reconciled all 27 Sol, 33 Opus and 40 Fable cross-review findings against it, and inspected every rendered page. The checked PDF and source hashes are pinned in `integrated/qa/`.

The strongest contribution is the connected account: completing a group, financing construction, executing it, receiving rent, meeting a debt and receiving a debtor's estate are different events. The corpus records those transitions, and its transcripts explain the economic stakes participants discuss. Four comparable completion totals lead to sharply different first-window construction totals under recorded response conditions. That is useful behavioral evidence without a claim of optimal play or clean model comparison.

Bargaining needed the most substantive revision. The pooled rejection contrast is now accompanied by thread position and configuration. Counter retention is paired with a non-completing population using the same predicate. Price and cash-direction cuts are exploratory appendix descriptions. The sixty-one agreed textual reasons are not claimed as a causal explanation of the aggregate contrast, and their seventy-four-record population is dominated by Grok and Gemini. Nineteen accepted author-only offers retain their actual outcomes while exposing response conflicts and recovery conditions.

The financing result has stronger local support than the general behavioral comparisons. Its negative classifications have an explicit generous bound, three positives have inherited executed witnesses, and the hotel case uses a restricted search. I re-read sources and re-tabulated retained outputs, but did not re-run any engine case. Static budgets for nine no-option windows are deliberately not presented as executed witnesses. My checks corrected errors in Opus's proposed collateral and list-price comparisons before adoption. This distinction must survive final review.

The incidents now connect to their economic populations. Eight main cases include a settled and a refused bluff, refusal of own completion, factual correction with acceptance, a financial-denial appeal, contradictory bankruptcy, an omission pitch and withdrawal of repeated terms. They are selected, channel-asymmetric cases under a deception-permitting prompt. G2's arithmetic error and $44 rent, G12's insufficient two-sale request, and G13's earlier appeal, alternate financing and later auction completion are stated directly. No outcome is used to infer sincerity, internal intent or causal persuasion.

I retain G7 despite the seven-case preference of Opus and Fable: its settled final-price bluff provides a clear contrast to G2. I accept Fable's conversion emphasis in the abstract and discussion, but retain chronological result order. I reject the interpretation that shared early purchase paths prove that dice determined seven games. I also reject interpreting close counter proportions as equivalent bargaining behavior across all deeds.

Verification performed in this integration: original quoted-field and span checks; targeted recipient-delivery checks inherited from my all-four review; new original G2 and G13 records; retained offer, counter, estate, episode and menu re-tabulations; source inspection of replay canonicalization; primary-source checks of pinned AgentMonopoly code and FinBoardBench v1; clean LuaLaTeX compilation; all-page contact inspection plus detailed revised pages. No game, engine action, model evaluation, synthetic test or transfer evaluation was run. Mechanical census validity and semantic agreement beyond these checks remain inherited, with scope marked in the evidence map.

The material limits remain a fixed confounded cohort, retrospective LLM-assisted selection/coding, no causal strategy evaluation, no learning or transfer experiment and no general-versus-multi-agent capability axis. These are not formatting defects or unfinished promised analyses. The paper now states its positive observations and maps all eleven motivating questions to the level supported, including unanswered questions. It cannot claim to have resolved their broader forms.

No unresolved factual defect was found by my current checks. The fresh reviewers should challenge that conclusion across the entire document, especially newly connected claims, denominator changes, inherited-versus-performed distinctions and the clarity of the positive scientific argument. They should report exact corrections rather than manufacture a numerical perfection score or ask for forbidden experiments.
