          11 Conclusion
          Thetwentygamesshowagentsbargainingoverrivaldevelopmentandprospectiveestates,followed
          by different paths from ownership to construction, debt payment and creditor transfer. Counters
          commonlypreserveandrepricetransactionswhetherornottheycompleteagroup. Recordeddevel-
          opmentdependsonbotheconomicaccessandresponseexecution. Mostcurrenteliminationdebts
          are unpayable, while distinct exceptions expose omitted collateral, a received-call conflict and a hotel
          lock. Contextual transcripts connect those mechanisms to blufÏng, correction, omission and changed
          assurances. Following the transactions through these stages explains behavior that deal counts and
          final outcomes alone leave unresolved. The result is a source-grounded account of strategic economic
          interaction in these configurations, with local evidence that remains distinct from causal persuasion,
          optimal investment and victory.
