8.2
Execution conditions belong in the behavioral result
The same record contains achieved targets, stated reserve choices and failed construction requests. A
win count or an unbuilt-group count alone cannot distinguish them. Reordered witnesses establish
that all sixty-one defaulted Opus allocations were reachable at their historical states, while the original
encodings failed. Received Grok calls sometimes create negotiations, end construction or declare
bankruptcy against another channel’s requested action. These are properties of the observed system,
with concrete consequences for the game.
Calling the unit a complete configuration is necessary but does not remove confounding. Opus’s
provider recovery changes the prompt, retry budget and later memory. Grok’s channel conflict has
unresolved origin. Misleading histories can coexist with correct current holdings. Dropping affected
records would select a different population rather than reconstruct ordinary play under a corrected
interface. We therefore retain actual transactions and carry their conditions into the relevant counts
and incidents.
Historical feasibility is most informative when the question is narrow. The fifty-six upper-bound
negatives settle own-asset payment at those debts. Three witnesses establish available payment routes.
The hotel lock separates reachable finance from nominal assets. None settles whether prior spending
was wise, whether payment was optimal, or which player would win under another action. This local
precision supports the behavioral account without making an unvalidated claim for a general auditor.
