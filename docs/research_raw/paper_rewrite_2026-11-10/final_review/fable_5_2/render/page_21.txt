Outcome patterns illustrate why the distinction matters. Sol wins one four-rotation block from
every seat. In another block, all four configurations win from seat 2, whose player purchases both
dark blues by turn 29. In G13–15, seat 1 completes orange through direct purchase on turn 47 under
Opus, Sol and Gemini respectively, and wins each game. In the fourth rotation, G16, seat 1 is Grok: it
completes orange by trade on turn 116 and loses. Thus seven games share early same-seat purchase
paths across different configurations, while four follow Sol across seats. The purchases, subsequent
decisions and random events are all part of the observed paths; these patterns do not establish that
early dice decided seven outcomes. Across the cohort Sol wins eight games, Gemini seven, Opus three
and Grok two. Neither pattern isolates strategic skill or chance, and no independent general-capability
axis is measured.

Related work
Negotiation and economic agency.
NegotiationArena studies bargaining through controlled tactic
changes [2]. Recent work distinguishes expressed counterparty modeling from better agreements,
including experiments that supply coarse ranked preference information [3]. MERIT studies feed-
back for bargaining under specified buyer and seller utilities [9]. Those designs provide criteria for
agreement quality that our multi-player game does not. Our response and cash tables describe the
exchanges proposed, while exact counter transitions show how their terms change; neither supplies an
optimal-utility reference.
Vending-Bench studies sustained operation of a simulated business [1]. Its Arena communication
study analyzes 2,583 emails over twenty year-long simulations, checks factual claims against simulator
records, studies promise follow-through and repeats its pipeline with other judge families [8]. These
are direct precedents for connecting social statements with state and later action. Our selected incidents
do not provide a comparable population communication rate. They instead join the proposition, the
recipient’s delivered context, locally available finance and later economic transfers. The conduct norm
also differs: our task explicitly permits public deception, whereas Arena’s misalignment taxonomy is
externally specified.
Games and configured-system evaluation.
AgentMonopoly examines model families, sizes and
personas through Monopoly actions and language [13]. The repository implementation inspected
at commit 2434f427 accepts or rejects single offers and eliminates at negative cash without the debt-
liquidation step studied here; it also transfers buildings intact to a player-creditor [11]. These are
implementation findings, distinguished from the paper’s reported experiments. Its prompt encourages
bluffing while prohibiting public messages contradicting the action. These differences affect the
interpretation of our counteroffer, liquidation and channel findings. Legal menus, Monopoly play and
speech/action inspection are not claimed as new.
