Interpretation and validation.
Work on explanation faithfulness motivates separating recorded
reasons from internal computation [7, 12]; MASK distinguishes honesty from factual accuracy [10].
Studies of agent-evaluation reliability and measurement-focused auditing use designs beyond recon-
struction of a single corpus [4, 14]. Our checks license specified historical classifications, not a generally
validated semantic metric, monitoring system or audit method. The contribution is the connected
economic account and its inspectable evidence under explicit conditions.

Limitations and research provenance
The cohort has five seeds, four cyclic rotations per seed and a fixed opponent roster. States and
responses are dependent, opponent identities are visible, and source revisions and provider conditions
are not assigned treatments. The full configuration is the appropriate descriptive unit, but this does
not establish model rankings or generalization to corrected interfaces, new opponents or other games.
The ruleset matters. Intact creditor inheritance, per-entry building validation, limited bank houses
and an available bankruptcy declaration produce the opportunities discussed here. Replay tests
agreement with that implementation. Floating-point redemption charges exceed the mathematical
ten-percent charge by one dollar for some mortgage principals, and bundled building sales limit the
negative search. Appendix C preserves these details. A historical witness is not an independent
certification of intended rules.
The bargaining categories contain different assets, prices, players and stages. Cash-poor thresholds
are exploratory, and eventual-winner splits condition on outcomes shaped by the same trajectories. No
causal tactic effect, optimal price, incremental investment return or payoff decomposition is identified.
Competent local actions and concrete errors can nevertheless be described without an optimal complete-
game policy.
Semantic evidence is retrospective and selected, and channels are unevenly observable. LLM
research assistants selected the incidents and developed coding categories after inspecting the records.
Sol and Opus assistants use the same model identifiers as two evaluated participants, under different
research tasks and settings. Two partly unblinded reason readings and repeated coauthor review are
not independent human validation. Six of the eight main incidents involve Grok, whose channels are
unusually observable; this selection cannot rank conduct. Exact quotes, source hashes, delivery checks
and disagreement records support scrutiny, not an inference of intent from text alone.
Human-directed research used Astra, Sol, Opus and Fable instances. The complete rewrite began
with four independent full manuscripts by Astra, Sol, Opus and Fable 5.2, followed by whole-paper
cross-review; earlier work includes Fable 5.1. These are research roles, not additional gameplay partici-
pants. The archive distinguishes original-source inspection, retained-ledger re-tabulation, inherited
engine executions and LLM-assisted semantic judgments. No new games or controlled evaluations
were added to this cohort. Public checkpoint and subsequent manuscript provenance are specified in
Appendix E.
