B
Economic units and the complete elimination ledger
Unit
Definition and boundary
Attempt
One saved provider response, including refused or invalid responses.
Decision
One engine menu resolved into one applied action, possibly through several
attempts.
Offer
One current proposal or counter awaiting the other party’s response.
Completion episode
Ownership of every deed in a color group, from completion until loss or game
end.
First management window
The owner’s first actual management turn after completion, including all its
management decisions.
Spending unit
One game/player/color triple with positive own construction expenditure.
Elimination
One declared or automatic bankruptcy.
