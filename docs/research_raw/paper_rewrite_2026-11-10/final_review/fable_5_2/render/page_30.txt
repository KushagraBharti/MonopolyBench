Transfer interest and redemption both use floating-point ceilings. Multiplying mortgage principal
by 0.1 agrees with the exact board-principal transfer charge. Multiplying by 1.1 for redemption exceeds
the exact mathematical ceiling by one dollar for principals $50, $90, $100, $110 and $200. A $100
mortgage therefore costs $111 to redeem in this implementation. Historical alternatives use that
actual charge. The present paper does not establish whether each overcharge changed an affordability
boundary.
Build validation checks evenness after each plan entry. Sale validation can accept a bundled
operation from final evenness and net stock. An atomic one-building-at-a-time search can therefore
miss a legal bundled sale. The corrected checker refuses negative graph certificates in states offering
building sales. Its retained negative graph result is the G7 mortgage-only state. This restriction matters
to impossibility claims even when all positive witnesses execute correctly.
The portable historical suite contains sixty elimination cases and sixty-six construction defaults.

raw records. A successful reproduction cannot supply those missing claims by itself.
