What happened. No sale occurs at $250. Subsequent $150 and $225 offers also fail. Opus later
receives Sol’s estate and wins. The earlier assurance is present in both focal requests at invocation and
withdrawal.
What this shows. The record shows a repeated assurance, its invocation and changed terms after
financial deterioration. The explanation cites the cost of prolonging a nearly bankrupt rival and of
redeeming and developing the group. That is a stated valuation, not an independently demonstrated
advantage of withdrawing. No costly action in reliance or dishonesty when the original offer was made
is identified. The engine supplies no binding standing-offer object, and the messages specify no expiry.
