6.1
Payable debt, genuine insolvency and inaccessible assets
The sixty eliminations include fifty-six declarations and four automatic terminations. In fifty-six cases,
the current debt exceeds the generous own-asset bound in Equation 1. No permitted sequence of
the debtor’s own sales and mortgages can pay those debts. Of the remaining four, three have legal
payment witnesses and one is blocked by liquidation rules despite sufficient nominal assets (Figure 2).
The payable cases have distinct evidence. Sol’s G12 d190 and Gemini’s G17 d234 accounts omit
collateral unlocked by building sales. Retained witnesses pay debts of $500 and $550 and leave $38
and $180 respectively. Cash plus immediately mortgageable deeds falls short in both, but that subtotal
is not the entire menu, which includes sales. The third case is a same-response conflict, shown below;
it can be resolved by house sales alone.
