The 526 threads include 414 with an earlier thread between the same pair in the same game. Among
opening offers, 524 permit acceptance and receive 32 accepts, 137 counters and 355 rejects. The 394
later offers receive 81 accepts, 256 counters and 57 rejects. The complete response population adds two
acceptance-ineligible openings, one countered and one rejected. Initiation propensity uses own turns
with a legal proposal opportunity: Sol proposes on 83/834, Gemini 16/766, Opus 160/660 and Grok
106/676. Multiple threads can begin in one turn, so these are not the thread totals in the main text.
The counter join follows every one of 394 counter responses to the next offer in its thread, checks
actor reversal and compares exact completed groups and noncash bundles. The 92/111 retained-
completion result concerns the same original author’s group. In the 88 counters leaving only that party
completing, negotiated cash changes agree with changes after transfer-interest accounting. Seventy-
nine are pure cash repricings of unchanged deeds and cards; nine change noncash terms too. These
distinctions prevent a larger cash request from being treated automatically as the price of an unchanged
asset.
The exploratory cash cuts use cash immediately before the offer, not net worth or maximum
accessible finance. Among cash settlements with sellers below thresholds of $50, $100, $150 and $200,
respectively, 14/20, 21/30, 22/36 and 24/40 complete the paying party’s group. The denominator
conditions on a low-cash seller and a settlement. It differs from the main-text 21/48, which conditions
on all payer-completing cash settlements. Opus is the cash receiver in sixteen of the thirty below-$100
settlements. These composition differences preclude a causal distress interpretation.
