## Fixed scope

- No new controlled evaluations, games, model evaluation calls, engine branches, synthetic tests, reference-policy runs or transfer tests. Existing-log re-tabulations, source inspection, literature verification and document compilation are allowed.
- Preserve all four independent drafts, the prior manuscript, the original outline and literature review, canonical games, engine and prompts. No commit or push in this stage.
- No subagents. Each reviewer does the complete review personally. Use only the assigned `reviews/<author>/` directory for writes and isolated compilation products.
- Do not message completed peers or revive their completed delegate runs. Report to the parent through the task result.
- No paper can truthfully be guaranteed perfect, 10/10 or superior to every comparator. Resolve substantive faults and preserve the remaining empirical limits.

## Every author's task

Read ALL FOUR manuscripts, including main sections, appendices, tables, transcript cards and handoffs. They are not assigned by domain. Write an initial all-four judgment before reading Astra's review to reduce anchoring, then read that review and challenge it. Inspect the strongest new claims against retained data or primary source records. Do not relabel inherited engine checks as a fresh execution.

## Required output

1. `initial_position.md`, written after all-four reading and before Astra's review.
2. `review.md`, a whole-paper comparison including a central thesis, strongest evidence, major objections to every draft including one's own, all-section recommendations, three likely rejection reasons, and explicit dissent from Astra where warranted.
3. `findings.json`, stable finding IDs with severity, exact source passage, evidence and proposed resolution, distinguishing performed checks from inherited results.
4. `integration_proposal.md`, a complete-paper architecture and specific keep/cut/rewrite decisions across setup, methods, every result, conduct, discussion, limitations, related work, abstract/title and appendix. Do not edit the user's original outline.
5. `replacement_prose.tex`, finished replacement prose for material changes that need it. This should make a coherent whole-paper proposal, not a domain handoff; it need not duplicate unchanged pages.
6. `verification.md`, source and render scope, limitations, citations inspected and any errors of the reviewer found and corrected.

## Questions all reviewers should attack

- Which positive scientific finding survives the observational design and changes what a reader learns about strategic economic interaction?
- How should rejection, counteroffer changes, cash direction and stated reasons be combined without inventing a uniform rival effect, agreement-bias verdict or price premium?
- How should the never-legal, default, conflict and valid-end populations be presented without turning no observed option into financing impossibility or defaults into a measured amount of lost rent?
- Which inheritance population belongs in the headline, given 69 terminal completions and 52 with further play? Which trajectories connect it to bargaining without causal survival claims?
- Which seven or eight distinct contextual incidents best answer the user's interests, with source channels, factual corrections, exposure and outcomes? Compare the new G2 and G15 cases with G7 and G11. Keep exact quotes even when their arithmetic is wrong, and say that it is wrong.
- What belongs in the main text versus an evidence appendix? How can repeated caveats be consolidated without losing material limits?
- Does every claim about another paper match the inspected version and section? Abstract-only knowledge is not a full-paper comparison.
- Are authorship, LLM-assisted coding, configuration conditions, source hashes and executable-check provenance candidly described?

The parent will integrate after these reviews, preserve dissent and run document and source checks. No independent draft becomes the shared manuscript by default.
