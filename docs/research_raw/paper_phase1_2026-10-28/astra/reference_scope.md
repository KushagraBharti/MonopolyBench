# Primary reference anchors

These are scope notes for the user's twelve requested anchors, checked against primary pages during phase 1. Abstract-level checks below are not a claim to have completed the later full-paper literature comparison. Suggested relevance is our interpretation, not an assertion that another benchmark lacks an unmentioned feature.

| Reference | Verified focus | Use in our paper |
|---|---|---|
| [Vending-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.15840) | Sustaining a simulated vending business over long runs, with inventory, ordering, pricing and fees. The original paper also motivates capital acquisition as a safety-relevant capability. | Lead motivation for persistent economic competence and failures over time. It does not make our cohort a test of alignment change as capabilities increase. |
| [AgentMonopoly v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.29330v2) | Multiplayer Monopoly behavior, model-size comparisons and persona instructions; language, actions and outcomes. | Closest direct comparison. Table 3 lists 40 + 40 + 40 + 20 games, totaling 140. The earlier 160 claim should be corrected. Cyclic seat orders and repeated seeds are already present. |
| [SOTOPIA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.11667) | Role-play social goals, coordination, collaboration and competition, assessed through SOTOPIA-Eval. | Social competence and goals are distinct from a general alignment judgment. Compare coding and evaluation methods in the full review. |
| [Concordia](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.03664) | A library for generative agent-based simulation, using component-based agents, memory and a game-master role. | Supports the broader simulation question. Describe the difference between language-mediated environment adjudication and our deterministic game rules precisely. |
| [TheAgentCompany](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.14161) | A self-contained simulated software company with websites, code, programs and coworker communication. | A reference for consequential work and persistent task context. Our game abstraction does not establish comparable workplace realism. |
| [AgentBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.03688) | Interactive agent evaluation across eight environments, including reasoning, decisions and instruction following. | Locate MonopolyBench within agent evaluation and distinguish outcome measures from trajectory diagnosis. |
| [WebArena](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.13854) | Reproducible functional websites and tasks evaluated by task-completion correctness. | Environment reproducibility and outcome evaluation. Do not claim that resettable or reproducible environments are new. |
| [OSWorld](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.07972) | Real computer tasks with setup configurations and execution-based evaluators. | A reference for state setup and verifiable consequences; our restricted game tools do not test general GUI control. |
| [τ-bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.12045) | Tool-agent-user interactions under domain policies, final-state checking and repeated-trial reliability. | Specify which policies our agents were actually given. Repeated-trial reliability is different from winning one game. |
| [AgentDojo](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.13352) | Tool use over untrusted data, with prompt-injection attacks and defenses. | A direct example of an explicit security target and threat model. Our social interaction data alone do not demonstrate prompt-injection robustness. |
| [MLE-bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.07095) | Machine-learning engineering on competition tasks, with human leaderboard baselines and resource-scaling analysis. | Distinguish an environment's potential for experimentation from demonstrated training or research performance. |
| [METR task-completion time horizons](https://metr.org/time-horizons/) | Success probability as a function of task duration measured by human expert completion time. | Correct the shorthand that it measures how long an agent literally runs before failing. Our turns, decisions and tokens are different units. |

The full related-work phase must also retain FinBoardBench, Cattle Trade, AgentBoard, NegotiationArena, the promise-breaking papers, and other relevant earlier sources. A comparison should name a measured or implemented advantage and its scope. It should not use an unsupported overall superiority claim.
