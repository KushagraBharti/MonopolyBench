# Astra's drafting position

This is a complete new manuscript, not a replacement outline. The result is an empirical paper about sustained economic capability and social conduct plus an evaluation method. It must give actual trajectories, resource consequences and social contrasts enough space to explain agents' behavior. Instrument faults clarify the affected results; they should not consume the whole narrative.

The method contribution is exact-prefix legal-alternative auditing, coupled to full-player accounting and proposition-level social chronology. It is not generic replay novelty. A legal route answers a local feasibility question, not who would have won. An explanation is an observed statement, not privileged intent. Shared dice are not shared opportunities. Public deception is permitted in this cohort, so the broader alignment motivation must become a precise discussion of norms and experimental opportunities, not an unsupported misalignment score.
