# Coding and endpoint scope

This is a fresh manual read of all 240 fixed public-unflagged records. The selection was made in phase 2, before this extension. It contains three SHA-ranked decisions for each player in each game. It is neither a random sample of all decisions nor a balanced sample of ordinary and noteworthy outcomes. Earlier review outcomes are not sampling strata.

The first proposed 80-row reduction was withdrawn before reading any scoped behavioral field. The full 240-row extraction, including empty fields, was then read in game order. Scripts never assign the semantic codes. `manual_coding.py` contains one authored interpretation for each row. `row_ledger.jsonl` joins these judgments to complete source text, source locations, hashes, the actual action, supplied menu, and later records.

## What the codes measure

The operational code P is a broad **forward-looking own-action passage**, not a count of executable plans. It includes contingent goals and one affordance-only borderline passage. This broad candidate screen prevents ambiguous statements from disappearing. Only the endpoint records identify an operational commitment. This clarifies the initial protocol's word "plan": the 59 positives must never be described as 59 executable plans or 59 scored adherence tests.

O is a passage about a prospective opponent action, capacity, exposure or outcome. The 36 positives are not 36 theory-of-mind predictions. `FORECAST_TYPE` separates movement, financial feasibility, hypothetical accepted-trade capacity, possible behavior, and unbounded outcome statements. Public-state arithmetic is not private-belief inference. A later failure to build does not make a statement that building was impossible true.

R marks an explicit textual correction, reference to rejection/feedback, or stated change of position. The 25 positives are not 25 demonstrated learning episodes. The earlier belief and causal exposure are not independently established for every R row. Four selected auction/negotiation chains connect such statements to original earlier and later actions. The parent's independent full-corpus feedback recurrence audit addresses a different unit and must not be pooled with R.

A marks an opponent or incentive assessment that lacks an independently scored prediction. F marks a definite relevant state, arithmetic, rule or affordance claim, without asserting correctness. H marks a reserve/retention preference. N means no eligible temporal or factual passage under the compact scheme; it does not mean no internal planning. Codes overlap. The code strings are not ordinal scores.

## Channels and authorship

Both public_message and applied private_thought were read in full. All 240 extracted actions match the canonical action record exactly. Private fields are absent in 53 records, all Opus. None of the 240 actions is an engine default; no literal fallback note occurs. If either were present, the synthetic field would be excluded from model-authored semantic evidence. Missing private text is not a negative planning label.

Provider summaries and assistant content are excluded from primary coding. Four sampled named-content/action conflicts are linked to the retained separate audit, explicitly marked as not reparsed here. The G16 d159 follow-up also belongs to that audit. Its same-record applied note/action disagreement is independently visible in the canonical action. Neither textual channel is privileged as intent.

## Endpoints and exclusions

Every one of the 59 P rows has a recorded disposition. Eight contain an explicit policy to buy named bank deeds if landed on or on sight. This text-defined subset is enumerated completely in `LANDING_POLICIES`. All later actual BUY_OR_AUCTION decisions by that actor are searched to game end; the first named-target opportunity is the endpoint. The broader phrase "a better open set" in G6 d37 is not converted into a named target. Repeated G6 statements remain two rows from one player-game. No-opportunity rows are censored, not failures.

Nineteen other P rows have illustrative source-backed chronologies. They were selected for interpretable endpoints and contrasts, not sampled independently of the result. There is no pooled adherence rate. Thirty-two P rows remain unscorable as single commitments because the condition, objective, price, horizon or starting-state account is insufficient. Their next twelve own decisions, or all remaining if fewer, are mechanically retained for inspection. This retained context is not a claim that every later passage was semantically adjudicated.

Four O rows have narrowly defined event checks. One is an auction outcome mechanically implied by cash limits. Another predicts remaining house stock: the first endpoint precedes any rival action because of doubles, while a later endpoint after rival actions also retains the stock. Neither validates every financing premise. Another combines a realized outbid with construction; the complete rival suffix through elimination has no delivered building menu after jail and debt intervene. This does not rule out unexecuted alternative financing sequences. The fourth predicts refusal without ever presenting an offer; it is unscored. No calibration, theory-of-mind accuracy or behavioral forecast rate is estimated.

## Provenance and tests

`source_hashes.json` freezes the 60 canonical action/decision/event files, the prior named-conflict audit, and the analysis scripts. Every witness records action and decision lines, emitted event lines, menus, cash and holdings before the action, and complete applied text. Field hashes permit exact comparison without relying on a prose summary. The source field itself is the complete retained quote for each row; short displayed excerpts are checked against it.

`audit_temporal.py` asserts sample identity and balance, exhaustive candidate disposition, actual opportunity counts, and selected endpoint amounts and actions. It does not rerun the engine or use new model calls. `validation.json` reports the performed checks. There is one fresh semantic coder. Prior project reviews mean this is exploratory and not blind. Reviewer agreement and causal learning require separate evidence.

- An initial reading of G17 d228 as abandoning its99 bid ceiling was wrong. The opponent jumped to100, making101 the next minimum; withdrawal respects the limit. Original next-menu records corrected the interpretation before the final ledger.
