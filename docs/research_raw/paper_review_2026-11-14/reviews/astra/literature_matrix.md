# Primary-source literature checks

14 November 2026. This is a targeted novelty and comparator audit, not a systematic review. “Methods read” means relevant methods/results passages were inspected; it does not mean every page was read. No claim of priority follows from a failed search. URLs identify the actual sources rather than search summaries.

Two further official abstracts were checked after the independent assessment: [Robotouille, ICLR 2025](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/d033407a1a4b7a75cd5f9e4575ad9fb5-Abstract-Conference.html) contrasts synchronous and asynchronous planning; [LexiCon, NeurIPS 2025](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/8ef63909f4bc1cedffb993c0e285a4e7-Abstract-Datasets_and_Benchmarks_Track.html) varies temporal constraints. They support the feedback's methodological comparison, not a claim of direct duplication. The Planning in the Dark AAAI page returned access denied.

Other named works — TravelPlanner, ReAct, Cattle Trade, DECE and others — merit a focused bibliography pass if the final research question is selected. I have not verified every claim about them in this review and do not use them to support a new novelty conclusion. The verified closest constructs already suffice to reject broad first-of-kind claims; an arbitrary total of forty references would not improve that judgment.

The private ChatGPT-library PDF linked within the first attachment returned a login page, and no matching PDF was present among local attachments. Both supplied text files were read completely; the separate linked PDF was not accessible and is not represented as read.

## Novelty conclusion

The potentially distinct object is a conditional relationship among dynamic resource availability, a separately scored feasibility report, termination and execution under an exact local objective. It would need a result that survives plausible alternative explanations. A new interface alone, the fact of false impossibility, or a computed optimum alone is not enough. No priority claim is warranted yet.

After the independent review, a targeted financial-memory search added three adjacent sources. [MemTrial v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.11732), October 11, 2026, studies attribution to retrieved experience in portfolio decisions; its PlantedMem effects are mechanically imposed on drafts, not a textual-reserve intervention. [Memory and Generative AI](https://afajof.org/management/viewp.php?n=192584), October 2025 working paper, concerns associative parameter memory and knowledge injection; only its abstract/introduction were read. [Human bias in AI models?](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbef.2024.100971) studies anchoring in LLM forecasts; the publisher-indexed abstract was available but direct full text returned 403. These make broad memory/financial-anchor novelty claims untenable while leaving the specific conditional-inventory question unresolved. Details and access limits are in `cross_review.md`.
