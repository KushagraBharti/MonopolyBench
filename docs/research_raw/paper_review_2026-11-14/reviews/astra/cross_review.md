## Additional novelty search after the independent review

A more specific search found financial anchoring and financial-memory work not named in the feedback. This further narrows the proposed novelty claim; it does not establish a duplicate of the conditional-collateral experiment.

- [Human bias in AI models? Anchoring effects and mitigation strategies in large language models](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbef.2024.100971): the publisher's indexed abstract describes numerical anchoring in forecasts. Direct full text returned 403; no full-method claim is made.
- [Memory and Generative AI](https://afajof.org/management/viewp.php?n=192584), October 28, 2025 working paper: the original abstract and introduction describe financial choices affected by associative memory and knowledge injection. Its memory construct is parameter-level associations, not ten carried game notes. This is an adjacent precedent, not evidence against our exact proposed mechanism.
- [MemTrial](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.11732), v2 October 11, 2026: I read the primary setup and methods surrounding memory ablations and PlantedMem. It compares decisions with different retrieved experiences in portfolio tasks. Crucially, PlantedMem imposes experience effects through a synthetic tilt of real LLM drafts; it does not demonstrate that a planted textual reserve number causes false surrender. Its name must not be mistaken for that result.

The research question must therefore be more precise than either generic memory influence or financial anchoring. The candidate distinction remains updating conditional resource categories when eligibility changes, and its relationship to termination under an exact objective. The search is targeted, not exhaustive; no priority claim follows.
