## What this does and does not support

It supports each named novelty objection. The planning construct, false impossibility, the gap between one-shot and interactive action choice, menu-order sensitivity, external verification, saved-state probing, the gap between a rule check and a call, and memory error propagation all have precedent.

It does not support any claim of absence. I found no analysis of post-obligation liquidation in the two Monopoly comparators, and I looked at two papers. I found no prior work scoring liquidation against an optimum, and I did not search for any.
