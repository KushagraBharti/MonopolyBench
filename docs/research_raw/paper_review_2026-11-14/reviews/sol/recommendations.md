## Work requiring changed authorization

Each following item is a distinct study choice. Approval for one does not authorize the rest.

| Proposed work | What it changes and estimates | Essential qualifications |
|---|---|---|
| Repeated original entry requests | Estimates conditional initial-response variability at historical states and current provider versions. | Single calls do not measure complete payment. Saved requests may preserve obsolete aliases and recovery differences. These are discovery states. |
| Full continued episodes from entry states | Estimates task completion and trajectory costs under a specified response policy. | Multi-call budget, retries, auto-payment and stopping rules must be fixed. Never call this replay of the historical response. |
| Transparent reactive policies | Calibrates completion/action count under the same legal information and interface. | Declare tie-breaking, stopping and error handling. Solving the sixteen does not measure prospective assessment. Include a cash-raising random policy separately from random play that includes declaration. |
| Feasibility verifier | Supplies legal witnesses or sound impossibility certificates under a chosen action contract. | Menu versus broader validator legality must be explicit. Unknown includes timeout. Validate against documented rules; a solver sharing engine bugs does not certify standard Monopoly. |
| Local cost optimizer | Measures route excess cost under a declared local objective. | Define net wealth loss versus gross restoration spending, residual cash, existing mortgages, float rounding, stock and bankruptcy handling. No global strategic-optimality claim. |
| Independent assessment/execution arms | Separates marginal assessment accuracy from marginal execution success at the same states. | Different calls do not identify a same-instance belief/action disconnect. Forecast-then-act is its own intervention. |
| Rule salience/reminder | Tests assistance from making the rule explicit. | May change information, not just salience; match generic reconsideration and added-token/call budgets. |
| Primitive values supplied | Tests numerical-information assistance. | Complete values can also reveal structure. A computed future-collateral subtotal supplies a deduction and must be labeled derived assistance. |
| Layout comparison | Tests representation with identical explicit facts, action proposals and execution rules. | Control length/order where practical. Test dependency-specific treatment interaction instead of only overall improvement. |
| Filtered versus broad tools/catalog | Tests a practical interface policy. | Proposal space, errors, feedback and cost change; underlying legal transitions should remain fixed. Invalid-action differences are partly schema mechanics. |
| Note deletion | Tests removal of that note context. | Deletes useful facts, intentions and history too, and changes length. Not a clean corruption mechanism by itself. |
| Planted reserve numbers | Tests response sensitivity to experimentally varied text. | Distinguish asserted facts from desired reserves. Include correct, false, irrelevant and no-note controls. True current mortgage totals are not corrupted memory. |
| Counterfactual rules | Tests adaptation to declared transition changes relative to familiarity. | Recompute feasibility labels; preserve validity and comparable difficulty. Unstated altered rules test interface specification as much as model skill. |
| Disable immediate declaration | Tests removing a stopping action and forcing continued finance. | It changes control authority. Rescue does not establish corrected belief or improved original-game strategy. |
| Fresh state families | Tests coverage beyond postselected historical examples. | Distinguish legal trajectories, valid synthetic snapshots and synthetic debts. Repeated debt variants remain clustered by portfolio/family. Remove stale contradictory notes/history. |
| Solver-controller replacement in fresh games | Tests the game-level effect of that controller change. | It changes later states, opponents and random exposure. Use paired designs appropriately; do not call this validation of a diagnostic ranking by analogy alone. |

Do not begin with all factors crossed at arbitrary sizes. Define the scientific question, unit of generalization and materially useful effect before a pilot. A 128-execution-episode illustration is not a power calculation or a 128-call budget. A threshold such as 95% is not proof of saturation without a precision target and representative family coverage. Rare false declarations may require more independent families or a simpler exact analysis than a many-parameter mixed model can support.

If new work is authorized, first freeze the rules, interface and objective. Repairing float interest, hotel menus, prompt field names and Opus recovery at once creates a new configuration with several changes. It may be the right product configuration, but it cannot isolate which historical issue mattered. Log conflicts rather than silently editing outputs, record all attempts and costs, and keep old artifacts immutable. Common nominal reasoning-effort settings do not guarantee equal cognitive effort or spending across providers.

## Decisions for human authors

The owner should choose the paper's intended contribution and audience. A full observational economic-behavior paper can keep bargaining in scope if it does not promise causal strategic evaluation. A new diagnostic paper needs a predefined local objective and new evidence; it should not inherit a finance-only title as a substitute for results. The current broad title is descriptive but easy to overread as a systematic plan-adherence metric.

Human authors must choose and accept responsibility for authorship, affiliations, AI-use disclosure, release, anonymity and venue template. The blank author block and local-only source package are unfinished publication preparation, not evidence of fabricated data. No assistant should invent the human contribution or turn four LLM reviews into independent human validation.

Human reviewers should adjudicate whether the local restoration penalty captures a useful economic question. If preserving productive assets, minimizing calls, maximizing residual cash and improving winning probability are all relevant, report them separately or justify the chosen priority. Do not silently assign a dollar price to bankruptcy to force a single regret number.

The strongest current-scope paper should retain the complete populations, source-channel distinctions, positive and negative financing cases, response conflicts, composition-sensitive bargaining and a few economically consequential incidents. The unresolved interface and memory mechanisms belong in hypotheses. No acceptance score, citation count or historical cost estimate determines which of those hypotheses will survive.
