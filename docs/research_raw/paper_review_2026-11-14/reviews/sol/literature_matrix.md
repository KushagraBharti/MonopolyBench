# Primary-source matrix

Access date is 14 November 2026 throughout. This is a claim-directed literature check, not a systematic search sufficient to prove absence of prior work. 'Methods read' means the specified actual paper passages were read. It does not mean that every appendix, implementation, dataset or result was independently reproduced. No external code was executed. Searches were used to locate primary sources; secondary summaries were not treated as evidence for technical claims.

## Other citations and access boundaries

The [Lewis 2017](https://aclanthology.org/D17-1259/) and [He 2018](https://aclanthology.org/D18-1256/) official abstracts and metadata were read. They support the broad negotiation/strategy distinctions, not a fresh audit of their full evaluations.

For [Guhe and Lascarides 2014](https://www.pure.ed.ac.uk/ws/files/19353900/CIG2014.pdf), the introduction, agent setup, parameterized simulations and embargo section were read in the accepted author manuscript. Crucially, embargo recipients always comply in those simulations. This tests the benefit of successful persuasion or an imposed trading restriction, not the efficacy of naturally generated arguments. The manuscript's wording should preserve that distinction.

For [Jehiel, Moldovanu and Stacchetti 1996](https://www.econ.uni-bonn.de/micro/en/moldovanu/publications-1/how_-not-_to_sell.pdf), the original abstract and opening model interpretation were read from the author's university-hosted article. The identity-dependent externality analogy is sound at that level. Its revenue-maximizing equilibrium is not a validated model of Monopoly transactions; no theorem proof or application of its mechanism to the games was audited.

Turpin and Lanham explanation-faithfulness citations and the provider documentation entry were read in the manuscript bibliography and access notes. I did not reopen their complete original texts or verify a current provider taxonomy. The review's faithfulness caution also follows directly from the observed channel disagreements and the measurement design; it does not depend on a claimed current provider feature.

The first report's comparator list also names Embodied Agent Interface, Robotouille, LexiCon, Planning in the Dark and hybrid Monopoly decision making. The inline feedback names TravelPlanner, Cattle Trade and DECE; the comparator report names Project Vend. These mentions are recorded as leads rather than full independent method audits in this review. No assertion of their numerical superiority, venue readiness or exhaustive coverage is adopted. Their omission from twelve printed references is not itself a novelty refutation. No bibliography-size quota is appropriate.

The linked 25-page reviewer PDF in pasted-text-2 is not itself one of the three supplied input files. Its full contents were not available locally and were not represented as read. Both actual supplied text reports and all inline pieces were read in full. This distinction matters because the report advertises additional findings and thirty questions that are not all present in the supplied text.
