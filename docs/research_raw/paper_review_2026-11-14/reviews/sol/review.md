## Overall judgment

The current manuscript is a credible, unusually inspectable observational study with a modest and incompletely measured scientific contribution. Its main counts and its narrow statement about additional resources appearing in successful initial responses survive inspection. Its presentation still invites readers to infer more planning difficulty and more coherent statement-action correspondence than the measurements establish. I would not currently recommend it as a strong main-track planning or financial-intelligence contribution. I would preserve it as a discovery study and audit resource. I reject the claim that its science is empty, and I also reject the claim that another round of careful prose alone can establish the proposed causal result.

The best existing result is a contrast among several independently observable things: an engine can make payment feasible; a response can identify that feasibility accurately or inaccurately; its applied action can agree or disagree with its text; and interactive menus can carry execution to payment. Those distinctions matter more than whether a referee calls the paper planning, inventory, liquidation, or premature abandonment. Neither a new title nor a solver-relative dollar score chooses the scientific question for us.

Two objections change my initial assessment materially. First, the source semantics and actual portfolios make sustained reactive payment substantially easier to justify than an unevaluated-baseline disclaimer suggests. Second, the six above-bound starts and several successful partial explanations show that the selected sixteen fields cannot stand in for a general statement-action measurement. My initial expectation that the main needed change would be calibration was too mild. The current evidence also needs a less selective account of the relationship it claims to study.

## Financing, feasibility and difficulty

### The certificate has a valid direction

Let cash be C, all nominal building salvage S, initially listed mortgages M0 and all unmortgaged owned mortgage values M*. If `C + S + M0 < D`, no route confined to the initial mortgage set can pay. If an actual legal payment or retained legal witness also exists, payment must use newly released collateral. This establishes the sixteen focal cases. `D <= C + S + M*` alone does not establish feasibility. G7 is the concrete reason that qualification matters.

Conversely, `C + S + M0 >= D` is not a general proof of a no-release route. The first report correctly identifies a logical overstatement in Table 2's middle-row label. It does not demonstrate that any of the 53 episodes is misclassified. The five successful payments that use released collateral despite passing that bound are the residual cases to inspect. G3 d228 and d356 contain houses only. G18 d428 has 29 bank houses and three orange hotels, enough for their twelve-house downgrades. G3 d482 has seventeen bank houses and one hotel. G3 d461 has six bank houses, two hotels, and ordinary red houses that can release stock. These observations make no-release witnesses plausible, but I have not executed or exhaustively proven the prohibited-mortgage variants. Keep the category bound-defined until its route claim is justified. This is a narrow classification repair, not collapse of the debt census.

G7 Opus is initially at $1,310 nominal resources against $750 owed. The retained search starts after an earlier mortgage, with $390, one $50 mortgage and no sale. The resulting $440 ceiling is about the actual menu. The portfolio includes a hotel and four houses on the other pinks; highest-level filtering hides those houses, and the hotel cannot downgrade without bank houses. A broader bundled validator is not evidence that the agent was offered that operation. Distinguish the callable sale schema, the listed targets, validator acceptance, and the actual prompt instructions. The manuscript already discloses that gap. A local solver must choose which contract it verifies.

### The fallback critique is substantially right for these sixteen states

I inspected the archived `decision_resolver.py` at source commit `d6808534a831862682a6940f394c6c87bb2691cb`, the engine sale/mortgage routines at the first source commit, and the actual sixteen entry portfolios. The active fallback chooses an available mortgage, otherwise one sale of the first listed building, otherwise declaration. A stale separate method remains in `llm_runner.py`; its existence does not identify the active path or justify deleting it without a call-site audit.

Fifteen focal portfolios contain houses and no hotels. Selling a currently highest house preserves evenness, adds cash and bank stock, and cannot reduce future mortgage capacity. Mortgaging an eligible deed adds cash and cannot lock the still-developed group. With no development, trading, redemption, random move or other player's action intervening during debt rescue, a finite sequence of such legal cash-raising steps cannot cycle. If payment has not occurred, houses or an unused mortgage remain until the full resource value is available. All sixteen have that value at least D.

The exception with hotels, G20 d327, starts with fifteen bank houses, two brown hotels and nine orange houses. Even downgrading both hotels before selling a single ordinary house needs only eight bank houses. Thus this entry has no hotel-stock obstruction. Under the inspected menu semantics, continued valid cash raising reaches payment for this case too. This is a source-level argument for eventual payment in the sixteen entries, not an executed 16/16 baseline score. It assumes valid fallback arguments, no call budget cutoff and the frozen rules. It does not provide its action count or efficiency.

The referee's unrestricted assertion that any order reaches U* is false as a claim about all states. G7 already refutes it. More generally, highest-level filtering plus insufficient downgrade stock can obstruct liquidation. For the actual sixteen entries, however, the narrower eventual-payment argument is strong. We should not refuse that argument merely because a baseline run is unauthorized. Nor should we print a measured baseline result that we did not run.

This changes the interpretation of the ten-action example. It remains evidence of expressed anticipation of a correct aggregate budget followed by execution. It supplies little evidence that success required ten difficult planning choices. Action count, prerequisite depth, branching, and sensitivity to action ordering are distinct. The two sale calls batch many primitive sales. The eight mortgages are mostly independent after the group is cleared. Immediate declaration is the consequential irreversible choice in the failed focal entries.

### The two failures and carryover notes

Both failed first actions are declarations with no preceding financing action or fallback. G12's public message also asserts impossibility. G17's public message repeats the $530 maximum. The stored positive witnesses contradict these maxima under the actual rules: G12 sells three reds and mortgages Baltic/Illinois/Indiana to leave $38 after payment; G17 sells five houses and mortgages Baltic/Boardwalk to leave $180. These witness reports precede this review.

G12's prompt carries repeated $130 emergency-capacity notes, including turns 69 and 73. G17 carries the turn-68 $30 Baltic emergency reserve. These figures exactly match currently eligible mortgage values. That creates observational equivalence between note copying and correctly recomputing an incomplete currently-visible inventory. The historical notes are not necessarily erroneous. They explicitly describe spare or idle mortgage reserves while preserving development. The later mistake is upgrading that partial resource category to a full liquidation maximum.

The strongest memory hypothesis is therefore category persistence or failure to expand an inventory when the objective changes, not merely corruption by a false number. Successful G13 d195 carries the same sort of $445 non-dark-blue reserve and goes beyond it; G20 d327 carries $325 idle capacity and includes other finance. G5 carries an incomplete possible-rent estimate and revises it. Four of five focal Opus successes have no carried authored notes; G4 d308 has ten. These are counterexamples to a deterministic carryover explanation, not estimates of the benefit of removing memory. The $130 and $30 may be useful experimental targets, but n=2 and numerical equality do not establish anchoring or causation.

The third payable declaration, Grok G12 d249, is an especially useful counterpoint. Its note and content identify sale-based rescue; the applied call declares. ## Timing, information and inference

The initial text is temporally before the environmental release of collateral. That makes it prospective relative to future states. The first action and applied private note are generated in the same tool response. There is no separate measured belief followed by a later independent first-action choice. JSON field order would not establish cognitive order even if recorded. Later actions do occur after feedback, so budget-to-later-execution correspondence is real. Causal mediation by the note remains unmeasured.

The menus omit dollar values and the release rule, but full requests carry holdings, buildings, history and notes. Familiar property keys may enable pretrained knowledge; earlier purchase states can have values; notes may repeat values and rules. Therefore 'the current menu does not list a resource' is supportable. 'The agent lacked the information' or 'this is pure recall' is not identified. Legal-action selection is externally aided; parameterized bundled sales still require some ordering and validity choices. These are empirical interface conditions, not a neutral test of bare models.

The focal sixteen are 8.51% of the 188 financing episodes, 12.21% of 131 demonstrated-payable episodes, and 0.229% of 6,989 decisions. The referee's '0.2% of the corpus' mixes units and frames a diagnostic inclusion rule as negligible sampling. It is relevant that the subset was found retrospectively, not that a financing phenomenon occupies few decisions in games with many other choices. No denominator converts these cases into a representative estimate of all financial planning.

Fourteen of sixteen is 87.5%. A binomial exact interval around 62% to 98% is arithmetically reasonable under independent identical Bernoulli sampling. Those assumptions do not hold automatically for endogenous obligations across one roster and five seed blocks. Model-specific 2/3, 5/6, 5/5 and 2/2 are not rankings. The proposed 2/16 versus 1/115 comparison gives Fisher two-sided p=0.0392194 by fixed-margin arithmetic. It is exploratory, uses postselected classes and ignores dependence and configuration/exposure differences. It does not establish that unlocking causes errors. I would not add it as an inferential rescue of the paper.

The three resampling procedures are bootstrap/reweighting procedures. Calling the ranges empirical does not change that. But the manuscript explicitly defines their descriptive target and declines general confidence claims. Enumerating all 3,125 block draws is exact conditional enumeration, not 3,125 new observations. My independent arithmetic recovers the printed intact-block ranges and pooled contrasts. With only five blocks, changing block weights cannot establish replication or fix retrospective selection. Game-level resampling changes rotation balance. The two-stage scheme changes it too. None is automatically the right conservative interval for a population claim that has not been defined.

The 'one uncontrolled roster once' criticism is too compressed. There are twenty complete games with five seed blocks and four rotations. There are no repeated identical seed/rotation cells. Shared seeds create dependence and comparability only imperfectly because actions redirect later random draws. The appropriate description is limited replication across encountered trajectories without repeated fixed-state sampling. Neither 6,989 decisions nor repeated future calls from a single state creates that many independent environments.

## Bargaining and economic content

The claim that bargaining now has no finding is too strong. It has a valid cohort description, material composition sensitivity, and direct examples of economic reasoning. It has no identified completion effect, optimal-price result or persuasion effect. An effect becoming smaller after an exclusion is a result about this observed population. It does not imply the remaining language is scientifically worthless.

Opening swaps give 95/116 versus 37/72 rejection, 30.5077 points. Excluding Grok authors gives 90/108 versus 24/36, 16.6667. Excluding conflicting openings gives 93/112 versus 26/43, 22.5706. The exclusions alter the offer population and downstream games remain contaminated by earlier interactions. Cash bids give 42/70 versus 67/103, -5.0485 points. The literal introduction claim 'negative only when pooled' is false: Grok's within-recipient contrast is about -0.245 points and Opus's is zero. This small error survived final review despite the intended correction. It should be corrected in a later authorized revision.

Accept/counter/reject must remain separate. Countering can preserve a bundle while demanding more cash, so lower rejection is not necessarily weaker strategic resistance. Offer compensation, relative holdings, rents, author, recipient, stage and survival all differ. Completing a group is an ownership property, not proof of immediate executable development. A recipient can rationally sell to a strong rival or irrationally refuse its own completion. Board list price supplies neither marginal value nor regret.

The 61/74 semantic count concerns legally acceptable recipient-completing rejections, disjoint from the proposer-only/neither comparison. Two Astra readings, one partly exposed, do not supply human validation or independent annotator uncertainty. The codebook should distinguish merely naming a monopoly, asserting an actionable rival consequence, and making a quantitatively accurate consequence claim. A wrong budget can still evidence consideration. It cannot evidence correct strategic understanding. The concentration of 56 agreed cases in Gemini/Grok and 31 in G1/G15 further limits generalization.

The asking-price observation 'higher in all seven cells' is literally a description that includes a cell of one. Treating that unanimity as seven reliable replications would be wrong. It is not inconsistent to plot small block cells while suppressing a proposer gap, provided both sizes are explicit, but the presentation convention is uneven. Six same-deed, same-mortgage-status pairs are more useful economically and are mixed, four higher, one lower, one equal. Time, buyer and selection remain unmatched. The fourteen refusal-to-settlement histories are conditioned on eventual settlement; thirteen falling cash histories cannot establish that cash loss generally causes concessions. The appendix already states this selection mechanism, so the report's suggestion that it is missing is partly stale.

The investment and estate context should remain. First-window development of trade-completed groups and $57,378 in subsequent receipts show consequential assets, not causal returns to trading. $53,810 from early-built groups reflects selection, exposure and holding time. Thirty-eight of forty nonterminal eliminations following developed rent, 227 transferred deeds, 52 in-play completions and 69 terminal completions are historically derived context. I did not independently refold every group history. Terminal inherited groups have no later earning window. These figures should not be converted into optimality, learning or trade-effect estimates.

1. G20 liquidation belongs in the center as a source-verifiable exact aggregate budget and endpoint. Show the two batched sales, eight independent mortgages and refreshed feedback. Avoid calling it ten-step difficult planning.
2. G15 refusal is evidence of a policy restated and later applied. The turn-33 policy precedes the turn-63 warning. $233 does not fund a full green layer, but it funds one $200 house. The incident undermines a simple equation of rival-focused language with accurate economic reasoning.
4. G19 correction is strong evidence that the recipient separates an incorrect pitch from acceptable terms. Grok's own note says the pitch is false and should not be sent; the received proposal conflicts. Calling this one coherent deceptive strategy would erase the most relevant system failure.
5. G7 bluff is directly supported by the public $600 limit and private willingness at $625. The later $700 New York sale is a different transaction with Gemini, not continuation of the same bargaining dyad. It funds two competing development programs. Liquidity caused neither the sale nor subsequent rent by identification here.
6. G13 selective omission is supported by explicit returned text and knowledge of the Ventnor option. The buyer considers estate identity after the initial appeal and before the omission-framed continuation, so uptake does not identify the omission's marginal effect on the extra $70. The 5/36 claim is mistaken; the later 4/36 is for one direct roll, not all movement in a doubles turn. Do not infer intentional probability deception from evidence of intentional collateral omission.
7. G20 denial is a public coordination appeal with the speaker's consistent refusal and unilateral anticipation by Sol before the appeal. Opus later offering finance prevents a finding of collective compliance. No stable cartel, coalition prevalence or cooperation benefit follows.
8. G18 assurance is tension between a public renewal and a concurrent reservation, followed by withdrawal after further financial deterioration. Sol invokes the assurance; no costly reliance or induced sacrifice is demonstrated. Opus's returned rationale concerns prolonging survival and development cost, not explicitly future inheritance. Terminal inheritance supplies no earning opportunity.

These acts occur under a prompt explicitly permitting and suggesting deception. They can establish permitted bluffing, omission and inconsistent assurance. They cannot establish spontaneous misconduct absent elicitation, legal violations, general deception prevalence, or conduct typical of ordinary deployed assistants. The paper appropriately resists most of those claims. Its use of economic externalities is a useful conceptual analogy, not an estimated equilibrium or welfare calculation.

I favor retaining one finance example and a small main-text selection of bargaining cases for the present broad observational paper, with complete supporting records in the appendix. The previous instruction to keep five to eight contextual incidents explains the integration choice. A future finance-only paper could move the rest, but a referee's preference does not automatically cancel the owner's intended scope.

## Literature, novelty and reviewer contradictions

The primary-source matrix records versions and actual reading depth. The broad constructs are established. ACPBench directly distinguishes currently applicable actions from reachability. The PlanBench o1 follow-up explicitly reports false impossibility on solvable problems. AutoPlanBench separates full-plan generation from interactive action choice. The menu paper includes a same-candidate reordering ablation. LLM-Modulo precedes a generic verifier-assisted intervention. Counterfactual task variation and memory error propagation also have close primary precedents. A new Monopoly location or a route solver alone does not establish conceptual novelty.

Those precedents do not establish the proposed mechanism in these games. Atomic chess's refuted candidate move followed by selection is not automatically the same phenomenon as provider/content/tool disagreement under required tool choice. MafiaScope's branching instrument does not mean all controlled fixed-state research is exhausted, and its accessible v1 itself treats its thirty forks as illustrative. Generic overlap requires specific comparison, not declaring whole research directions 'taken'.

One older comparator deserves a more exact qualification than either team prose or generic reviewer advice gives it. Guhe and Lascarides's Catan embargo simulations force recipients to comply with the persuasion move. Their results quantify the benefit of successfully imposed cooperation under controlled timing and duration. They do not demonstrate that an argument persuades an autonomous counterparty. That is useful precedent for conditional economic consequences, with a different estimand from persuasion effectiveness in our games. Similarly, LLM-Modulo's soundness is conditional on sound external critics and completeness depends on candidate generation; invoking a verifier does not create unconditional correctness, optimality or cost guarantees. See the [primary-source matrix](literature_matrix.md) for exact versions and access depth.

The comparator reports disagree in ways that matter. AgentMonopoly v2 reports 140 games; the inline report says 120 from code. The inspected pinned repository recipe and paper do not straightforwardly match, so report both sources and do not choose a total by preference. The paper has explicit rules and immediate negative-cash elimination, so it does not expose our post-obligation rescue problem. Renaming streets does not remove Monopoly familiarity. Temperature zero plus local weights and seeds is not a proof of deterministic generation across hardware/software. Same-model games remove family mixture while preserving interaction effects; they are not contamination-free isolates.

FinBoardBench supplies a useful common-opponent design, but its thinking-mode table gives 4/36 versus 10/36 wins, too little evidence for 'deeper thinking cannot significantly improve' performance. Its judge-derived trade quality is not ground truth and its cross-game breadth is not transfer. Vending-Bench's one human run cannot establish human variance. The $63,000 Vending-Bench 2 reference is a calculated strategy, not an observed human baseline. Epoch's creator-code audit found real implementation concerns but did not observe its experimental exploits in the two official traces it inspected; the elicitation changes do not isolate context. AutoVend provides a useful observable-information baseline and conditional diagnostic, but three runs in one frozen world do not make its environmental coverage uniformly superior.

The Arena communication paper is a useful example of complete-population coding, yet its v3 reference set is fifty emails labeled by one author and described as codebook consistency rather than independent gold-standard validation. Replication across judge families does not erase shared codebooks or reference limitations. Its no-conduct-instruction audit covers 52 machine-readable payloads of 58, so 'verified no conduct instruction' needs that scope. Our absence of human validation remains a limitation; it should not be defended by pretending the comparator's validation is stronger than it is.

I disagree with mandatory forty-reference quotas, categorical venue predictions and cost estimates treated as budgets. The literature needed is what changes the claim. Additional low-relevance citations do not add novelty. Main-track acceptance cannot be promised by a planted-number result, a significant coefficient or an oracle-regret gap. Conversely, twenty games are not inherently disqualifying for an existence claim or instrument audit.

The prior team correctly repaired provenance and overclaims. It nevertheless settled too early on 'coherent and supported' as a paper-level conclusion. The G20 accounting error, residual no-release classification gap, missing both-channel view, six above-bound starts, and easy continued-financing structure deserve substantive analysis, not another round of qualifications around a selected success count. The final-review language about readiness should have separated completed editing from scientific readiness for a particular contribution.

## Restoration-cost regret and research design

An exact solver needs an explicit state/action contract and objective before optimality means anything. U* is a bound, not an oracle. Minimize over legal payment routes under the actual offered interface or explicitly define a broader validator-action task. Include bank stock, hotel conversion, final evenness, batched operations, auto-payment timing and historical integer redemption charges. An exhausted finite search can certify impossibility; a timeout cannot. The solver should never be called strategic ground truth merely because it is deterministic.

The proposed local cost can be coherent under restrictive accounting. Let B be full replacement cost of buildings sold, with proceeds B/2, and M newly mortgaged principal. If rebuilding the original portfolio later at unchanged prices is feasible, gross restoration expenditure is B + 1.1M. If residual cash and financing proceeds are accounted for and the same obligation is held fixed, the incremental wealth loss is B/2 + 0.1M. Thus 'building value lost plus mortgage interest' is a net restoration penalty, not the literal cash needed to restore. These objectives can rank routes equally after subtracting route-specific residual cash and common debt/cash constants under those assumptions. State those assumptions instead of asserting the metric is meaningless or naturally correct.

Future rents, blocking value, mortgage-disabled development, rebuilding opportunity, time, liquidity risk, altered bank stock and eventual victory remain outside this local penalty. Existing mortgages should be treated consistently, and historical float ceilings differ from exact 10% for some principals. Bankruptcy is not a finite-cost successful route. Score feasibility/termination separately and define regret on valid payment routes; assigning an arbitrary finite bankruptcy loss can determine the result. Median regret near zero does not establish construct saturation because tails, wrong declarations and state coverage remain.

Suppressing immediate bankruptcy tests the effect of removing a terminal choice, not whether an agent corrected its initial belief. Supplying a witness tests route assistance, not spontaneous planning. Rule reminders change salience/information; primitive values change available numerical facts; derived future-collateral totals supply a deduction. Current-action versus transition-centered layouts can identify a representation effect only if explicit information, rules, tools, retries and budgets remain fixed. Broad tool catalogs change proposal spaces and rejected-call feedback as well as presentation. Fewer invalid actions under a filtered schema is partly mechanical.

Separate assessment and execution instances avoid contaminating execution with an elicited answer, but they identify marginal behavior under different tasks. A correct assessment in one call and failure in another does not show the very same hidden belief failed to govern action. A forecast-then-act arm measures the effect of elicitation. Memory deletion changes available useful information and context length; planted values require true/false and irrelevant-number controls plus clear distinction between factual estimates and desired reserve policies. Counterfactual rules change the transition system and potentially feasibility, so labels must be recomputed and structurally matched; they do not isolate recall by magic.

Future repetitions should be nested within independent state families, conditions interleaved in collection time, and configurations reported separately. A matched state contrast supports an interface effect in that state population. Interaction between dependency class and treatment is needed for a selective dynamic-availability explanation. Impossible controls are essential to detect generic persistence. Call budgets, refusals, rejected calls, retries and compaction belong in the outcome accounting. Random effects cannot create missing independent families, and rare failures can make a complex logistic model unstable.

Replacing liquidation with a solver in fresh games estimates the effect of that controller substitution in a coupled multi-agent system. It is not the same validation as correlating diagnostic spot scores with live performance. One changed elimination alters later opponents, asset distributions and randomness. PokerBench is useful precedent for asking about external validity, not a transferable guarantee that this replacement identifies a global strategy-quality construct.

The quoted 128-episode pilot calculation is an episode count, not API-call count. The 1,920 replay requests are initial-call probes unless full continuations are specified. They cannot estimate payment or local route regret from one response alone. Opus's historical recovery path further changes calls and channels. Historical average costs with unpriced attempts cannot guarantee $40, $150 or $140 new budgets. No evaluation, probe or code fix in those proposals is authorized by this review.

## Strongest dissent and handoff

My strongest dissent from the latest referee is its proposed headline: the failures have not been shown steerable by a number in the agent's notes, and bankruptcy to preserve a reserve is not unambiguously erroneous under the original strategic prompt. The proposed optimum is a chosen local accounting reference. Turning those into present-tense findings would replace a cautious observational paper with unsupported mechanism and economic claims.

My strongest dissent from the prior team is that complete records and carefully narrowed prose do not finish the measurement. The paper should directly confront successful execution despite bad assessments, terminal action despite an adequate assessment, and channel conflict despite correct resource arithmetic. The sixteen-case count is real but cannot carry that broader relationship alone.

What remains scientifically unresolved is whether the observed false maxima arise from persistent resource categories, rule/value retrieval, the emphasis of currently executable options, response handling, strategic surrender, or ordinary variability; whether any correction selectively improves dependency judgments rather than merely encouraging persistence; and whether a local resource diagnostic predicts meaningful game behavior. The present paper should keep the full financing and response censuses, the two false maxima with both channels, the positive historical witnesses with explicit inherited status, the G20 exact aggregate forecast, the G12 action conflict, the six above-bound starts, the composition-sensitive bargaining description, and a carefully selected set of economically situated incidents. It should remain honest discovery evidence until changed authorization and new results support more.
