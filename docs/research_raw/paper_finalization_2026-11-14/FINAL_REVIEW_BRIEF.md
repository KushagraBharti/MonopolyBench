Form an initial judgment before reading the integration decision log. Then read `INTEGRATION_DECISIONS.md` and identify any substantive idea that was lost or any conclusion that exceeds its evidence.

Preserve positive findings, the full finance and offer populations, and all eight incidents in the complete package.

No new games, engine execution, replays, searches over game states, policy/baseline runs, synthetic evaluations, model probes or judge calls. No human labels or reviewer scores may be invented.
