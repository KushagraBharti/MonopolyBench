# Final paper and research record

**Financial Assessment and Strategic Bargaining by LLM Agents in Twenty Complete Monopoly Games** — Kushagra Bharti, November 2026.

- [Read the paper](integrated/manuscript.pdf): seven main pages; 30 pages with references and supplement.
- [Download source and evidence](integrated/release.zip), with [build instructions](integrated/README.md) and an [active-source map](integrated/ACTIVE_SOURCES.md).
- [Verification record](integrated/VERIFICATION.md), [machine-readable completion record](RELEASE_COMPLETION.json), and [release checklist](RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md).
- [Final review adjudication](final_review/ADJUDICATION.md) and [whole-paper integration decisions](INTEGRATION_DECISIONS.md).

The paper studies the resources agents count and the economic actions that follow. Its evidence includes complete financing and negotiation censuses, source-based financial feasibility, selective preservation of development, and bargaining over a rival's financing opportunities. It distinguishes these observations from unmeasured planning difficulty, strategic optimality and causal effects.

The four independent complete manuscripts are preserved under [Astra](full_drafts/astra/), [Sol](full_drafts/sol/), [Opus](full_drafts/opus/) and [Fable 5.2](full_drafts/fable_5_2/). Final whole-paper reviews are under [Astra](final_review/astra/), [Sol](final_review/sol/), [Opus](final_review/opus/) and [Fable 5.2](final_review/fable_5_2/); the additional fresh-context review is under [adversarial/astra_sol_fresh/](adversarial/astra_sol_fresh/).

All eight incidents, full focal response fields, prompts, game mapping and the original research-question map are retained. No new evaluations were run. LLM assistants are disclosed as tools; their reviews and text coding are not human validation. This is a named preprint, not a submitted or accepted conference paper.
