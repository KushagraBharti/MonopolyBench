## Scientific contribution and coherence

The paper has a coherent question: what financial resources agents describe, and how their choices and strategic communication relate to those assessments. Own-resource finance and rival-resource bargaining share a concrete economic object. Clearing development releases collateral; a purchase can finance both parties' development; keeping a rival solvent changes who may inherit its estate. The paper checks local financial facts while leaving global strategic value unresolved.

Its positive content is substantive. G5 states the sale-to-mortgage dependency and realizes an exact budget. G13 selects particular deeds under slack and preserves the named alternatives. G20 correctly values a binding estate, with channel-specific attribution and a forced full-liquidation end state. The 28/30 subset extends preservation beyond a selected anecdote. G7 contains reciprocal calculations of post-trade finance. G19 shows a recipient correcting a false premise while accepting the terms. These observations survive the criticism that payment alone is a weak planning measure.

The active paper does not need another thesis or a larger failure catalogue. It gives the false budgets, contrary calls, response conditions and rule mismatches enough space to constrain its positive findings. The main text ends on page 7, with references beginning there, and the appendix begins on page 9. The 29-page package is readable. The main uses ordinary 10-point two-column type and 0.75-inch margins. The long focal fields make the supplement lengthy, but they expose important counterevidence that a shorter presentation could conceal.

## Financing census, source argument and asset selection

The 188 episodes and 128 payments are supported by saved-record extraction. Payment arithmetic reproduces 180 mortgage actions, 89 sale actions and $51,997 of obligations. I accept the 131-entry continuation result on its stated conditions. The payment resolver auto-pays the current obligation. The source's mortgage and sale eligibility rules support the no-dead-end arguments. The regenerated premise classes are 108 hotel-free entries, 12 with persistent sufficient initial mortgages, 9 with sufficient stock for every downgrade and 2 requiring recyclable stock.

The last two entries deserve the scrutiny they receive. G3 d456 and d461 each have one hotel per hotel-bearing group. Bank houses plus houses in hotel-free owned groups start at eight. A sale in a hotel-free group transfers stock to the bank; removing a group's sole hotel makes its houses enter the count. Valid bundles preserve the same aggregate accounting even though the validator checks final stock and evenness. Thus stock cannot strand the remaining resources before payment. This reasoning does not enumerate states or execute a policy. It does not cover invalid calls, zero-proceeds actions, interruption, exhausted call budgets or later obligations. G7's menu-locked entry remains outside the payable population. The main and supplement maintain these limits.

The development-preservation membership rule is also sound. All thirty members own buildings and have cash plus initially listed mortgages at least equal to debt. Original resolved action sequences confirm 28 without sales. The two exceptions are Opus G12 d282 and G18 d372. The count describes a useful regularity without proving an optimal route.

One phrase overstates its interpretation. At `sections/main.tex:74`, "Asset selection shows a consistent preference" attributes a stable preference to choices that can also depend on menu presentation or a simple mortgage-first response. The later disclaimer excludes optimality but does not remove that motive inference. Replace the first sentence with "Asset selection shows a development-preserving pattern in one defined population." Keep the count and the rest of the paragraph. G13's explicit statement of which deeds to retain supports a local stated preference more directly, so its separate description need not change.

## Textual evidence and stopping

The 5/3/5/1/2 rubric is defensible under the declared field-selection rule. It classifies eleven authored notes and five Opus explanations, not the strongest evidence across all channels. Explicit release language, an ordered route through released collateral, an inclusive inventory, a tentative resource and an incomplete claimed maximum are distinct observations. Some boundary judgments remain interpretive, particularly a budget accompanied by a first-step statement. Both failed calculations are correct additions over incomplete inventories. The written maxima exclude $340 and $375 of released collateral. The six above-bound financing starts and the payable Grok declaration retain their applied calls and contrary text. G8 d287 has the correct $300 proceeds alongside a wrong shortfall. G17 d267 contains the bound numeral with a confused referent. It leaves out payable non-necessity entries with that collateral. I agree with declining the exhaustive 5/37 interpretation. ## Decisions, dissent and necessary change

I agree with the proof scope, the selected-field rubric, the restricted numeric-presence supplement, rejection of an exhaustive 37 denominator, and preservation of the richer G7 sequence and eight continuations. My substantive dissent is limited to the phrase "consistent preference". The 28/30 result should remain a descriptive pattern. The only necessary local prose change I propose is the one sentence about the preservation pattern. Retain the current structure and scientific scope.
