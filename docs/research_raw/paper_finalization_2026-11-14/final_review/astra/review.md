## Scientific judgment

The paper has a defensible observational purpose: relate expressed financial assessments to available resources, selected assets and strategic transactions in complete games. Its strongest positive examples distinguish a selective sufficient budget (G13) from exact valuation at a binding budget (G20), and connect local asset values to another player's development opportunity (G7). The 28/30 building-preservation result provides a defined population for an asset-choice observation beyond selected incidents.

The continuation argument is essential to interpreting the data, but it is the measurement discipline rather than the paper's subject. It explains why a successful sequence is not evidence of difficult advance planning. The manuscript now uses the financing census for coverage, the sixteen responses for specific assessment evidence, and the bargaining census and chronologies for strategic conduct. These roles are compatible; none supports model ranking or a causal explanation of behavior.

All four drafts contributed something substantive. Sol's selective-versus-binding opening and portable build structure improve the paper. Opus's building-preservation population and G7 bilateral budget exchange strengthen the positive findings. Fable's source argument and complete first-action table improve the supplement. Astra's main structure, evidence layers and compact casebook provide the integrated form. The decision record documents declined claims, including a whole-assessment interpretation of the 43/48 numerical screen and treating 37 selected cases as all instances of potentially released collateral.

## Remaining scientific limits

The observational design, few independent seed blocks, configurations with different response conditions, retrospective selection and model-assisted semantic readings remain limits. They cannot be removed by rewriting. They do not invalidate the documented resource calculations, choices, applied transactions or source argument. The paper should be released as a named preprint with its evidence and scope intact; no acceptance score is asserted.
