**The scientific contribution and its limits.** The best unifying question is already present: how expressed financial assessments correspond to available resources and economic choices in complete play. Financing and bargaining belong together when the paper follows resources that become usable after actions, including resources transferred to rivals or future creditors. The financing census supplies locally checkable arithmetic; bargaining supplies contextual evidence of expressed consequences and tactics. Their evidential strength differs, and the current draft generally respects that difference.

The affirmative evidence should stay prominent. G5 d263 explicitly connects selling dark-blue houses to their mortgages and states a correct $1,049 budget and $149 remainder. G13 d195 specifies a sufficient selection that preserves Boardwalk and Kentucky, then applies that selection and retains $28. G20 d327 states the exact $1,414 total and $14 remainder, later realized in events. These observations support application of financial knowledge under the recorded context. They do not identify the source of that knowledge, establish optimal financing, or imply that every sentence in the returned explanations is accurate.

The bargaining evidence also has economic content. G15 states a persistent blocking policy while refusing its own group completion. G7 juxtaposes a public $600 limit with a higher private contingency and a settled purchase. Its later New York transaction finances competing investments. G13 explicitly bargains over possible creditor succession, with selective presentation of a mortgage option. The buildings-transfer-intact implementation makes these inheritance stakes especially concrete. These are supported observations of conduct under this implementation, rather than evidence that a tactic increased winning chances. A mandatory controlled-study pivot would change the authorized question and is not needed to correct this manuscript.

**The continuation proof passes, with its stated scope.** The argument at active `sections/final_supplement.tex:70-76` is sound for the recorded entries. Let Q be cash plus remaining nominal building salvage plus unmortgaged principal. A mortgage exchanges principal for cash. A house sale exchanges one level's salvage for cash. A hotel downgrade exchanges one of its five salvage-equivalent levels for cash and leaves four houses. Thus Q is invariant before payment. The nonnegative integer consisting of building levels plus unmortgaged deeds strictly decreases under every valid positive-proceeds action. Automatic settlement pays the current debt when cash suffices.

Termination alone does not establish payment. The essential additional premise is that no unpaid state reachable by the allowed continuation can run out of financing options while Q remains sufficient. The first 108 entries have no hotels, so complete ownership and even development leave a highest house sellable until development clears. The next twelve have enough cash plus initially eligible mortgages; those mortgage options persist until used. The next nine start with bank houses at least four times the number of hotels. Even downgrading every hotel before selling the returned houses cannot exhaust the required stock.

For the last two entries, I verified the actual portfolios. Each has a sole light-blue hotel alongside two four-house deeds, a sole dark-blue hotel alongside one four-house deed, and a hotel-free red group. The bank/available-house pairs are (0,8) for d456 and (6,2) for d461. Hence R = B + H starts at eight in both. Selling a house in a hotel-free group transfers one unit from H to B. A sole-hotel downgrade consumes four bank houses but creates at least four newly available houses, including any previously blocked companion houses. R cannot decrease, including when further legal house sales are bundled. While a hotel remains, B below four therefore implies H is positive; a highest house in a hotel-free group supplies a legal sale. Otherwise a highest hotel can be downgraded. Once all hotels are gone, the hotel-free argument applies. This proves the needed absence of an unpaid dead end without enumerating action sequences.

I also checked the five middle-category cases using released mortgages in their observed routes. G3 d228 and d356 have no hotels. G3 d482 has one hotel and seventeen bank houses. G18 d428 has three hotels and twenty-nine bank houses. G3 d461 has the recyclable-stock argument above. Their full salvage plus initial mortgages covers the debt, so a source-based no-release route exists. This is a sufficient construction argument, not a newly executed route.

The paper is right to exclude invalid calls, zero-proceeds plans, call-budget exhaustion, external changes and later obligations. It must retain the distinction between menu-admissible financing and broader plans accepted by the separate validator. G7 remains outside the 131-payable population. Nothing here establishes a measured fallback score or strategic liquidation quality. I would not weaken the result back to 129 entries merely because no baseline was executed.

**Required factual corrections.** Exact active passages, evidence paths and suggested repairs also appear in [findings.json](/Users/kushagra/CS-Projects/MonopolyBench/docs/research_raw/paper_finalization_2026-11-14/adversarial/astra_sol_fresh/findings.json).

1. **F-01, factual blocker to leaving the sentence unchanged.** Active `sections/main.tex:115`, PDF p. 5: "Grok authors half the comparison swaps and fifteen of their seventeen acceptances". The comparison has 188 swaps, of which Grok authors 44: eight proposer-only and 36 neither-completing. Half applies to the 72 neither-completing swaps, not the combined comparison. The seventeen acceptances likewise belong to the neither-completing cell; the combined comparison has eighteen. Correct the sentence to state those denominators. The rejection gaps and the conclusion that composition matters survive this correction. Removing Grok changes the observed population and is not causal adjustment.

2. **F-02, factual blocker to the current channel attribution.** Active `sections/main.tex:97`, PDF p. 3: "Its note gives $700 salvage, $665 mortgage principal and a final $14." The complete d327 private note gives the total $1,414, the $14 remainder and the instruction to liquidate everything, without the component amounts. The returned explanation supplies $450+$250 salvage and $325+$280+$60 principal; recorded events realize $700 and $665. Separate those channels in the sentence. This preserves the exact-total competence example and matters because channel fidelity is part of the paper's claim.

These are local factual blockers, not a finding that the census or the paper's central argument is invalid.

**Economic and LLM implications.** The scientifically useful distinction is between stating a resource inventory, choosing to continue, executing with feedback, and selecting assets to retain. The source argument removes action-order success as an independent explanation of payment, but does not erase prospective dependency language or a sufficient selective route. Conversely, an incomplete maximum-resource statement is false regardless of whether surrender might also have a strategic motive. The third payable Grok declaration is a channel conflict, not another instance of the same inventory omission. The six above-bound starts and the unequal observability of the nine-conflict scan are properly preserved. I checked their first applied calls and text channels; the G9 first action is one mortgage, not the episode's four mortgages.

The undisclosed settlement variants are consequential. Inherited buildings and the absence of immediate mortgage interest change what a creditor gains and what a debtor may try to deny it. Gemini's G18 fee rationale fits the published rulebook but not the engine. The paper correctly separates this from bad rule recall and from the two false budgets. The Hasbro comparator supports the mortgage-release, hotel-sale and bankruptcy distinctions. [Published rules, selling/mortgages/bankruptcy, pp. 4-5](https://www.hasbro.com/common/instruct/monins.pdf).

For bargaining, the response-style differences are descriptive findings even when final acceptance totals are similar. Rejection alone is not resistance or trade quality; the full accept/counter/reject table makes that visible. The 30.5-point and 16.7-point comparisons do not isolate a completion effect. Intervals spanning zero also do not establish no effect. The conditional bootstrap framing is acceptable, the small-cell block denominators are now exposed, and the same-deed and settlement histories no longer claim a stable premium or liquidity effect. The all-eight casebook retains correction, bluffing and successful finance alongside mistakes. G18 does not establish costly reliance, G20 does not establish a cartel, and G13 does not identify persuasive effectiveness. Those are appropriate scope decisions.
