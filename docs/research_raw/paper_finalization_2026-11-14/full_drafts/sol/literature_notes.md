# Literature scope and access

This is a claim-directed synthesis of primary literature already examined in the historical whole-paper reviews, with targeted fresh primary retrieval for unfamiliar claims. It is not an exhaustive survey or an absence-of-prior-work argument. No reference quota was used. The bibliography contains only cited works, with versions fixed where material. No comparison uses outside leaderboard scores as a general-intelligence axis.

Fresh primary retrieval in this draft covered ACPBench v1's action-reachability definition, the o1 PlanBench v1 false-impossibility table, FinBoardBench v1 including its Monopoly financing rules, AutoPlanBench v4's planning/policy setup, the Menu paper's official publication and abstract, the declared-plan paper's original abstract and metadata, ACL memory-paper metadata, MemTrial v2's setup and synthetic-tilt distinction, AutoVend v2's benchmark/baseline description, MafiaScope v3's saved-state probe description, ReAct's original abstract/metadata, and LLM-Modulo's original position-paper record. The MemTrial full-method interpretation is corroborated by its primary limitations passage explaining the synthetic tilt. No paper's code or experiment was executed.

The following narrower claims rely on the historical primary-source readings recorded in the November 14 review matrices, which were read here: Hasbro's selling/mortgage/bankruptcy clauses; AgentMonopoly's negative-cash elimination and information setup; NegotiationArena's controlled negotiation design; the dialogue-agent strategy/language distinction; Catan's imposed embargo compliance; identity-dependent externalities; explanation faithfulness; Vending-Bench's repeated trajectories; and Arena's whole-population communication analysis. Those are inherited source readings, not new full-paper audits by this draft. Bibliographic entries were copied from the reviewed package when available and otherwise checked against primary records.

The related-work prose makes no scale or score superiority claim, no universal equivalence between atomic-chess refutation and Grok's channels, no causal memory claim, no novel-reachability claim, and no claim that FinBoardBench lacks multi-step financing. Its unverified payable-declaration policy remains explicitly unverified. The linked private reviewer PDF advertised in one supplied text report is not available as a local input and was not represented as read. All three actual supplied feedback files and the historical whole-paper reviews/corrections were read. Future-study proposals were assessed as criticism, not performed or scheduled.
