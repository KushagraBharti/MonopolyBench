# Independent revision and dissent

I read the finalization brief in full, the complete historical synthesis, all three input files containing both attachments and four inline pieces, all four whole-paper reviews and cross-reviews including their corrections, the complete active manuscript and appendix, and its relevant evidence/build entry points. No other author's new full draft was read. The prior reviews are evidence and criticism, not instructions to adopt their preferred new headline.

The independent central question is what financial resources these configured agents represent in first assessments and how applied choices and strategic communication relate to those assessments. The paper leads with positive exact valuation and selective finance. Payment is population context, with a source proof explaining what continuation guarantees. Strategic economic conduct remains substantive, with six compact main-text incidents and all eight retained in the full paper.

Substantive judgments:

1. I accept Astra's extension from 129 to all 131 payable entries. I independently recomputed entry premises and checked the final two-state R=B+H invariant against the source. This is a conditional source proof, not 131/131 measured baseline performance. It does not cover invalid loops, exhausted budgets, no-proceeds plans, new obligations, interruptions, or arbitrary actions. The earlier statement that a baseline was not run was true but scientifically insufficient as a reply to calibration.
2. I accept the finer 5/3/5/1/2 content rubric. I dissent from treating inclusive budgets as having no useful financial content. They can contain exact values and proposed financing even without discriminating prerequisite knowledge. Nor should nine stronger fields become a new accuracy headline. The appendix exposes every field.
3. I retain G20 as exact valuation and G13 as selective route choice. G20 has a binding full-estate end state, not a unique order or ten difficult decisions. G13 is illustrative, not established as the only case with choice. Payment is not counted as a separate independent planning accomplishment under the continuation proof.
4. I reject the failure-only thesis that the entire result consists of three stops. The complete population and competent assessments explain why those stops are informative. Accurate sums, expressed dependency knowledge, selective liquidation, and strategic transactions are empirical content even without a causal mechanism.
5. I accept both failed U0 identities and disclose mixed motive/provider incompleteness. I dissent from ranking carried-note anchoring as the established or preferred cause. Those amounts are also true current-menu mortgage totals. Successful conditional-inventory revisions and lack of an intervention leave several explanations open.
6. I accept the named-action screen but reject its proposed 179/188 accuracy headline. A nominal bound is not always a reachable route, some menus force declaration, strategic surrender is permitted, and the scan has unequal observability. Valid applied calls remain actions; conflicting prose does not reveal true intention or demonstrate resolver malfunction.
7. I accept the unpublished-rule correction. Correct rulebook knowledge can predict the implemented settlement incorrectly. A false budget remains false; a declaration's complete motive is a different question. I dissent from extending one fee example into the claim that all agents simply follow the rulebook.
8. I preserve bargaining as exploratory economic evidence. Composition and uncertainty weaken a causal completion reading; they do not prove a null or erase the conduct. I retain separate accept/counter/reject outcomes, the separate 61/74 population, raw small-cell counts, and six main incidents. I do not adopt Fable's three-incident preference or turn a model judge into strategic ground truth.
9. I reject mandatory reference counts, acceptance ratings, proposed results, and a new experimental-method framing. The related work names actual overlaps. There is no claim that action reachability, false impossibility, menu sensitivity, memory errors, saved-state probing, or statement/action gaps are new.
10. I do not compute a local-cost optimum. Such an objective can be coherent, but a net penalty and literal restoration spending differ, and neither supplies global game value. Search-free summaries do not prove saturation. A solver proposal is not the present paper's result.
