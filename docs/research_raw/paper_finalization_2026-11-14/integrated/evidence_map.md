## Literature access

The November 14 four-author reviews and their literature matrices supply the primary-source comparisons. During finalization, Astra rechecked PlanBench's o1 evaluation, counterfactual-task work, the menu-ordering paper and ACPBench primary pages. Sol and the fresh Sol reviewer checked the relevant planning and negotiation comparisons. Opus directly retrieved the Hasbro rulebook, FinBoardBench financing passages and the Catan embargo-compliance discussion. Other claims retain the documented earlier primary-source readings; they are not represented as new complete-paper reads by every author.

The paper claims no priority for action reachability, false impossibility, interactive feedback, saved-state branching, explanation/action inconsistency or LLM Monopoly play. Economic theory supplies an interpretation of identity-dependent consequences, not a tested equilibrium result. No reference count or reviewer score serves as validation.

## Reproduction levels

1. `bash build.sh` compiles static sources without raw game files or network access.
2. `python3 tools/package_release.py --verify` checks an extracted release against its manifest.
3. The optional record-check commands in `README.md` require the full repository archive and check original observations, source hashes and arithmetic. They write only within this package.
4. Historical engine witnesses and replay reports are supplied as prior evidence; none of the manuscript commands executes them.

The final archive hash identifies the shipped package. Its existence does not by itself establish that an external repository checkout or anonymous download has been tested; those checks, if performed, are reported separately in the final verification record.
