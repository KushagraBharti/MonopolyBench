## Integration process

- Read all four complete new drafts and their dissent/evidence maps after handoff. Do not select solely by title, length or reviewer endorsement.
- Record material differences and choose the best-supported wording, counts, figures and structure in a new `integrated/` package. Keep independent drafts frozen.
- Resolve factual and material interpretive findings with source evidence. Fix small issues directly; fresh review is for the complete integrated argument, not an indefinite series of re-checks.
- Finish once the material scientific findings are resolved or accurately scoped, the main paper is readable and within the target, the supplement is complete, and the release builds and verifies. Do not keep rewriting to chase an unverifiable perfect score.

## Release gates

- Named human preprint author and AI-assistance disclosure; no invented affiliation, submission or acceptance claim.
- At most nine ordinary two-column main pages; readable figures, tables, quotations and appendices.
- Exact repository-relative entry point and source archive map; actual included scripts match the reproduction description.
- Core numbers reconcile; active quotations match canonical source spans and appear in PDF extraction.
- Clean references and build diagnostics; every final rendered page inspected.
- Historical manuscript and raw-game files unchanged, and unrelated dirty work preserved.
- Final manuscript PDF, source package, README, evidence map, integration decisions and verification record are delivered together. Submission and any new evaluation remain separate actions.
