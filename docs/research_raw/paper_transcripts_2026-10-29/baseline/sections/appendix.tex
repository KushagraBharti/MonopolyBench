In addition to the rules in Section~\ref{sec:setup}, Free Parking pays nothing; income and luxury taxes are \$200 and \$100; jail allows up to three doubles attempts before a forced fine; and public state names the opponents' models. Both charges use floating-point ceilings. Trade interest computes mortgage value times 0.1 and equals the exact charge for all board principals. \section{Response provenance and evidence availability}
\label{app:channels}

An initial request contains the standard prompt, whereas a later attempt can carry a changed recovery prompt. The selected valid attempt and a deterministic fallback are distinct origins of the applied action. The latter writes a synthetic private note, which can enter future memory. The recorded Opus condition contains 1,604 first-attempt refusal chains and one additional refusal on the second attempt of G2 d129. These are 1,605 refused attempts affecting 1,605 decisions; 1,604 action-only chains do not imply 1,604 model-authored private notes or successful moves. Sixty action-only retries fail validation and default, alongside the differently ordered G2 d129 chain.

Across all configurations there are sixty-six defaults: sixty-one Opus, four Sol and one Gemini. All occur on buildable management menus following failed construction attempts. The new portable census tests every recorded target: 63 of 66 decisions contain a target reachable by atomic expansion and ordering, including all sixty-one Opus cases. Other attempts are unaffordable, malformed or lack a target; per-attempt and per-decision counts remain distinct. The counts in Table~\ref{tab:recovery} describe decisions encountering the specified uneven-building rejection, rather than every possible invalid action.

Readable provider reasoning is available on at least one attempt in 4,834 decisions. Opaque provider reasoning artifacts occur in 4,016 decisions; these populations overlap and neither count indicates that the encrypted content was read. Availability restricted to the selected valid attempt differs from availability anywhere in the attempt chain. These inventories are useful for defining a source-qualified case population, but do not repair selection into refusal, recovery, survival or readable explanation. The main results consequently avoid a cross-model semantic error or deception rate.

\section{Validation, provenance and research workflow}
\label{app:validation}

The canonical artifacts are \texttt{events.jsonl}, \texttt{actions.jsonl}, \texttt{decisions.jsonl}, per-attempt prompts and responses, state snapshots, run configuration, summary and provider usage. Event sequence and decision ID bind the analysis to the actual game. Raw tool calls, selected attempts and fallback origins remain separate from generated narratives. The study's event-accounting checks, attempt audits, exact-prefix branches and interpretation updates are retained in the phase-1 and phase-2 research directories; this draft's evidence map identifies the paths supporting each section.

Four model coauthor streams contributed overlapping review and writing: Astra, Sol, Opus and Fable. These are research-assistant streams, not the game participants. The manuscript-stage streams used GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6.1 Sol, Claude Opus 5.5 and Claude Fable 5.1, respectively. The Sol and Opus streams share model families with two evaluated participants, and review was not blinded to this overlap. Extraction and adjudication were separated, initial judgments were preserved, and peer-informed checks were identified as such. The 614-item catalog includes twenty-one historical checkpoints and overlaps between public and full-channel extractions. The 240 original-bundle checks are a fixed stratified audit with sixty decisions per configuration, not a blind random estimate of misconduct. All reported mechanical replications are distinguished from endorsements of a retained result. No model-coauthor agreement rate is treated as human annotation reliability.

The draft adds one independent event recount during writing, which reproduces the cohort's deed routes, stable-boundary completions, negotiation denominators and all 103 spending units. It also corrects two trajectory summaries: G15's orange group, completed at event~711 and first built at~718, collects \$3,250 before green arrives only in the terminal estate; G19 Gemini spends \$2,000 cumulatively on dark blues, rather than the \$1,200 of one construction step. The earlier source judgments are retained in the research history instead of silently overwritten.

The manuscript build is separate from gameplay analysis. Its LaTeX, retained datasets, figure and table generators, bibliography and review dispositions are packaged together. Existing outline and literature-review files remain unchanged. The public repository commit and new portable certificate package have different release status, as Section~\ref{sec:validation} states. Appendix~\ref{app:certificates} gives the executable artifact and verification provenance; aggregate figure regeneration alone does not verify a behavioral claim.
