# MonopolyBench Research Roadmap

Updated 2026-09-13. Current scope: two complementary papers.

## Paper A: Benchmark and evaluation infrastructure — Group 3

The contribution is a reusable, validated instrument: authoritative rules, explicit
agent interfaces, controlled execution, metrics, complete artifacts, replay,
recovery, and behavioral diagnosis. Demonstrate correctness and usefulness through
targeted validation, reference policies, and representative model experiments.

## Paper B: Economic capability and trustworthy behavior — Groups 1 + 2

The contribution is substantive evidence about economic performance, sustained
strategy, negotiation, cooperation, deception, commitments, and especially
collusion. Connect outcomes to complete interaction histories. Keep capability
and trustworthiness as separate dimensions rather than a single alignment score.

## Shared plan

Audit and improve the benchmark; validate its measurements; freeze one controlled
campaign; verify operation and budget; execute; analyze the shared corpus for the
two distinct contributions. See [the detailed plan](docs/two_paper_plan.md).

The historical eight games remain exploratory evidence. Separate mandatory E1 and
E2 phases are no longer the default roadmap. Existing campaign gates are unchanged:
implement and test a new campaign contract before execution, without bypassing
safeguards or falsely marking the older program complete.

Benchmark quality and behavioral evidence take priority over paper polish.
Blender, real-estate extensions, and broad orchestration experiments are outside
this plan. Publication targets will be explored separately.

Engine authority, determinism, legal-action enforcement, render-only UI,
OpenRouter-only access, and artifact preservation remain mandatory. Research
labels stay downstream. This plan does not authorize changes to prompts, memory,
tool schemas, retry wording, or fallback behavior, or spending on provider calls.

The [previous roadmap](docs/archive/research_direction_before_two_paper_plan_2026-09-13.md)
and the older docs/research_protocol/ program remain historical reference material.
